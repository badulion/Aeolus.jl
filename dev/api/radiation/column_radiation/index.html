Column radiation

The radiation of a model's mean column through the radiation stack: the stitched column, correlated-k and Mie optics, the two-stream solves and the heating profile (moved from the urban case's glue in ONE-e, #454).

Aeolus.ColumnRadiation — Module
Aeolus.ColumnRadiation

The radiation of a model's mean column: the model's mean profiles of θ, qv and ql on its own layers, made hydrostatic and stitched under a standard atmosphere to 30 km, through the radiation stack — correlated-k gas optics (GasOptics), Mie cloud optics and the two-stream solvers (TwoStream) — at the sun of SolarGeometry. It gives the downwelling longwave and the direct and diffuse shortwave at the surface, and the heating rate of the model layers from the net-flux divergence.

column_radiation_solver builds the optics and the solvers, stitched_column the column, column_radiation! runs one call. The urban case's column_radiation coupling (its domain-mean LES column) and its nest parent (its plane-mean column on stretched levels) both use it. Moved from the urban case's glue (ONE-e, #454); see docs/src/design/berlin_surface_radiation.md.

Aeolus.ColumnRadiation.ColumnRadiationModel — Type
ColumnRadiationModel

The state of the radiation of one model column (column_radiation_solver):

  • the optics and solvers: gas_sw, gas_lw (correlated-k), cloud_sw, cloud_lw (Mie), solver_sw, solver_lw (two-stream) and their multi-column scratch ws_sw, ws_lw (KA-n, #305);
  • the settings: nz_upper (layers of the standard atmosphere above the model), r_eff_liq (µm, the droplets' effective radius), p_surf (Pa, the ground pressure of the column), surface_sw (:twostream: the surface shortwave is the column's; :bird: the caller's clear sky);
  • the last call (column_radiation!): col (the stitched column), flw, fsw (nothing at night) and fsw_clear (the shortwave of the column without the model's condensate), heat, heat_lw, heat_sw (K s⁻¹, dT/dt of the model layers), μ₀, sun and ncalls;
  • buf (scratch of the length of heat) and dt_applied, the step of the caller's last application of heat.
Aeolus.ColumnRadiation.column_heating_kday — Method
column_heating_kday(c) -> (total, longwave, shortwave)

The mean heating of the model layers of the last call, in K day⁻¹, weighted by their mass $ρ\,Δz$.

Aeolus.ColumnRadiation.column_radiation! — Method
column_radiation!(c, col, sp, ε, α; clear = () -> col) -> c

One radiation call on the stitched column col (stitched_column) at the solar position sp (SolarGeometry.SolarPosition), for a surface of emissivity ε and shortwave albedo α: correlated-k optics (plus Mie cloud optics where col has condensate), solve_lw! and, with the sun up, solve_sw! scaled by the Earth–Sun distance factor. Fills c.heat_lw, c.heat_sw, c.heat (K/s, dT/dt of the model layers, from the net-flux divergence), c.col, c.flw, c.fsw (nothing at night), c.fsw_clear — the shortwave of the column clear() returns (the column without the model's condensate; only built when col has any) — c.μ₀ and c.sun.

The surface downwelling longwave is c.flw.flux_dn[1] and the direct / diffuse shortwave are c.fsw_clear.flux_dn_dir[1] and c.fsw_clear.flux_dn[1] − dir.

Aeolus.ColumnRadiation.column_radiation_solver — Method
column_radiation_solver(Nz, p_surf; nz_upper = 60, r_eff_liq = 10, surface_sw = :twostream)
    -> ColumnRadiationModel

The column-radiation state for a model with Nz levels and ground pressure p_surf (Pa): correlated-k gas optics, Mie cloud optics and the two-stream solvers of the shortwave and longwave bands. The one constructor: the urban case's :column_radiation coupling builds it for its LES column, the nest parent for its own grid, and both then call column_radiation!.

Aeolus.ColumnRadiation.stitched_column — Method
stitched_column(c, θ, qv, ql, dz; T_rad, humid = :midlatitude_summer) -> AtmosphericColumn

The radiation column of a model's mean profiles: θ, qv, ql (K, kg kg⁻¹) on layers of thickness dz (m, a vector: uniform for the urban child, stretched for its parent), hydrostatic from c.p_surf (column_from_profiles), with the surface radiative temperature T_rad, under us_standard_column (c.nz_upper layers to 30 km, its surface temperature chosen so that its lapse rate, 6.5 K km⁻¹, continues from the model top temperature; humidity profile humid).
