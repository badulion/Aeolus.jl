Correlated-k gas optics (Phase 32)

Aeolus.GasOptics.CorrelatedKGasOptics — Type
CorrelatedKGasOptics{T} <: AbstractGasOptics

Correlated-k gas optics built on the Malkmus band model.

Construct with correlated_k_optics. compute_optics! fills τ per layer and g-point from the column's mixing ratios; ssa and g are left at zero because gas absorption is non-scattering — Rayleigh is added separately by rayleigh_optics! so the two can be enabled independently.

Aeolus.GasOptics.MalkmusParams — Type
MalkmusParams

Per-gas, per-band Malkmus parameters.

  • k — band-mean mass absorption coefficient at reference conditions, m² kg⁻¹ (so k·u is dimensionless for u in kg m⁻²)
  • B — the line-structure parameter 2πγ̄/d̄ at reference pressure; dimensionless. Large B ⇒ weak-line (grey) behaviour, small B ⇒ strong-line (square-root) behaviour
  • n_T — temperature exponent, k ∝ (T₀/T)^{n_T}

Provenance. These are representative band-model parameters in the spirit of the Goody & Yung and Rodgers-Walshaw compilations, adjusted so that the clear-sky column validation targets in the test suite (window vs CO₂-band optical depth ordering, total column water vapour, CO₂ doubling response) are met. They are tuned representative values, not transcribed measurements — anywhere a number here is quoted as physical it should be read as "reproduces the right band behaviour", not "is the published parameter for this band".

Aeolus.GasOptics._band_wavelength — Method

Band-centre wavelength in μm from band limits in cm⁻¹.

Aeolus.GasOptics.column_absorber_amount — Method
column_absorber_amount(col, gas, k) -> kg m⁻²

Absorber mass path of gas in layer k. Converts volume mixing ratio to mass mixing ratio with the molecular-weight ratio, then multiplies by the layer mass Δp/g (hydrostatic), which is more robust than ρ·dz when the column is not exactly hydrostatic.

Aeolus.GasOptics.continuum_optical_depth — Method
continuum_optical_depth(band_lo, band_hi, u_h2o, p_Pa, T_K, vmr_h2o)

Water-vapour continuum optical depth of one layer in one band.

u_h2o is the layer water path in kg m⁻². The continuum is evaluated at the band centre; this is adequate because k_c varies smoothly, unlike the line spectrum it complements.

Outside roughly 700-1250 cm⁻¹ the Roberts fit is not meant to apply and the line spectrum dominates anyway, so the contribution is tapered to zero rather than extrapolated.

Aeolus.GasOptics.correlated_k_optics — Method
correlated_k_optics(spec; gases, continuum = true, rayleigh = true)

Build gas optics for a spectral grid. Default gas set is the standard radiatively active species for the grid's spectral region.

Aeolus.GasOptics.erf_series — Method
erf_series(x)

Error function by Taylor series for |x| ≤ 3 and by the complement of the continued fraction beyond. Accurate to ~1e-14 over the whole range; verified against tabulated values in the test suite.

Aeolus.GasOptics.erfc_series — Method
erfc_series(x)

Complementary error function. For x > 3 uses the Lentz-evaluated continued fraction

erfc(x) = e^{-x²}/√π · 1/(x + ½/(x + 1/(x + 3/2/(x + …))))

which converges rapidly in exactly the regime where the Taylor series for erf loses relative accuracy.

Aeolus.GasOptics.kdist_from_band_model — Method
kdist_from_band_model(p::MalkmusParams, ngpt; pressure, temperature)
    -> (k_g, w_g)

Reorder the Malkmus band model into ngpt correlated-k quadrature points. Returns absorption coefficients k_g (m² kg⁻¹) and weights w_g summing to 1, such that

\[\sum_g w_g e^{-k_g u} \approx T_\text{band}(u)\]

across many decades of u — the property the test suite measures directly, and the thing that distinguishes a correlated-k scheme from a band average.

Implementation: g-points are placed at the midpoints of ngpt equal probability intervals and k(g) is obtained by bisecting the analytic cumulative malkmus_cumulative_k. Equal-probability (rather than Gauss-Legendre) spacing is used because the cumulative is exactly invertible here, so uniform sampling in g already integrates the exponential faithfully and avoids negative or clustered weights.

Aeolus.GasOptics.lw_source! — Method
lw_source!(src_lay, go, col) -> src_lay

Planck source per layer and g-point, W m⁻². Each g-point carries its share w_g of the band-integrated Planck emission π·B_Δν(T), so summing over all g-points and bands recovers σT⁴ to the band-truncation error documented in RadiationCore.

Aeolus.GasOptics.lw_surface_source — Method
lw_surface_source(go, T_sfc) -> Vector

Surface Planck emission per g-point, W m⁻².

Aeolus.GasOptics.malkmus_cumulative_k — Method
malkmus_cumulative_k(k, kbar, B) -> g ∈ [0,1]

Cumulative k-distribution of the Malkmus model — the fraction of the band whose absorption coefficient is below k:

\[g(k) = \tfrac12\left\{\operatorname{erfc}\!\left[\sqrt{\tfrac{B}{4}}\left(\sqrt{\tfrac{\bar k}{k}} - \sqrt{\tfrac{k}{\bar k}}\right)\right] + e^{B}\operatorname{erfc}\!\left[\sqrt{\tfrac{B}{4}}\left(\sqrt{\tfrac{\bar k}{k}} + \sqrt{\tfrac{k}{\bar k}}\right)\right]\right\}\]

This is the analytic inverse Laplace transform of the Malkmus transmission (Domoto 1974), which is what makes the reordering below exact rather than a fit. The e^B erfc(·) product is evaluated in log space because e^B overflows for moderate B while the erfc factor underflows in step.

Aeolus.GasOptics.malkmus_log_transmission — Method
malkmus_log_transmission(u, p::MalkmusParams; pressure, temperature)

log T(u) evaluated directly, without forming T first. Needed because on a saturated path the optical depth reaches several hundred and T underflows to exactly zero — at which point log(T) is -Inf and any relative comparison becomes NaN. Working in log space keeps the strong-line asymptotics usable, and it is also the form a solver wants when accumulating optical depth over many layers.

Aeolus.GasOptics.malkmus_params — Method
malkmus_params(kind, gas, band) -> MalkmusParams

Look up band parameters. kind is :longwave or :shortwave; gas one of :h2o, :co2, :o3, :ch4, :n2o, :o2. Unknown gases return a zero-k (transparent) parameter set rather than erroring, so a column carrying extra species is harmless.

Aeolus.GasOptics.malkmus_transmission — Method
malkmus_transmission(u, p::MalkmusParams; pressure, temperature)

Band-mean transmission through absorber amount u (kg m⁻²):

\[T(u) = \exp\left\{-\frac{B}{2}\left[\sqrt{1 + \frac{4ku}{B}} - 1\right]\right\}\]

with B scaled by p/p₀ (collision broadening) and k by (T₀/T)^{n_T}.

Limits, both exercised in the test suite:

  • weak-line (ku ≪ B): T → e^{-ku}, a grey absorber
  • strong-line (ku ≫ B): T → e^{-\sqrt{kuB}}, the square-root law
Aeolus.GasOptics.optical_depth_profile — Method
optical_depth_profile(optics, spec, band) -> Vector

Band-integrated optical depth per layer (g-point weighted).

Aeolus.GasOptics.rayleigh_cross_section — Method
rayleigh_cross_section(λ_μm) -> m² per molecule

Molecular (dry-air) Rayleigh scattering cross-section at wavelength λ_μm, the single definition used by rayleigh_optical_depth and by the clear-air term of the Phase 44 visibility diagnostic (Visibility.rayleigh_extinction). It is the λ⁻⁴ fit σ = 4.577 × 10⁻²⁸ λ⁻⁴ cm²; at 550 nm that gives 5.00 × 10⁻³¹ m², roughly 10 % above the tabulated standard-air values of Bucholtz (1995) and Bodhaine et al. (1999), ≈ 4.5–4.6 × 10⁻³¹ m². Callers inherit that bias; it is left alone here because the column optical depth it feeds is validated in the Phase 32 tests.

Aeolus.GasOptics.rayleigh_optical_depth — Method
rayleigh_optical_depth(λ_μm, dp_Pa) -> τ

Rayleigh optical depth of a layer of pressure thickness dp_Pa, using the molecular column N = dp/(m̄ g) and the λ⁻⁴ cross-section. The standard-atmosphere column value at 550 nm is ≈ 0.0973, which the test suite checks.

Aeolus.GasOptics.rayleigh_optics! — Method
rayleigh_optics!(optics, spec, col)

Add molecular Rayleigh scattering to optics: increments τ and sets the combined single-scattering albedo and asymmetry consistently (Rayleigh is conservative, ω = 1, and isotropic to first order, g = 0), using the optical-depth and scattering-weighted combination rule so a later combine_optics! stays correct.

Aeolus.GasOptics.roberts_continuum_coefficient — Method
roberts_continuum_coefficient(ν) -> cm² g⁻¹ atm⁻¹

Roberts et al. (1976) water-vapour continuum absorption coefficient at wavenumber ν (cm⁻¹), before the amount, partial-pressure and temperature factors are applied. Falls steeply with wavenumber across the window — ~13 cm² g⁻¹ atm⁻¹ at 820 cm⁻¹ down to ~4.6 at 1180 cm⁻¹ — which is why the long-wavelength side of the window is the murkier one.

Aeolus.GasOptics.transmission_band — Method
transmission_band(optics, spec, band; μ = 1.0) -> T

Band transmission of the whole column along a slant path 1/μ, computed as the g-point quadrature Σ_g w_g exp(-τ_g/μ). This is the correct spectral average — note it is not exp(-τ̄/μ), and the difference is exactly the reason correlated-k exists.

Aeolus.GasOptics.us_standard_column — Method
us_standard_column(T = Float64; nz = 30, z_top = 30_000, T_sfc = 288.15,
                   co2_ppm = 420, humid = :midlatitude_summer)

A US-Standard-Atmosphere-like column on nz layers: temperature from the standard lapse-rate profile (6.5 K/km troposphere, isothermal stratosphere from 11 km, warming above 20 km), hydrostatic pressure, and a representative humidity and ozone profile. Provided so the gas optics can be exercised on a realistic path without any external data file.

humid is :midlatitude_summer (≈ 14 kg/m² precipitable water) or :subarctic_winter (≈ 4 kg/m²).

Aeolus.RadiationCore.compute_optics! — Method
compute_optics!(optics, go::CorrelatedKGasOptics, col; backend = default_backend())

Fill optics.τ for every layer and g-point. Each gas contributes its correlated-k absorption coefficient times its absorber amount; the contributions add because optical depths are additive. The water-vapour continuum and (in the shortwave) Rayleigh scattering are added when enabled.

The layers are the work items of one launch_weighted! on backend (KA-n, #305): a layer's g-points are filled by one item, in the order of the serial loops, so the optics are bit-identical at every thread count and to the serial code before the port. The k-distribution reordering (a bisection per layer, gas and g-point) is what a radiation call spends its time on: 33 ms of the Berlin child's 37 ms call on one thread.

Aeolus.RadiationCore.compute_optics! — Method
compute_optics!(optics, go::CorrelatedKGasOptics, cols::AbstractVector{<:AtmosphericColumn};
                backend = default_backend())

Multi-column form (KA-n, #305): optics has the shape (nlay, ngpt, ncol) (τ[k, g, c], from allocate_optics(T, (nlay,), spec) per column or OpticalProperties of 3-D arrays) and column c is cols[c]. Every (layer, column) is one work item of a launch_weighted! on backend, so the optics are identical at every thread count, and column c gets exactly the bits of the single-column compute_optics! on cols[c].
