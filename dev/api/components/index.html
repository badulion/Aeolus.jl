Components

Aeolus.Components (ONE-a, #450) is the component interface and the fixed stage pipeline of the single model. A Model holds Domains; a domain holds a dynamical core, its time scheme, its drivers and a concretely typed tuple of components, and step!(model, dt) runs the pipeline. The design is One model; what is built, and how stages 6–8 run inside the core, is on Components and the stage pipeline.

using Aeolus, Aeolus.Components
mesh = RegularGrid(; size = (32, 32, 32), extent = (3200.0, 3200.0, 1600.0))
sim  = BoussinesqSimulation(mesh; θ₀ = 300.0, bcs = FieldBoundaryConditions(:periodic, :periodic, :wall))
d0   = Domain(sim; validate = false)                 # the components need the domain to build
comps = Components.build_components((CentredAdvection => (; scheme = :centred),
                                     SGS => (model = :smagorinsky, c_s = 0.17, pr_t = 1/3, stability = :none),
                                     Coriolis => (; f = 1e-4)), d0)
model = Model(Domain(d0; components = comps))
step!(model, 2.0)

Several domains are a nest (ONE-f, #455): nested_model couples them with one NestLink per child (its parent, its Nesting.NestedGrid, its feedback, two-way by default, and optional synthetic inflow turbulence), and step!(model, dt) (or step_adaptive!(model)) takes one window of the nest engine (nest_window!, NEST-p2 #482): the root steps, every child subcycles through its own pipeline, the two-way children feed back, and a failed step retries the window. A config builds it from its [[domain]] blocks; see Run configuration.

Aeolus.Components — Module
Aeolus.Components

The component interface and the fixed stage pipeline of the single model (ONE-a, #450; the design is docs/src/design/single_model.md).

A component (AbstractComponent) is a reusable module instance configured by one TOML section: a thin adapter from a physics module (the equations live there) to the pipeline. A Domain holds a dynamical core (BoussinesqSimulation or AnelasticSimulation), its time scheme, its drivers and its concretely typed tuple of components; a Model holds the domains and, for several, their nest coupling (nested_model, ONE-f #455). step!(model, dt) runs the fixed stage pipeline (PIPELINE): the stages before the core, the core step with the components of stages 6–8 called from inside it (every substage of RK3), then the stages after it.

The core's own pieces are the first components: CentredAdvection (slot :advection), SGS (slot :sgs), Coriolis, SurfaceThetaFlux and Sponge. The boundary-layer physics of the stable-boundary-layer cases (ONE-g, #456) are the LargeScale forcing, the Monin–Obukhov SurfaceLayer (slot :surface) and the virtual Towers.

A domain's budgets (with_budgets, ONE-c #452) attribute every change of the measured quantities to the stage entries automatically (Aeolus.BudgetSystem); its output catalogue (domain_catalogue) is the core's variables and every component's outputs, which Aeolus.Output writes; its checks (domain_checks, ONE-d #453) are the core's (the sanity check and the budget closures) and every component's checks, which Aeolus.Checks evaluates.

Aeolus.Components.BoundaryLayerTracker — Type

The running maximum of the boundary-layer height of a domain, the height a boundary-layer-dependent NudgingWeight reads (nudging_height): every every steps the parcel height of the plane-mean virtual potential temperature (parcel_zi) raises it, and it never falls below floor (m; the urban nest parent: the largest parcel height of its sounding's launches). Aeolus.Components.CentredAdvection — Type
CentredAdvection()

The provider of the :advection slot: the second-order centred flux form of the momentum and scalar advection −∂ⱼ(uⱼϕ) on the staggered grid (Operators.momentum_advect! and advect_centered! on the Boussinesq core, run fused with the molecular diffusion, SC-d #404; the ρ̄-weighted flux form weighted_momentum_advect! on the anelastic core). The core calls it inside stage 6 of the pipeline; it attaches to no stage itself. Every domain has an :advection provider: [advection] scheme = "centred" (the default and only one).

Aeolus.Components.Coriolis — Type
Coriolis(f)

The f-plane Coriolis term (+f v, −f u) on the horizontal momentum tendencies (Operators.add_coriolis!, the core's operator), in stage 6 (:tendencies) of every (sub)step. f is [coriolis] f (s⁻¹), or 2Ω sin φ of [coriolis] latitude (degrees, Operators.coriolis_parameter); f = 0 without a latitude enables no component. On an adaptive step it limits the step to C_coriolis/|f| (limit_dt, label :coriolis). The term does no work: it rotates (u, v) at the inertial frequency |f| and keeps the horizontal kinetic energy.

Aeolus.Components.Domain — Type
Domain(state; id = "main", scheme = :euler, cfl = nothing, drivers = (;),
       components = ())

One domain of a Model:

  • state — its core, the dynamical core with its prognostic state and its grid (state.mesh): a BoussinesqSimulation or an AnelasticSimulation (core); its clock is state.t (clock);
  • scheme — the core's time scheme, :euler (step!) or :rk3 (step_rk3!, SSP-RK3), on either core (ONE-j, #460);
  • cfl — nothing for a fixed step, or the CFLConstraints of an adaptive one (step_adaptive!(domain)), on either core;
  • drivers — its input drivers: boundary, the FileBoundaryFeed of open lateral faces, advanced at the start of every step, and dynamic, the DynamicInputs of the domain's dynamic driver (its large-scale forcing tables and surface series, which components read when they are built: LargeScale, SurfaceLayer);
  • components — its components, validated (validate_components) and stored as a concretely typed tuple in the fixed order of order_components, so dispatch on them is static;
  • budgets — nothing (the default: no measurement), or the DomainBudgets of with_budgets.

Domain(d::Domain; components) is d with other components (how a run type builds them: build_components needs the domain), and its budgets off.

Aeolus.Components.DomainBudgets — Type
DomainBudgets

The measured budgets of a Domain: a Budgets with one StageBudget per measured quantity, the fields each is measured on (state; a tuple for the water of the air: vapour and what the components carry) and the core tendency that the components inside the core add to (tendency, or nothing), and the count of the core's in-core hook calls of the current step (to weight the RK3 substages and to notice a retried step), and hour, the function of the model time that picks a step's hour-of-day bin (nothing: one bin). Built by with_budgets, or around a Budgets of its own by DomainBudgets(budgets, quantities, state, tendency; nsub, hour).

Aeolus.Components.Fringe — Type
Fringe(fringe, target, update!, U∞)

A fringe region over the last [fringe] length of an x-periodic domain (Nordström, Nordin & Henningson 1999; Transition.FringeRegion, ramps rise and fall, rate lambda_max) that relaxes the outflow to the Blasius boundary layer of a flat plate whose virtual leading edge lies upstream of the inflow (Transition.blasius_target), so the flow that re-enters at x = 0 is that layer at $Re_{δ^*_0}$ = inflow_reynolds. Units are those of the inflow displacement thickness $δ^*_0$ and the free stream $U_∞$ = velocity ($δ^*_0 = 1$ m), so dynamics.nu = $U_∞/Re_{δ^*_0}$ (a cross check).

  • Initial state: the Blasius layer at its distance from the leading edge, at least the inflow's (Transition.set_blasius!), set when the component is built.
  • Top: top = "freestream" (the default) is the free stream above a growing layer, the displacement velocity $U_∞ dδ^*/dx$ with its return in the fringe rise (Transition.blasius_freestream, a FreeStreamBoundary; core_boundaries); "wall" keeps the [boundaries] lid. The plate is the [boundaries] z = "noslip" floor.
  • Free-stream turbulence: with tu > 0 the target carries the random-mode von Kármán field of Transition.FreeStreamDisturbance (modes modes, integral scale length_scale, seed; the largest wavenumber $2π/(\text{kappa\_cells}\,\max Δ)$) swept at $U_∞$, faded in above damping_height × the inflow δ₉₉ (Transition.disturbance_fringe_update; Brandt, Schlatter & Henningson 2004).

The relaxation $−λ(x)(\mathbf u − \mathbf u_{\rm target})$ is added in stage 7 (:body_force) of every substage, the target refreshed to the substage's time first. Its checkpoint state is the target and the time of its last refresh (Transition.DisturbanceFringeUpdate: the first substage of a step keeps the previous step's last target, so a restart that recomputed it would not continue the run bit for bit). Its output skin_friction is the plate's $c_f = 2τ_w/U_∞^2$ per column (Transition.skin_friction).

References: Nordström, J., Nordin, N. & Henningson, D. (1999), SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 20, 1365–1393; Brandt, L., Schlatter, P. & Henningson, D. S. (2004), J. Fluid Mech. 517, 167–198.

Aeolus.Components.KinematicFlow — Type
KinematicFlow(u, v, w)

A prescribed, steady velocity: the dynamics do not change it, and the scalars (θ, and qv with moisture) are carried by it with the core's scalar advection and time scheme. [kinematic_flow] kind:

  • "solid_body_rotation" — $u = −ω (y − y_c)$, $v = ω (x − x_c)$, $w = 0$ (omega, center), the rotation of the plane about the axis $(x_c, y_c)$; on the staggered grid $u$ depends on $y$ only and $v$ on $x$ only, so it is discretely divergence-free and the projection leaves it unchanged;
  • "uniform" — the constant velocity $(u, v, w)$;
  • "none" (the default) — off.

The velocity is set when the component is built (over the initial state), at the start of every step (stage 1, :clock), after every predictor (stage 8, :post_predictor, inside every substage) and after the core step (stage 11, :surface_update: SSP-RK3 combines the substages' velocities, which may round off the prescribed values), so each substage's tendencies and the projection, and the state after the step, carry exactly the prescribed field. It is stateless: a restart rebuilds it from the configuration. The core still computes the momentum tendencies (advection, diffusion, buoyancy and every component's), which the prescription then discards; with dynamics.theta0 = 0 θ is a passive scalar.

Aeolus.Components.LargeScale — Type
LargeScale(geostrophic, extra, U_g, V_g, t0)

The large-scale forcing of a domain, [large_scale_forcing]: the LargeScaleForcing objects it applies in stage 7 (:body_force) of every (sub)step, each with its exact mean over the step [t₀, t₀ + Δt] (apply_forcing!; t₀ is the step start, recorded in stage 1, :clock):

  • geostrophic — the GeostrophicPressureGradient of the geostrophic wind (U_g, V_g): u_g, v_g are numbers (m s⁻¹), or "driver", the dynamic driver's ls_u_g, ls_v_g height-time tables. It takes the Coriolis parameter of the domain's Coriolis component;
  • extra — from the dynamic driver, each when its switch is on: advective_tendencies (AdvectiveTendencies of ls_u_adv, ls_v_adv, ls_theta_adv, ls_qv_adv), subsidence (Subsidence of ls_w_subs, its fields and form from the driver's attributes) and nudging (ProfileNudging toward ls_nudge_*, the time scale nudging_tau), in that order (InputDrivers.dynamic_forcing).

The driver's times (s since its init_time) are read at the model time minus the run's start ([time] start). The equations are those of Aeolus.LargeScaleForcing; this component only selects and applies them. The geostrophic wind is also the target of a sponge retargeted at it (Sponge, target = "geostrophic").

Built in code (LargeScale(geostrophic, extra; U_g, V_g, clock, zi), the urban nest parent's domain, NEST-p1 #481), the component also takes:

  • geostrophic = nothing — no pressure gradient, only the extra forcings;
  • clock — the time window of each (sub)step: :step (the default and the configuration's) is the step [t₀, t₀ + Δt] in every substage, so the SSP-RK3 combination applies exactly the step mean; :core is the core's own clock sim.t at each substage, which SSP-RK3 advances by Δt per substage (windows $[t₀ + (s-1)Δt, t₀ + sΔt]$, s = 1, 2, 3): the urban nest parent's forcing since #259, kept bit for bit;
  • zi — a BoundaryLayerTracker: the running maximum of the boundary-layer height that a ProfileNudging's NudgingWeight reads (the nudging acts above a multiple of it), updated in stage 11 (:column_tendency).
Aeolus.Components.Model — Type
Model(domain)
Model(domains::Tuple, nest)

The model: its domains and the nest coupling between them. One domain needs no coupling (nest = nothing); several are a nest tree (ONE-f, #455), built from the [[domain]] graph by nested_model, whose nest is a ModelNest. step!(model, dt) advances every domain by dt through the stage pipeline: one domain alone, a nest tree with the children subcycling inside each step of their parent (ModelNest).

Aeolus.Components.ModelNest — Type
ModelNest

The nest coupling of a Model with several domains, and the one nest engine that steps it (NEST-p2, #482; the design is docs/src/design/single_model.md, "Nesting"): hierarchy, the Nesting.NestHierarchy whose domains are the model's cores (domain i of the model is domain i of the hierarchy, with the model's id) and whose links carry the boundary data, flux registers, feedback and statistics of every parent–child pair, and links, the NestLinks it was built from.

One step!(model, dt) (or step_adaptive!(model)) is one window, one step of the root (Berger & Oliger 1984), taken by the engine (nest_window!):

  1. each domain steps through stages 1–11 of its own pipeline;
  2. the boundary data of each child are prolongated at both ends of that step, and every child takes n equal substeps to land on its end, recursively for its own children (n the largest refinement for a fixed step; adaptive, the smallest whole number that keeps the child within its own stability limit, chosen at every window);
  3. the two-way children feed back (anterpolation, interface flux correction, Berger–Colella refluxing, one re-projection of the parent): stage 12, :nest_feedback;
  4. the domain finishes its step with stage 13 (:budgets, :checks, :output), so its budgets close and its checks and output see the state after the feedback.

Engine state: adaptive (the root steps adaptively and every child picks its n), validate (a step is checked and a failure retries the window, nest_window!), concurrent (siblings step in parallel, and a domain whose children are all one-way steps ahead of them, nest_prefetches; bit for bit the sequential result), max_retries, the window-start snapshot, the per-link n of the current window and the floor a retry puts on it, each domain's previous step (the growth limit of its adaptive pick), and the counters retries (windows redone over the run) and last_retries (in the last window).

Aeolus.Components.SGS — Type
SGS(closure)

The provider of the :sgs slot: an AbstractSGSClosure the core runs inside stage 6 (its coefficients from the substage state into sim.νt/sim.κt, its momentum and scalar tendencies, and its memory committed once per step, advance_sgs!). The closures are those of Operators; this component only selects and builds one from the [sgs] section (schema), a slot section (ONE-m, #476): model (smagorinsky, the default, dynamic_smagorinsky, vreman, sigma, amd, tke) and that closure's options only: its coefficient c_s, pr_t, the Richardson-number stability correction (StabilityCorrected; the eddy-viscosity closures but amd) and the form of the momentum divergence, stress (laplacian, the default, or symmetric: the stress form, through which an anisotropic vertical viscosity such as the stability correction's reaches the momentum equation; Operators.add_sgs_stress_divergence!). model = "none" enables no component (no SGS terms). Its checkpoint state is the closure's memory (sgs_state).

model = "tke" (ONE-l, #475) is the Deardorff (1980) / Moeng (1984) prognostic SGS TKE closure (PrognosticSGS.TKEClosure): over a z wall Moeng's surface_factor multiplies $c_ε$ in the first level, and with wall_cap the mixing length is capped at $κ$ × the distance to the wall (IBM.wall_distance); $e$ is seeded from the local equilibrium of the initial flow, at least e_min (initialize_tke!: $e = 0$ is a fixed point). Its buoyant production at the walls takes the θ fluxes that the domain's components apply there (wall_theta_flux), gathered in stage 3 (:surface_flux, after the components that compute them); its memory $e$ is checkpointed. On the anelastic core its buoyancy reference is the base state's θ̄ at the first level. On a StretchedGrid the wall distance is the exact height of each level centre above the bottom face (and below the top face): IBM.wall_distance assumes uniform levels (NEST-p1, #481). wall_cap_top = false caps at the bottom only (a free-slip lid under a sponge is no rough wall: the urban nest parent). With surface_value, $e$ in the first level is the surface value of the u* of the domain's :surface component (surface_friction_velocity; PrognosticSGS.tke_surface_value), copied in stage 3 after that component has computed it.

Aeolus.Components.nest_window! — Method
nest_window!(mn::ModelNest, Δt; dt_min = 0) -> (Δt_taken, retries)

One window of the nest: the root's step of Δt with every descendant subcycling to land on its end (see ModelNest). With mn.validate, the state at the window start is saved first, and a failed step (a non-finite state of a core or a nest-bound field, or a numerical failure) retries the window from it (the design's decision of 2026-10-12):

  • the root failed — the window is redone with half the root step (not below dt_min), every child choosing its substeps anew;
  • a child failed — the window is redone with the same root step and twice that child's substeps (the floor holds for the rest of the window). The parent and the other domains repeat their steps bit for bit: the restore puts back everything (cores, components, budgets, registers, statistics), so a child failure costs the parent a repeated step but the result is that of the child alone redoing its window.

After mn.max_retries retries the window is restored and an error raised.

With fixed steps (every domain's cfl === nothing) each child takes max(r) substeps per step of its parent and no step is checked (a blow-up is the run's sanity check: the blow-up guard inspects every domain, component_nonfinite_fields, a one-way child too). Adaptive (every domain has a cfl), the root picks its step and each child the number of substeps it needs, a failed step retries the window (up to max_retries times, nest_window!). concurrent (default: more than one Julia thread): siblings step in parallel and a domain below the root whose children are all one-way steps ahead of them (nest_prefetches), bit for bit the sequential result. budget_exchange ([nesting] budget_exchange): :exchange_term (the default) lets the domains measure budgets, each closing its own with one nest-exchange term per quantity (NEST_EXCHANGE); :off measures none in a nest. Refused with an ArgumentError that names the domains: a mix of fixed and adaptive domains, a domain that measures budgets under budget_exchange = :off, a child without a link or with two, a child that is not properly nested in its parent, overlapping siblings, or a two-way pair without a conservation box (those of Nesting.add_nest!). A mixed core pair (a Boussinesq domain and an anelastic one, either way round, either feedback) is nested with the conservative density conversion of Nesting.NestCoreConversion, the Boussinesq side's density ρ₀ = p_ref / (R_d θ₀).

hierarchy (default: a fresh nest_hierarchy of the root) is the hierarchy to complete: a child that joined it while it was built has a prebuilt NestLink and is checked, not added (the urban site, NEST-p3 #483). A domain whose core step a component takes (the urban site) picks its own substeps (nest_domain_limit) and books what its nest faces carry itself (no nest-exchange term); such a nest does not save and retry its windows (validate = false: the snapshot of the root alone cost the Berlin summer nest 3.5-4.4 s per window of 1.5 s), the site retries its own pieces and a failed step is the run's blow-up.
