Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.advect_positive! — Method
advect_positive!(tend, u, v, w, ϕ, dt; scheme = :weno_z5, C_T = 1e-5,
                 p = 2, workspace = nothing, bcs = nothing)

Positivity-preserving high-order scalar advection: reconstruct, limit, difference. Adds -∂(uⱼϕ)/∂xⱼ to tend such that a forward-Euler update of ϕ with the same dt keeps ϕ ≥ 0 exactly, provided

  • ϕ ≥ 0 on entry (including its halos), and
  • the first-order CFL condition Σ_d |u_d| dt/Δx_d ≤ 1 holds.

scheme selects the high-order reconstruction (:weno_z5, :weno_z7, :teno5, :teno8, :weno5, :centered), or is a reconstruction descriptor (AdvectionSchemes.WENOZ(5, 2), TENO(5), …; C_T and p are then ignored). A name is turned into a descriptor on every call (a dynamic dispatch per call, not per cell); a time loop may build it once with AdvectionSchemes._reconstruction(name) and pass that.

The flux correction blends towards first-order upwind only in the cells that would otherwise go negative, so smooth regions keep the full high-order accuracy.

tend must hold only this advection contribution for the guarantee to be meaningful; other tendencies (diffusion, sources) are limited separately or must be sign-safe themselves. Pass a FluxLimiterWorkspace as workspace to avoid the internal allocation.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.advect_teno! — Method
advect_teno!(tend, u, v, w, ϕ; order = 5, C_T = 1e-5, q = 6)

Add -∂(uⱼϕ)/∂xⱼ to tend using TENO (targeted ENO) reconstruction. order = 5 (halo ≥ 3) or order = 8 (halo ≥ 5).

C_T is the ENO cut-off on the normalised stencil measures: candidate stencils with χ_k < C_T are discarded outright, the survivors are combined with their optimal linear weights. Smaller C_T → closer to the linear scheme (less dissipative, less robust); larger → more ENO-like. In smooth regions the scheme is the linear upwind scheme of design order, which makes TENO markedly less dissipative than WENO at the same stencil width.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.advect_variance_conserving! — Method
advect_variance_conserving!(tend, u, v, w, ϕ)

Skew-symmetric scalar advection: add -½[∂(uⱼϕ)/∂xⱼ + uⱼ∂ϕ/∂xⱼ] to tend for a cell-centred scalar with face-centred transporting velocities.

Conservation properties (periodic domain, interior sum):

  • Σϕ² is conserved exactly (round-off), for any velocity field — the discrete analogue of d/dt ∫ϕ²/2 = -∫ϕ²(∇·u)/2 = 0;
  • Σϕ is conserved when the discrete velocity divergence vanishes;
  • 2nd-order accurate, centred, non-dissipative — it does not bound ϕ and will overshoot at sharp fronts. Use advect_weno_z! / advect_teno! there, or advect_positive! for positive-definite species.

This is the scalar counterpart of momentum_advect_skew! and is the right choice when the physics of interest is the scalar-variance cascade (spectra, structure functions, SGS-model calibration), where the centred flux form's spurious variance production contaminates the budget.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.advect_weno_z! — Method
advect_weno_z!(tend, u, v, w, ϕ; order = 5, p = 2)

Add -∂(uⱼϕ)/∂xⱼ to tend using WENO-Z upwind reconstruction of ϕ at each cell face, upwinded on the sign of the face velocity. Staggered Arakawa-C only: u @ FaceX, v @ FaceY, w @ FaceZ, ϕ, tend @ CellCenter.

order = 5 (halo ≥ 3) or order = 7 (halo ≥ 4). p is the exponent of the τ-ratio in the Z weights (1 = Borges et al. 2008 original, 2 = default, sharper near discontinuities).

Drop-in replacement for advect_weno5! with the accuracy loss at critical points removed. Conserves Σϕ to round-off; not positivity preserving — wrap with advect_positive! for moisture and chemical species.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.advective_face_flux — Method
advective_face_flux(r, ϕd, veld, ax, i, j, k) -> F

The advective flux u_n ϕ_f of a cell-centred scalar through one face: the face of storage index (i, j, k) normal to axis ax (1, 2, 3 for x, y, z), i.e. the face between the cells (i-1, j, k) and (i, j, k) along ax. veld is the storage array of the face-normal velocity (u.data, v.data or w.data), ϕd that of the scalar (halos filled), r a reconstruction descriptor (Centered2() is the Boussinesq core's advect_centered!; see boundary_scalar_flux for the scheme names), upwinded on the sign of u_n.

This is the face flux every flux-form operator of this module uses, and the one source of boundary_scalar_flux (its boundary faces) and of the two-way nest's flux registers (its interface faces, N-nest-e #263).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.apply_positivity_limiter! — Method
apply_positivity_limiter!(flux_x, flux_y, flux_z, ϕ, dt, mesh;
                          flux_lo = nothing, ratio = nothing, bcs = nothing)

Limit the face fluxes (flux_x, flux_y, flux_z) in place so that the forward-Euler update

ϕ^{n+1} = ϕ - dt * ∇·flux

cannot make a non-negative ϕ negative. Flux-corrected-transport / Zhang–Shu style: the antidiffusive part of each flux (relative to flux_lo, or to zero) is scaled by the smallest factor the donor cell can afford. Conservative — the two cells sharing a face see the same limited flux.

  • flux_lo — optional (Field{FaceX}, Field{FaceY}, Field{FaceZ}) of positivity-safe low-order (first-order upwind) fluxes to fall back to. With nothing the fallback is zero flux, which makes the guarantee unconditional in dt; with upwind fluxes the guarantee holds under the first-order CFL condition Σ_d |u_d| dt/Δx_d ≤ 1.
  • ratio — optional Field{CellCenter} scratch for the limiter factors.
  • bcs — if given, the limiter field's halo is filled with fill_halos!(ratio, bcs); otherwise a one-layer periodic wrap is used (harmless at impermeable walls, where the wall-face flux is zero anyway).

Returns (flux_x, flux_y, flux_z).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.boundary_scalar_flux — Method
boundary_scalar_flux(sim, ϕ; scheme = :centered, bcs = sim.θ_bcs,
                     weights = nothing, fill = true,
                     diffusivity = nothing, levels = nothing,
                     correction_levels = nothing)
    -> (west, east, south, north, bottom, top, total)

The flux of the cell-centred scalar ϕ out of the domain through each boundary face, ∮ ϕ u·n dA (units [ϕ] m³ s⁻¹; with weights = (Wc, Wf) the weighted flux ∮ W ϕ u·n dA of a sound-proof core, Wc[k] on the lateral faces, Wf[k] on the bottom and top, both indexed by the storage level k).

The advective flux is formed from the same face fluxes the advection scheme uses — the face velocity of sim.u, sim.v, sim.w times the face value of ϕ reconstructed by scheme (:centered = advect_centered!, the Boussinesq core's θ and qv advection; :upwind, :weno_js5, :weno_z5, :weno_z7, :teno5, :teno8 = the reconstructions of advect_weno_z!, advect_teno! and advection_scheme, or any reconstruction descriptor), upwinded on the sign of the face velocity. Because the scheme is in flux form, the interior fluxes telescope and

∫ϕ dV (after a forward-Euler step) − ∫ϕ dV (before) = −Δt · total

to round-off, for every scheme: this is the term that closes a scalar or energy budget of an open (nested) domain. With fill = true (default) the halos of u, v, w (with sim.bcs) and of ϕ (with bcs) are filled first, from the open-boundary buffers as the step itself fills them, so the result is the flux the next step uses. Faces of a periodic axis carry no flux (0); wall faces carry 0 because their normal velocity is zero.

N-open-d (#262) adds three keywords for the budgets of an open domain:

  • diffusivity = (ν = ν, κh = κh, κv = κv) adds the diffusive flux −K ∂ϕ/∂n A through every face of bcs that is an AbstractOpenBoundary (a Dirichlet scalar halo carries a gradient; mirror and wall halos carry none, and a fixed-gradient FluxBC top is booked by its own meter), with the face diffusivity of the core's kernels: K = ν + ½(κ_in + κ_out), κh on the lateral faces and κv on the top and an open bottom (κh, κv cell-centred Fields with filled halos, or nothing; κv defaults to κh; an optional scale entry multiplies the averaged coefficient, 1/Prₜ for the (νt, νt, 1/Prₜ) form of Operators.sgs_scalar_face_diffusivity). An optional weights entry weights the diffusive part instead of weights. Vertical gradients use the stretched-aware centre distance;
  • levels (a vector of length Nz) receives, per interior level k, the (weighted) outward flux into that level — its lateral faces, plus the top face for k = Nz and the bottom face for k = 1 — so a budget that weights levels differently (the air-heat ledger's ρ̄c_pΠ̄) can book it level by level; the entries are added to;
  • correction_levels (length Nz) receives, per level, the part of the advective outward flux carried by the last global mass-flux correction δ of each open boundary set (correct_boundary_mass_flux!): on every face the correction adjusted, −δ W ϕ_face A. The data's own flux is the rest.

face_fluxes = (Fx, Fy, Fz) (N-berlin-nest, #265) takes the advective face fluxes u ϕ_face the transport actually used instead of reconstructing them — the limited fluxes advect_positive! leaves in its FluxLimiterWorkspace (ws.Fx, ws.Fy, ws.Fz): where the positivity limiter scaled a boundary flux, the reconstructed one is not what left the domain. The face value of the correction split is then F/u (the reconstruction where u = 0).

A mesh whose halo along an axis with an AbstractOpenBoundary face is shallower than required_halo(scheme) throws an ArgumentError.

Every face flux is scalar_face_flux, the kernel the two-way nest's flux registers use too (#327, M2). This is the only boundary-flux function of the code base: the nesting budgets (N-nest-d #261, N-nest-e #263), the open-boundary Berlin option (N-open-d #262) and the nested Berlin ledger (N-berlin-nest #265) use it.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.enstrophy — Method
enstrophy(sim) -> Float64
enstrophy(u, v, w) -> Float64

Total enstrophy ½∫|ω|² dV with ω = ∇×u evaluated on the staggered grid.

On the Arakawa-C grid the vorticity components live on cell edges, each built from the two differences that need no interpolation at all:

  • ω_x = ∂w/∂y − ∂v/∂z on the x-edge (centre_x, face_y, face_z)
  • ω_y = ∂u/∂z − ∂w/∂x on the y-edge (face_x, centre_y, face_z)
  • ω_z = ∂v/∂x − ∂u/∂y on the z-edge (face_x, face_y, centre_z)

The sum runs over one edge per interior cell, which tiles the periodic domain exactly. enstrophy(sim) refills the velocity halos first.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.face_diffusivity — Method
face_diffusivity(ν, κ, s, ax, i, j, k) -> D

The diffusivity of scalar_face_flux on the face (i, j, k) normal to ax: D = ν + s · ½(κ_L + κ_R), the cell-centred coefficient κ (a Field or its storage array, halos filled; nothing for none) averaged linearly to the face, as in add_eddy_diffusion_scalar!; s the closure's scale (1/Prₜ for an eddy-viscosity closure, see Operators.sgs_scalar_face_diffusivity).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.face_inv_spacing — Method
face_inv_spacing(mesh, ax, k) -> 1/Δ

The inverse distance between the two cell centres a face normal to ax separates: 1/Δx, 1/Δy, or 1/Δz_f(k) (stretched-aware; k the storage index of the face).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.kinetic_energy_budget — Method
kinetic_energy_budget(sim; momentum_advection! = momentum_advect!, sgs = nothing) -> NamedTuple

Term-by-term budget of the resolved kinetic energy E = ∫½(u²+v²+w²) dV for the current state of sim, evaluated with the simulation's own physics settings. Returned fields (all in energy per unit time, integrated over the interior volume):

fieldmeaning / expected value
kinetic_energyE itself
advectionΣu·C(u) — ≈ 0 iff the advection operator conserves energy on this state; this is the instrument that validates Phase 28
buoyancyΣw·b — buoyancy production/destruction (0 if buoyancy off)
coriolisexactly 0 — the C-grid 4-point averaging in add_coriolis! is its own adjoint, so f×u does no work even discretely
pressure-Σu·∇p — 0 up to round-off for a discretely divergence-free field
viscousνΣu·∇²u ≤ 0 — molecular dissipation (0 if ν = 0)
sgsSGS dissipation (0 if C_s = 0); ≤ 0 for a positive eddy viscosity
totalΣu·F with F the fully assembled tendency = dE/dt
residualtotal − Σ(terms) — closure of the decomposition, round-off sized

Pass momentum_advection! = momentum_advect_skew! (or momentum_advect_weno_z!) to budget an alternative operator: the advection entry then measures that operator's energy conservation.

sgs: an SGS closure of the Era VI contract (Operators.AbstractSGSClosure, or a function f(νt, u, v, w)) to budget instead of the built-in Smagorinsky: its coefficients are computed with compute_sgs! (pure: no closure memory changes; sim.νt, sim.κt are overwritten) and its momentum tendency add_sgs_momentum! gives the sgs entry. This is the resolved-KE sink the prognostic TKE closure's shear production must equal (P56-b, #182).

Diagnostic routine — it allocates its own scratch fields and does not touch sim's prognostic state (halos are refilled as a side effect).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.momentum_advect_skew! — Method
momentum_advect_skew!(tu, tv, tw, u, v, w)

Add the Morinishi (1998) fully-conservative ("skew-symmetric") momentum advection -∂(uⱼuᵢ)/∂xⱼ to the face-centred tendencies on the Arakawa-C grid.

Conservation properties (periodic domain, interior sum):

  • discrete kinetic energy Σ½(u²+v²+w²) is conserved exactly — to round-off, and for an arbitrary velocity field, not only a divergence-free one. This is the defining property of the form.
  • momentum Σuᵢ is conserved only for discretely divergence-free velocity (unlike the flux form momentum_advect!, which conserves momentum unconditionally and energy only for ∇·u = 0).
  • 2nd-order accurate, centred, zero numerical dissipation: it needs an SGS model or explicit filtering to remove enstrophy at the grid scale, and it will ring at underresolved fronts.

Halos of u, v, w must be filled (with Periodic BCs for the exact conservation statement to apply).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.momentum_advect_weno_z! — Method
momentum_advect_weno_z!(tu, tv, tw, u, v, w; order = 5, p = 2)

Add -∂(uⱼuᵢ)/∂xⱼ to the face-centred momentum tendencies using WENO-Z reconstruction of the transported momentum component, with the transporting velocity linearly interpolated to the flux face (Arakawa-C conventions of momentum_advect! preserved exactly).

order = 5 needs halo ≥ 3, order = 7 halo ≥ 4. Upwind-biased and therefore dissipative: momentum is conserved (flux form) but discrete kinetic energy is not — use momentum_advect_skew! when energy conservation matters, or this one when robustness at sharp shear layers does.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.required_halo — Method
required_halo(r) -> Int

Minimum halo width (in every direction) needed by reconstruction descriptor r inside the flux-form advection sweep: 1 for Centered2, 3 for WENO-Z5 / TENO5 / WENO-JS5, 4 for WENO-Z7, 5 for TENO8 (whose 8-cell union reaches five cells upwind of the interface).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.scalar_face_flux — Function
scalar_face_flux(r, ϕd, veld, ax, i, j, k, D = 0, invΔ = 0) -> (Fa, Fd, ϕ_f)

The one per-face flux kernel of a cell-centred scalar (#327, M2): the advective flux density Fa = u_n ϕ_f (advective_face_flux: ϕ_f reconstructed by r, upwinded on u_n = veld[i, j, k]; r = nothing for no advection, Fa = ϕ_f = 0) and the diffusive flux density

Fd = −D (ϕ[i, j, k] − ϕ[i − e_ax]) · invΔ

through the face of storage index (i, j, k) normal to axis ax, with the face diffusivity D (face_diffusivity) and the inverse centre-to-centre distance invΔ (face_inv_spacing) — the two-point face form of the Laplacian and of add_eddy_diffusion_scalar!. Positive along +ax.

Its two users book exactly the same numbers: boundary_scalar_flux (the open-face budgets, among them the Berlin heat ledger) and the flux registers of a two-way nest (Nesting.NestFluxRegisters).

scalar_variance_budget(sim, ϕ; advection! = advect_centered!) -> NamedTuple

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.teno5_left — Method
teno5_left(fm2, fm1, f0, fp1, fp2; C_T = 1e-5, q = 6)

5th-order TENO reconstruction at i+1/2, left-biased. Three 3-point candidate stencils, ENO-like discrete selection with cut-off C_T, then the optimal linear weights d = (1/10, 6/10, 3/10) over the survivors.

Reference: Fu, Hu & Adams, A family of high-order targeted ENO schemes for compressible-flow simulations, JCP 305 (2016) 333.

teno5_right(fm1, f0, fp1, fp2, fp3; C_T = 1e-5, q = 6)

Right-biased 5th-order TENO reconstruction — teno5_left on the mirrored stencil.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.teno8_left — Method
teno8_left(fm4, fm3, fm2, fm1, f0, fp1, fp2, fp3; C_T = 1e-5, q = 6)

8th-order TENO reconstruction at i+1/2, left-biased, from the eight cell averages (f_{i-4}, …, f_{i+3}). Six candidate stencils of widths 3,3,3,4,4,5; ENO cut-off C_T; optimal linear weights d = (3/28, 15/28, 3/14, 3/56, 1/14, 1/56) reproducing the 8th-order upwind-biased reconstruction when all candidates survive.

The global smoothness measure is τ = |β₅ − (β₀ + 4β₁ + β₂)/6|, the TENO-style comparison of the widest candidate against the Simpson-weighted narrow ones.

Reference: Fu, Hu & Adams, JCP 305 (2016) 333.

teno8_right(fm2, fm1, f0, fp1, fp2, fp3, fp4, fp5; C_T = 1e-5, q = 6)

Right-biased 8th-order TENO reconstruction at i+1/2 — teno8_left on the mirrored stencil (f_{i-2}, …, f_{i+5}).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.weno_js5_left — Method
weno_js5_left(fm2, fm1, f0, fp1, fp2; ε = 1e-6)

Classic 5th-order WENO-JS (Jiang & Shu, JCP 126 (1996) 202) left-biased reconstruction, α_k = d_k/(β_k+ε)². Kept here as the reference scheme for accuracy comparisons against weno_z5_left; production runs should prefer the Z weights, which do not lose order at critical points.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.weno_js5_right — Method
weno_js5_right(fm1, f0, fp1, fp2, fp3; ε = 1e-6)

Right-biased classic WENO-JS 5 reconstruction — weno_js5_left on the mirrored stencil.

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.weno_z5_left — Method
weno_z5_left(fm2, fm1, f0, fp1, fp2; p = 2)

5th-order WENO-Z reconstruction of the interface value at i+1/2 from the five cell averages (f_{i-2}, …, f_{i+2}) — the left-biased (upwind for positive advecting velocity) stencil.

Z-type nonlinear weights α_k = d_k (1 + (τ₅/(β_k+ε))^p) with τ₅ = |β₀ − β₂|, ideal weights d = (1/10, 6/10, 3/10).

Reference: Borges, Carmona, Costa & Don, An improved weighted essentially non-oscillatory scheme for hyperbolic conservation laws, JCP 227 (2008).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.weno_z5_right — Method
weno_z5_right(fm1, f0, fp1, fp2, fp3; p = 2)

Right-biased 5th-order WENO-Z reconstruction at i+1/2 (upwind for a negative advecting velocity), i.e. weno_z5_left applied to the mirrored stencil (f_{i-1}, …, f_{i+3}).

Aeolus.AdvectionSchemes.weno_z7_left — Method
weno_z7_left(fm3, fm2, fm1, f0, fp1, fp2, fp3; p = 2)

7th-order WENO-Z reconstruction of the interface value at i+1/2 from the seven cell averages (f_{i-3}, …, f_{i+3}) — left-biased stencil.

Four 4-point candidate stencils with ideal weights d = (1/35, 12/35, 18/35, 4/35) and the 7th-order global smoothness measure τ₇ = |β₀ − β₁ − β₂ + β₃|.

Reference: Castro, Costa & Don, High order weighted essentially non-oscillatory WENO-Z schemes for hyperbolic conservation laws, JCP 230 (2011) 1766 (extending Borges et al. 2008 to arbitrary order; candidate polynomials/indicators after Balsara & Shu, JCP 160 (2000) 405).

weno_z7_right(fm2, fm1, f0, fp1, fp2, fp3, fp4; p = 2)

Right-biased 7th-order WENO-Z reconstruction at i+1/2: weno_z7_left on the mirrored stencil (f_{i-2}, …, f_{i+4}).
