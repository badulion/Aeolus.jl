LandSurface — the vegetated land column

LandSurface is the vegetated land column (Phase 47, #21): a two-big-leaf (sunlit/shaded) canopy over a SoilColumn.SoilThermalColumn, coupled to the atmosphere through SurfaceExchange's Monin–Obukhov solver and implementing AbstractSurface, so the tile framework can aggregate it like any other surface. The theory, the validation against five FLUXNET towers and what the model gets wrong are on the land surface page.

It owns what only a vegetated column needs:

  • the land-column columns of the CLM4.5 PFT tables — soil-water stress potentials ψo, ψc (Table 8.1), Zeng (2001) root parameters (Table 8.3), leaf optics (Table 3.1), aerodynamic ratios (Table 5.1) — gathered with the photosynthetic parameters of Photosynthesis by pft_vegetation;
  • root fractions, the CLM wilting factor and the root-zone stress factor βt (`zengrootfractions,CLMWaterStress,soilwaterstress), and the root-uptake sink that takes the transpiration out of the soil (rootwater_uptake`);
  • the big-leaf canopy radiation (bigleaf_shortwave, bigleaf_longwave) and canopy aerodynamics (canopy_roughness, undercanopy_transfer_coefficient);
  • VegetatedLandColumn, its step_surface!, and the diagnostics and budgets of a step.

It delegates the leaf biochemistry, the stomatal closures, the leaf boundary layer and the water-stress hook to Photosynthesis; soil water, heat, freeze–thaw and the water table to SoilColumn; the surface layer and constants to SurfaceExchange; saturation and latent heats to MoistThermodynamics; and, in the FLUXNET tests, the solar position and the diffuse fraction to SolarGeometry.

using Aeolus
using Aeolus.SoilColumn: SoilThermalColumn
grid = clm5_soil_grid(13)                                     # 2.7 m
water = SoilWaterColumn(grid, cosby_clapp_hornberger(4.0, 40.0); ψ = -3.37,
                        ice_impedance = PowerLawIceImpedance())
soil = SoilThermalColumn(water, clm_soil_thermal_properties(4.0, 40.0); T_soil = 288.0)
col = VegetatedLandColumn(soil, pft_vegetation(:bdt_temperate); LAI = 5.0, z_top = 30.0)

f = SurfaceForcing(sw_down = 700.0, lw_down = 340.0, T_air = 295.0, q_air = 0.010,
                   U = 3.0, z_ref = 45.0)
fl = step_surface!(col, f, 3600.0; cosz = 0.8, diffuse_fraction = 0.2)
fl.Rn - fl.H - fl.LE - fl.G            # ≈ 1e-11 W m⁻²: the column closes its budget
col.last.β_t, col.last.LE_v, col.last.GPP, col.last.T_v

cosz, diffuse_fraction and co2 are state of the column, set by the keyword arguments of step_surface!, because SurfaceForcing carries only the global shortwave. set_leaf_area! prescribes the phenology.

Aeolus.LandSurface — Module
LandSurface

The vegetated land column (Phase 47, #21): a two-big-leaf (sunlit/shaded) canopy over a SoilThermalColumn, coupled to the atmosphere through the Monin–Obukhov solver of SurfaceExchange and implementing its AbstractSurface interface, so the tile framework can aggregate it like any other surface.

It owns what a vegetated column needs and no other module has:

  1. Plant parameters for the column — the soil-water-stress (ψo, ψc), root-distribution (Zeng 2001), leaf-optics and aerodynamic columns of the CLM4.5 PFT tables (VegetationParameters, pft_vegetation). The photosynthetic columns belong to Photosynthesis and are taken from there.
  2. Soil-water stress — root fractions per soil layer (zeng_root_fractions), the CLM plant wilting factor and the root-weighted stress factor βt (CLMWaterStress, [`soilwaterstress](@ref)), and the root-water-uptake sink that removes the transpiration from the soil layers ([rootwater_uptake`](@ref)).
  3. Big-leaf canopy radiation — sunlit/shaded shortwave absorption after Goudriaan (1977) and de Pury & Farquhar (1997) (bigleaf_shortwave) and the CLM canopy longwave exchange (bigleaf_longwave).
  4. The column — VegetatedLandColumn: a canopy air space joined to the atmosphere (MOST), to the leaves (leaf boundary layer in series with the sunlit and shaded stomata of Photosynthesis) and to the ground (the under-canopy resistance of Zeng et al. 2005), with the leaf energy balance solved for the leaf temperature (CLM4.5 §5.3; Oleson et al. 2013).

What it delegates: leaf biochemistry, stomatal closures, leaf boundary layer and the water-stress hook to Photosynthesis; soil water, heat, freeze–thaw and the water table to SoilColumn; the surface layer and material constants to SurfaceExchange; saturation, latent heats and air properties to MoistThermodynamics.

Sign convention

As the contract: H, LE, E, τ positive upward, Rn positive into the surface, G positive into the soil, so $R_n = H + LE + G$ (the canopy stores no heat).

Aeolus.LandSurface.VegetatedLandColumn — Type
VegetatedLandColumn(soil::SoilThermalColumn, veg::VegetationParameters;
                    LAI, SAI = 0, z_top, co2 = 400, cosz = 0.5,
                    diffuse_fraction = 0.3, par_fraction = 0.5,
                    stress = CLMWaterStress(veg), roots = zeng_root_fractions(…),
                    T_veg = soil skin temperature, sf = BusingerDyerBeljaarsHoltslag(),
                    U_min = 0.1, maxiter = 60, tol_T = 1e-4)

A vegetated land column — a two-big-leaf canopy (leaf area LAI, stem area SAI, height z_top m) over the soil column soil — implementing AbstractSurface. The canopy follows the CLM4.5 formulation (Oleson et al. 2013, §5.3): leaves, ground and atmosphere exchange heat and vapour through a canopy air space with no storage, whose temperature $T_{ac}$ and humidity $q_{ac}$ follow from flux continuity,

T_ac = (c_a θ_a + c_g T_g + c_v T_v)/(c_a + c_g + c_v)             (5.90)
q_ac = (c_a^w q_a + c_g^w q_g + c_v^w q_sat(T_v))/(c_a^w + c_g^w + c_v^w)   (5.104)

with conductances (m s⁻¹): $c_a = C_H U$ above the canopy from SurfaceExchange.solve_surface_layer between the canopy air (at $d + z_0$) and the reference height; $c_v = (L+S)\, g_b$ for the leaves, $g_b$ from Photosynthesis.leaf_boundary_layer_conductance at $U = u_*$; $c_g = C_s u_*$ below the canopy (undercanopy_transfer_coefficient); for vapour $c_v^w = Σ_{sun,sha} L_x g_b g_{s,x}/(g_b + g_{s,x})$ (leaf boundary layer and stomata in series, eqs. 5.106, 5.110) and $c_g^w = β_{soil} c_g$ with the Lee & Pielke β of the soil's top layer.

Stomata and water stress. The sunlit and shaded big leaves each call Photosynthesis.leaf_gas_exchange with their absorbed PAR (bigleaf_shortwave), the leaf temperature, the canopy-air vapour pressure, the leaf boundary layer, and the root-zone stress factor βt of [`soilwaterstress`](@ref), which scales Vcmax, Rd and g0 (veg.stress_targets). Leaf capacities (Vcmax25, Jmax25, Tp25, Rd25; C4: Vcmax25, kp25, Rd25) decline through the canopy as ``e^{-Kn x}`` and are integrated over the sunlit and shaded leaf area (CLM4.5 eqs. 8.19-8.23). With β_t = 0 the roots can supply no water and transpiration is shut off.

Leaf energy balance. The leaf temperature $T_v$ solves $R_{n,v}(T_v) = H_v(T_v) + λ E_v(T_v)$ (eq. 5.129; the canopy stores no heat) by Newton's method with the conductances fixed, inside an outer iteration that updates the surface layer, the stomata and the canopy air until $T_v$ and $T_{ac}$ change by less than tol_T (default 10⁻⁴ K; CLM uses 0.01 K and 40 iterations), with the canopy-air update under-relaxed adaptively after 10 iterations. The leaf balance is solved to round-off in every iteration, so the column closes its energy budget even when the outer iteration stops at maxiter (converged = false; 0.3-3 % of the hours in the FLUXNET tests, all with a last change below 0.03 K). When the air at the leaves is supersaturated ($E_v < 0$) dew forms through $(L+S) g_b$ and is passed to the soil surface: the column has no canopy interception store.

Ground. The ground skin is the soil column's: its fluxes $H_g$, $E_g$, $R_{n,g}$ are linearised in $T_g$ with the leaf temperature and conductances held (as CLM does after the canopy iteration) and handed to SoilColumn.step_soil_heat! as a LinearizedSkinBalance, which solves the skin and soil temperatures implicitly; the ground fluxes are then evaluated at the step-mean skin temperature, so $R_n - H - LE - G$ is the canopy residual alone. Finally SoilColumn.step_soil_water! takes rain and leaf dew as input, the ground evaporation as the surface demand, and the transpiration as a root sink (root_water_uptake).

Radiation. cosz (cosine of the solar zenith angle), diffuse_fraction and par_fraction (0.5: the split of CLM's atmospheric forcing) are state, set before a step or passed to step_surface!, because SurfaceForcing carries only the global shortwave — e.g. from SolarGeometry.solar_position and partition_global_irradiance. The ground albedo and emissivity are the soil column's.

Snow is not handled (no snowpack; snowfall is recorded in the budget as ignored), nor is canopy interception of rain.

Aeolus.LandSurface.bigleaf_longwave — Method
bigleaf_longwave(L↓, T_v, T_g, LS; ε_leaf = 0.97, ε_ground, σ_SB) -> BigLeafLongwave

Longwave exchange between sky, canopy and ground (CLM4.5 §4.2, eqs. 4.16-4.20 without the ground-temperature correction). The canopy of leaf plus stem area LS has emissivity $ε_v = 1 - e^{-LS/\bar μ}$ with $\bar μ = 1$ (as CLM; ε_leaf is accepted for API symmetry and not used). With one reflection at the ground:

canopy = ε_v[1 + (1-ε_g)(1-ε_v)] L↓ + ε_v ε_g σT_g⁴ - [2 - ε_v(1-ε_g)] ε_v σT_v⁴
ground = ε_g(1-ε_v) L↓ + ε_g ε_v σT_v⁴ - ε_g σT_g⁴
upward = (1-ε_g)(1-ε_v)² L↓ + ε_v[1 + (1-ε_g)(1-ε_v)] σT_v⁴ + ε_g(1-ε_v) σT_g⁴

and $canopy + ground + upward = L↓$ exactly.

Aeolus.LandSurface.bigleaf_shortwave — Method
bigleaf_shortwave(I_beam, I_diffuse, μ, L; σ, α_ground, Ω = 1) -> BigLeafShortwave

Two-big-leaf (sunlit/shaded) shortwave absorption for one waveband, after Goudriaan (1977) and de Pury & Farquhar (1997). Inputs: horizontal beam and diffuse irradiance above the canopy (W m⁻²), cosine of the solar zenith angle μ, leaf area index L, leaf scattering coefficient $σ = ρ_{leaf} + τ_{leaf}$, ground albedo α_ground and clumping index Ω. Leaves are spherically distributed ($G = 1/2$), so the beam extinction coefficient is $k_b = Ω/(2μ)$ and the sunlit leaf area is $L_{sun} = (1 - e^{-k_b L})/k_b$.

The beam (and, integrated over an isotropic sky, the diffuse) radiation decays in the canopy as $e^{-k' l}$ with $k' = k\sqrt{1-σ}$, after a canopy reflection $ρ_{cb}$ (Goudriaan) or $ρ_{cd}$. The diffuse $k_d'$ is chosen so that $e^{-k_d' L}$ is the exact hemispheric transmittance $2∫_0^1 e^{-k_b'(μ) L} μ\, dμ$, and $ρ_{cd}$ is the hemispheric average of $ρ_{cb}$. Sunlit leaves absorb the unscattered beam, the diffuse and the scattered beam in proportion to the sunlit fraction $e^{-k_b l}$ (de Pury & Farquhar 1997, eqs. A25-A28, integrated in closed form); shaded leaves absorb the rest of the canopy absorption.

The ground receives $(1-ρ_{cb}) I_b e^{-k_b' L} + (1-ρ_{cd}) I_d e^{-k_d' L}$ and reflects α_ground of it upward; of that, the fraction $e^{-k_d' L}$ escapes and the rest is absorbed by the shaded leaves (one ground reflection, no further canopy–ground reflections). Energy is conserved exactly: sunlit + shaded + ground + reflected = incoming. Stems and the leaf-orientation index χ_L of CLM are not represented. Below the horizon (μ ≤ 0) all incident light is treated as diffuse and there are no sunlit leaves.

Aeolus.LandSurface.canopy_roughness — Method
canopy_roughness(z_top, LS, R_z0m, R_d, z0m_ground) -> (z0m, d)

Momentum roughness length and displacement height of a canopy of height z_top (m) with leaf + stem area LS (CLM4.5 eqs. 5.126-5.128): the full-canopy values $R_{z0m} z_{top}$ and $R_d z_{top}$ are reached at $LS ≥ 2$, and below that the roughness is interpolated in log space towards the ground's with the weight $V = [1 - e^{-\min(LS, 2)}]/(1 - e^{-2})$. CLM sets the heat and vapour roughness of a vegetated surface equal to z0m.

Aeolus.LandSurface.pft_vegetation — Method
pft_vegetation(pft::Symbol; model = :medlyn, kwargs...) -> VegetationParameters

The VegetationParameters of a CLM plant functional type (a key of CLM45_PFT_PHOTOSYNTHESIS): leaf biochemistry from pft_photosynthesis_parameters, the stomatal closure from pft_stomatal_model(pft; model) (:medlyn, CLM5, or :ball_berry, CLM4.5), and the CLM4.5 Tables 3.1, 5.1, 8.1 (ψo, ψc) and 8.3 columns held here. Keyword arguments override any field.

radiative_temperature(s::VegetatedLandColumn; Ldown = 0) -> K

Aeolus.LandSurface.root_water_uptake — Method
root_water_uptake(E_t, roots, w, β_t; ρ_w = 1000.0) -> sink (m s⁻¹ per layer)

Distribute the transpiration E_t (kg m⁻² s⁻¹) over the soil layers with the effective root fraction $r_{e,i} = r_i w_i / β_t$ (CLM4.5 eqs. 7.104, 7.107): layers that are wetter and hold more roots supply more. Returns the per-layer sink in m s⁻¹ of liquid water, the sink argument of SoilColumn.step_soil_water!; $Σ_i \text{sink}_i = E_t/ρ_w$ exactly when βt > 0. With βt = 0 no layer can supply water and the sink is zero (VegetatedLandColumn then allows no transpiration).

Aeolus.LandSurface.soil_water_stress — Method
soil_water_stress(stress, col, roots) -> (β_t, w)

The root-zone soil-water stress factor $β_t = Σ_i r_i w_i$ (CLM4.5 eq. 8.26) of a soil column col (SoilWaterColumn or SoilThermalColumn) for the root fractions roots, together with the per-layer wilting factors w. Each layer's factor is wilting_factor at the layer's matric potential, scaled by the unfrozen pore fraction $(θ_{sat} - θ_{ice})/θ_{sat}$ and set to zero when the layer is colder than $T_f -$ T_limit or holds no liquid water (eq. 8.27). βt is the factor `Photosynthesis.leafgasexchange` applies to Vcmax, Rd and g0.

Aeolus.LandSurface.undercanopy_transfer_coefficient — Method
undercanopy_transfer_coefficient(u_star, LS, z0m_ground; T_ac, T_g, z_top,
                                 g = 9.80665, κ = 0.4, ν = 1.5e-5) -> C_s

Turbulent transfer coefficient between the ground and the canopy air (Zeng et al. 2005; Sakaguchi & Zeng 2009; CLM4.5 eqs. 5.115-5.119), so the ground–canopy-air conductance is $C_s u_*$: $C_s = C_{s,bare} W + C_{s,dense}(1 - W)$, $W = e^{-LS}$, with $C_{s,bare} = (κ/a)(z_{0m,g} u_*/ν)^{-0.45}$, $a = 0.13$, and $C_{s,dense} = 0.004$, reduced to $0.004/(1 + γ \min(S, 10))$ with γ = 0.5 when the canopy air is warmer than the ground (stable under-canopy layer), $S = g z_{top}(T_{ac} - T_g)/(T_{ac} u_*²)$.

Aeolus.LandSurface.zeng_root_fractions — Method
zeng_root_fractions(grid::SoilGrid, r_a, r_b) -> Vector

Fraction of roots in each soil layer after Zeng (2001), CLM4.5 eq. 8.30: the cumulative root fraction above depth z is $Y(z) = 1 - \tfrac12\,(e^{-r_a z} + e^{-r_b z})$, so layer $i$ (interfaces $z_{h,i-1}$, $z_{h,i}$) holds $Y(z_{h,i}) - Y(z_{h,i-1})$ and the bottom layer also takes the roots below the column. The fractions sum to one on any grid.

Aeolus.SurfaceExchange.step_surface! — Method
step_surface!(s::VegetatedLandColumn, forcing, dt; cosz = s.cosz,
              diffuse_fraction = s.diffuse_fraction, co2 = s.co2,
              gustiness = 0) -> SurfaceFluxes

Advance the vegetated column by dt seconds: canopy iteration (surface layer, stomata, leaf energy balance, canopy air), then soil heat with the ground balance linearised in the ground temperature, then soil water with rain, dew, ground evaporation and root uptake. Returns the column's SurfaceFluxes (canopy + ground; $R_n - H - LE - G$ equals the canopy residual, ≈ 0). Details in VegetatedLandColumn; the component fluxes are in s.last (LandSurfaceDiagnostics).
