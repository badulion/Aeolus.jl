Land, urban and surface exchange

The land, urban and surface-exchange work is summarised, with its measured validation, in Land, urban and surface exchange: overview. The theory behind these modules is developed in Surface exchange and Monin–Obukhov similarity; the pages below are the generated API reference, one per module.

  • Shared contract — SurfaceExchange — Aeolus.SurfaceExchange
  • Soil column — SoilColumn — Aeolus.SoilColumn (Phase 46, Richards equation; theory) and heat conduction with freeze–thaw (#16; theory)
  • Photosynthesis — Aeolus.Photosynthesis (Farquhar C3 / Collatz C4, Ball-Berry / Medlyn stomatal conductance, Phase 47)
  • Vegetated land column — LandSurface — Aeolus.LandSurface (Phase 47, #21: two-big-leaf canopy over the soil column, soil-water stress, FLUXNET validation; theory)
  • Plant canopy — PlantCanopy — Aeolus.PlantCanopy (theory: Resolved plant canopy)
  • Urban facets — UrbanSurface — Aeolus.UrbanSurface (theory: Urban facet conduction, Glazing and window facets, Facet–radiosity coupling)
  • Building interior — BuildingEnergy — Aeolus.BuildingEnergy (theory: Building energy: indoor air and thermal mass, Occupancy schedules and BESTEST validation, Anthropogenic heat from the building energy model)
  • Snowpack — Aeolus.Snowpack
  • Lake and sea ice — IceThermodynamics — Aeolus.IceThermodynamics (theory)
  • Lake and sea surface — OceanMixedLayer — Aeolus.OceanMixedLayer
  • Air–sea fluxes — AirSeaFlux — Aeolus.AirSeaFlux (wave-dependent roughness, COARE bulk fluxes, sea spray, full COARE with cool skin and warm layer)
  • Sub-grid surface tiles — SurfaceTiles — Aeolus.SurfaceTiles
