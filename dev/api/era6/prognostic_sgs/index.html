Prognostic SGS TKE and Reynolds stress — PrognosticSGS

PrognosticSGS is the Deardorff (1980) 1.5-order closure with a prognostic subgrid-scale turbulent kinetic energy $e$ and the Moeng (1984) coefficients (Phase 56, P56-b, #182). TKEClosure is an AbstractSGSClosure of the P54-a contract, so every core and time stepper takes it as sgs = closure: sgs_coefficients! reads $e$, advance_sgs! integrates the TKE equation once per accepted step, and a step step_adaptive! rejects is rolled back exactly. The theory, the discrete duality of the production terms and the measured validation are on Prognostic SGS TKE (Deardorff–Moeng).

The mixing length is capped at $κ d$ with the distance of IBM.wall_distance, and Moeng's factor 3.9 on $c_ε$ applies at IBM.first_level_cells (both documented with Aeolus.IBM on Physics modules). Where a wall model replaces the SGS stencil flux through immersed walls (WallModels.immersed_walls, the Berlin facades), attach_immersed_walls! (or the walls keyword) makes the shear production there the wall stress times the resolved shear instead of the stencil's (#232).

using Aeolus
m   = RegularGrid(; size = (32, 32, 32), extent = (1600.0, 1600.0, 1600.0), halo = (3, 3, 3))
sim = BoussinesqSimulation(m; θ₀ = 300.0, θ_flux_bottom = 0.1,
                           bcs = FieldBoundaryConditions(:periodic, :periodic, :wall),
                           θ_bcs = FieldBoundaryConditions(:periodic, :periodic, :noflux))
tke = TKEClosure(m; first_level = first_level_cells(SolidMask(m)))
initialize_tke!(tke, sim; e_min = 1e-3)      # e = 0 is a fixed point: seed it
step_rk3!(sim, 2.0; sgs = tke)
tke_budget(tke, sim)                          # Σ P_s V, Σ P_b V, Σ ε V, transport, Σ e V

ReynoldsStressClosure (P56-d, #188) carries the six subgrid stresses $τ_{ij}$ instead: exact production, Daly–Harlow buoyancy through the P55-c tensor operator, exact Coriolis, LRR-IP pressure–strain, the momentum flux $−∂_jτ^{dev}_{ij}$ through the P55-b stress operator, and Schumann realizability, with $l$, $ε$ and $ν_t$ the TKE closure's functions (theory: the Reynolds-stress section of the same page).

rsm = ReynoldsStressClosure(m; first_level = first_level_cells(SolidMask(m)))
initialize_reynolds_stress!(rsm, sim; e_min = 1e-3)   # isotropic seed
step_rk3!(sim, 2.0; sgs = rsm)                         # not step_imex!: sgs_explicit_stress
reynolds_stress_budget(rsm, sim)                       # Σ −τ:∇u V, Σ βh₃ V, Σ ε V, Σ e V
reynolds_stress_statistics(rsm)                        # realizability corrections, booked
Aeolus.PrognosticSGS — Module
PrognosticSGS

Era VI, Phase 56 (P56-b, #182): the Deardorff (1980) 1.5-order closure with a prognostic subgrid-scale turbulent kinetic energy $e$, with the coefficients of Moeng (1984),

\[\frac{∂e}{∂t} + \mathbf u·∇e = P_s + P_b − ε + ∇·(2ν_t ∇e),\]

\[ν_t = c_k\, l \sqrt e, \qquad κ_t = \Big(1 + \frac{2l}{Δ}\Big) ν_t, \qquad ε = c_ε \frac{e^{3/2}}{l}, \qquad c_ε = 0.19 + 0.51\,\frac{l}{Δ},\]

\[l = \min\!\Big(Δ,\; 0.76\frac{\sqrt e}{N},\; κ\, d_{wall}\Big) \quad (N^2 > 0; \text{ otherwise } \min(Δ, κ d_{wall})),\]

with $c_k = 0.1$, $Δ = (Δx Δy Δz)^{1/3}$ and $c_ε$ multiplied by 3.9 at the first level above a surface (Moeng 1984).

  • TKEClosure is an Operators.AbstractSGSClosure (the P54-a contract, #167): its memory is $e$; sgs_coefficients! reads it, advance_sgs! integrates its equation over each accepted step, and sgs_state / restore_sgs_state! make a rejected adaptive step exact.
  • The shear production is the discrete dual of the SGS momentum operator (dual_strain!): $Σ P_s V = −Σ \mathbf u·\mathbf T_{sgs} V$ exactly, so the energy the resolved flow loses to the closure is the energy $e$ gains.
  • The buoyancy production $P_b = −κ_t N^2$ uses the $N^2$ of Operators.buoyancy_frequency_squared! (P58-a, #170), which on the uniform grid is the face average the κt scalar operator differences: ``Σ Pb V`` is exactly the potential-energy change of the SGS heat flux.
  • The TKE closure has its own stability length scale (the $0.76\sqrt e/N$ limit), so no TurbulenceStability stability function (nor the P55-e StabilityCorrected wrapper, #187) is applied on top of it.

References:

  • Deardorff, J. W. (1980), Stratocumulus-capped mixed layers derived from a three-dimensional model, Boundary-Layer Meteorol. 18, 495–527.
  • Moeng, C.-H. (1984), A large-eddy-simulation model for the study of planetary boundary-layer turbulence, J. Atmos. Sci. 41, 2052–2062.
  • Moeng, C.-H. & Wyngaard, J. C. (1988), Spectral analysis of large-eddy simulations of the convective boundary layer, J. Atmos. Sci. 45, 3573–3587 (the c_k = 0.1 calibration).
  • Patankar, S. V. (1980), Numerical Heat Transfer and Fluid Flow, §7.2 (the linearised implicit sink that keeps $e ≥ 0$).

ReynoldsStressClosure(mesh::RegularGrid; τ = nothing, e0 = 0, C1 = 1.8, C2 = 0.6,

A differential subgrid-scale Reynolds-stress closure (Deardorff 1973): six prognostic cell-centred stresses $τ_{ij}$ (m² s⁻²) with

\[\frac{Dτ_{ij}}{Dt} = P_{ij} + G_{ij} + C_{ij} + Φ_{ij} − \tfrac23 ε δ_{ij} + ∇·(2ν_t ∇τ_{ij})\]

(terms in reynolds_stress_terms; LRR-IP pressure–strain with C1, C2, C3). It is an Operators.AbstractSGSClosure:

  • shared with the TKE closure, by function call: $l$ from tke_length_scale (with $N^2$ of θv, the wall cap ``κ d{wall}$),$ε = cε e^{3/2}/l`with [tkedissipationcoefficient](@ref) (Moeng's 3.9 infirstlevel),ν_tfrom [tkeeddycoefficients`](@ref);
  • momentum: add_sgs_momentum! adds $−∂_jτ^{dev}_{ij}$ through Operators.add_stress_divergence! (P55-b) with the edge placement of stress_edges! (the stress keyword of the cores is ignored);
  • scalars: add_sgs_scalar! applies the Daly–Harlow tensor $K_{ij} = c_θ τ_{ij}$ (daly_harlow_coefficient) through Operators.add_tensor_diffusion_scalar! (P55-c) — θ and qv; sim.νt holds ``νt$and `sim.κt` the isotropic part$\operatorname{tr}K/3``;
  • advance_sgs! integrates the stresses over each accepted step: the transport (with transport: the flux-limited advection of the diagonals, the plain advection scheme for the off-diagonals, and $∇·(2ν_t∇τ_{ij})$) explicitly; the Coriolis term exactly (coriolis_rotation); and the remaining sources by one backward-Euler step of the 6×6 linear system they form with $ε/e$ and $c_θ$ lagged at $τ^n$ — so a steady state of the discrete update is exactly a root of the stress equations. Then every cell is projected onto the realizable set (project_realizable, Schumann 1977), and every correction is booked (reynolds_stress_statistics);
  • sgs_state / restore_sgs_state! roll a rejected adaptive step back exactly; step_imex! refuses the closure (sgs_explicit_stress).

Ω is the rotation vector (rad s⁻¹): :auto takes $(0, 0, f/2)$ from a simulation with Coriolis (the f-plane the momentum uses), else zero. surface — nothing, or (u_star = M, k = K) as for TKEClosure: after each step the first air cell of every column takes the constant-stress surface-layer equilibrium — $τ_{13}, τ_{23} = −u_*^2 (\cos α, \sin α)$ against the cell's wind direction $α$, the normal stresses $(a_{11}, a_{22}, a_{22})e_s$ in the wind frame with $e_s = u_*^2/a_{13}$ (reynolds_stress_shear_equilibrium), the LRR-IP counterpart of the TKE closure's $e_s$ (booked: surface_energy). τ passes in six fields to share (the Berlin case passes its tracers); else they are allocated isotropic, $τ = \tfrac23 e_0 I$. Seed them with initialize_reynolds_stress!. transport = false when a driver transports the stresses itself. advection (:auto = WENO-Z5 if the halo is ≥ 3, else centred) is the scheme of the transport: the diagonals use it flux-limited (advect_positive!), the off-diagonals plain. Prefer a halo of 3: with centred differences the off-diagonals overshoot and the realizability projection fires (measured in a 16³ decaying box, amplitude 0.3 m/s, 60 s: 9140 corrections adding 2.8 % of the SGS energy, against none with WENO-Z5). The other keywords are those of TKEClosure. A condensate field qc (with p, #239) enters the $N^2$ of the length scale (cloudy-air $N^2$); the buoyancy terms $G_{ij}$ of the stress equations keep the θ_v gradients.

References: Deardorff, J. W. (1973), The use of subgrid transport equations in a three-dimensional model of atmospheric turbulence, J. Fluids Eng. 95, 429–438; Launder, B. E., Reece, G. J. & Rodi, W. (1975), Progress in the development of a Reynolds-stress turbulence closure, J. Fluid Mech. 68, 537–566; Schumann, U. (1977), Realizability of Reynolds-stress turbulence models, Phys. Fluids 20, 721–725.

TKEClosure(mesh; e = nothing, e0 = 0, c_k = 0.1, c_ε0 = 0.19,

The Deardorff (1980) / Moeng (1984) prognostic-TKE closure, an AbstractSGSClosure for every core's sgs keyword (see the module docstring for the equations).

Memory: the cell-centred SGS TKE c.e (m² s⁻²); e passes in a field to share (the Berlin case passes its registered tracer), else it is allocated with the value e0. Seed it with initialize_tke!: $e = 0$ is a fixed point of the equation ($ν_t = 0$ produces nothing).

Keywords:

  • c_k, c_ε0, c_ε1, c_l, κ — the coefficients (Moeng 1984; von Kármán κ for the wall cap); surface_factor multiplies $c_ε$ in the first_level cells (Moeng's 3.9).
  • wall_distance — interior-sized distances (m) from IBM.wall_distance; $l$ is capped at $κ d$. nothing: no cap.
  • first_level — interior-sized Bools (IBM.first_level_cells): the cells where $c_ε$ gets surface_factor. nothing: none.
  • mask — storage-sized Bool solid mask: $e = ν_t = κ_t = 0$ in solids and no TKE flux through a solid face.
  • surface — nothing, or (u_star = M, k = K), per-column friction velocity (m s⁻¹) and the interior level of the first air cell; after each step that cell is set to tke_surface_value(u*, c_ε) (the value is read at every step, so M may be updated in place).
  • θ₀, g_accel — buoyancy reference; nothing takes sim.θ₀, sim.g_accel (no buoyancy terms if the simulation has none). qv — :auto (the simulation's qv when it is moist), nothing (dry), or a field: $N^2$ then uses $θ_v$. qc — nothing, or a condensate field (cloud or fog water) with p its pressure (Pa; a number or a storage-indexed vector): $N^2$ is then that of cloudy air (#239, Operators.buoyancy_frequency_squared!), with condensate loading and, where $q_c > 0$, the saturated $N^2$ of Durran & Klemp (1982) — the stratocumulus buoyancy of Deardorff (1980), so the length scale $c_l\sqrt{e}/N$ and the buoyant production do not treat moist-neutral cloud as stable. Needs a humidity (qv).
  • transport — advect (flux-limited, AdvectionSchemes.advect_positive! with advection: :auto = WENO-Z5 if the halo is ≥ 3, else centred) and diffuse ($∇·(2ν_t∇e)$, the three-argument Operators.add_eddy_diffusion_scalar!) $e$ inside advance_sgs!. false when a driver transports $e$ itself (the Berlin :transport stage) or for a sources-only box.
  • Mesh. A RegularGrid, or a vertically stretched StretchedGrid (#259, the Berlin nesting parent): there the filter width is the local $Δ_k = (Δx Δy Δz_k)^{1/3}$ of each level ($Δz_k$ the cell thickness, the width the core's Smagorinsky uses on that grid, Deardorff 1970), the shear production is the dual of the stretched Laplacian momentum operator (volume-weighted, see dual_strain!), $N^2$ is the face difference over the cell thickness (the dual of the stretched κt operator), and $e$ is transported by `AdvectionSchemes.advectpositive!with the centred reconstruction (the WENO / TENO stencils assume uniform spacing), flux-limited toward first-order upwind so that it stays positive. A:uniformStretchedGridgives theRegularGrid` closure to round-off except for the transport (centred instead of WENO-Z5).
  • dissipation — :semi_implicit (default): the linearised implicit sink $e^{n+1} = e^*/(1 + Δt\,(c_ε s^n + r_b^n))$ (Patankar), $e^*$ the value after transport and production, $s^n = \sqrt{e^n}/l^n$, $r_b$ the buoyant destruction rate $κ_t N^2/e$; positive with no clipping, first order, and its fixed point is the exact equilibrium. :exact: the exact solution of $de/dt = −c_ε e^{3/2}/l$ over the step at frozen $l$, $e^{n+1} = (e^{*−1/2} + c_εΔt/(2l))^{−2}$ (then the buoyant sink).
  • walls — nothing, or the Aeolus.WallModels.ImmersedWalls whose stencil flux the momentum step replaces by a wall-model stress (remove_stencil_wall_flux! + apply_immersed_wall_stress!). The shear production at those faces is then the wall stress times the resolved shear across the wall instead of the stencil's: see attach_immersed_walls!, which also attaches them later.

Aeolus.Operators.advance_sgs! — Method
advance_sgs!(c::ReynoldsStressClosure, sim, dt)

Integrate the stresses over the accepted step dt (once per step, after the projection):

  1. with transport: the explicit tendency $T_{ij}$ of the advection (flux-limited for the diagonals) and of $∇·(2ν_t∇τ_{ij})$, $ν_t$ of $τ^n$;
  2. per cell, $τ^* = R(τ^n + Δt\,T)R^T$ with the exact Coriolis rotation coriolis_rotation;
  3. $(I − Δt\,L)τ^{n+1} = τ^*$ with $L$ the (linear) source operator of reynolds_stress_terms at the cell's velocity gradient (dual_velocity_gradient), $∇θ_v$, and $ε/e$, $c_θ$ of $τ^n$;
  4. $τ^{n+1}$ projected onto the realizable set, the correction booked; $τ = 0$ in solids.
Aeolus.Operators.advance_sgs! — Method
advance_sgs!(c::TKEClosure, sim, dt)

Integrate the TKE equation over the accepted step dt (called once per step, or once per accepted piece of an adaptive step, after the projection; the sum over the pieces is the step):

  1. the coefficients $ν_t, κ_t, l, s$ from $e^n$ and the new state's $N^2$; $P_s = ν_t D$ (dual_strain!), with the facade production of attach_immersed_walls! when walls are attached, $P_b = −κ_t N^2$;
  2. with transport: $T = −∇·(\mathbf u e)$ (flux-limited) $+ ∇·(2ν_t∇e)$;
  3. $e^* = e^n + Δt (T + P_s + \max(P_b, 0))$, clipped at 0 if negative (booked: tke_statistics);
  4. the sinks (dissipation, buoyant destruction), linearised implicit or exact (dissipation), so $e^{n+1} ≥ 0$;
  5. $e = 0$ in solids; the first air cell of each column set to tke_surface_value when surface is given (booked).

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.attach_immersed_walls! — Method
attach_immersed_walls!(c, walls) -> Bool

Tell the closure c that the momentum step replaces the SGS stencil flux through the immersed walls walls (an Aeolus.WallModels.ImmersedWalls) by a wall-model stress: remove_stencil_wall_flux! takes $ν_{t,e}(q_N − q_F)/Δ_d^2$ out of the tendency of every wall face $F$ ($N$ its zeroed neighbour across the wall) and apply_immersed_wall_stress! applies $τ = a\,Δ_d\,q_F$ ($a$ from immersed_wall_drag!). Returns true for a TKEClosure; false (nothing attached) for any other closure. walls = nothing detaches.

With walls attached, the shear production of every (face, wall) pair is

\[P_s V \;\mathrel{+}=\; \underbrace{-\,ν_{t,e}\,q_F\,\frac{q_F − q_N}{Δ_d^2}\,ΔV}_{\text{stencil share out}} \;+\; \underbrace{τ\,\frac{q_F}{Δ_d}\,ΔV = a\,q_F^2\,ΔV}_{\text{wall stress × resolved shear}},\]

the stencil share removed from the four cells around the edge exactly as dual_strain! put it there, and the wall-stress work split equally between the two cells the face separates. So $Σ P_s V = −Σ \mathbf u·\mathbf T_{sgs}V + Σ a\,q_F^2 ΔV$ is again the resolved kinetic energy the SGS operator as modified by the wall model and the wall stress take out of the flow: the energy the wall-model stress removes is the shear production of the unresolved wall layer ($∫ τ_w ∂U/∂n\,dn = τ_w U$ across a constant-stress log layer), booked in the first fluid cells, and no energy the resolved flow never lost is produced. Without walls the closure books the stencil's $ν_{t,e}(q_F/Δ_d)^2$, a production of a stress the step no longer applies.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.buoyancy_production! — Method
buoyancy_production!(P, κt, N2) -> P

$P_b = −κ_t N^2$ at cell centres. With $N^2$ from Operators.buoyancy_frequency_squared! (on a uniform grid the average of the two face gradients) this is the discrete dual of the κt scalar operator: ``Σ Pb V = (g/θ0) Σ z\, Tθ V$, the potential-energy change of the SGS heat flux. (A prescribed surface heat flux adds$(g/θ0)F/2`to the boundary cells insideadvancesgs!`; not here.)

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.coriolis_rotation — Method
coriolis_rotation(Ω, dt) -> R

The rotation $R = \exp(−2\,dt\,[Ω]_×)$ (a 3×3 tuple of rows, Rodrigues' formula) that solves the Coriolis part of the stress equation exactly: $dτ/dt = C(τ)$ gives $τ(t + dt) = R τ(t) R^T$ — a rotation about Ω by the angle $−2|Ω|dt$, which keeps the trace, the determinant and realizability.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.daly_harlow_coefficient — Method
daly_harlow_coefficient(e, l, Δ, r; C_θ = :tke, c_k = 0.1) -> c_θ

The factor $c_θ$ (s) of the Daly & Harlow (1970) scalar flux $h_i = −c_θ τ_{ij}∂_jθ$ (diffusivity tensor $K_{ij} = c_θ τ_{ij}$).

  • C_θ a number: $c_θ = C_θ e/ε = C_θ/r$ (Daly & Harlow's form);
  • C_θ = :tke (default): $c_θ = \tfrac32 κ_t/e$ with the TKE closure's $κ_t = (1 + 2l/Δ) c_k l\sqrt e$ (tke_eddy_coefficients), so an isotropic $τ = \tfrac23 e I$ diffuses scalars with exactly the P56-b $κ_t$. It is the Daly–Harlow form with $C_θ = \tfrac32 (1 + 2l/Δ) c_k c_ε$ (0.315 at $l = Δ$, $c_ε = 0.70$; 0.099 in the $l → 0$ stable limit), within the 0.1–0.3 range of the published constants — a calibration, not a published value.

Zero for $e ≤ 0$.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.dual_strain! — Method
dual_strain!(D, u, v, w, bcs) -> D

The velocity factor of the shear production that is the exact discrete dual (summation by parts) of the Laplacian SGS momentum operator Operators.add_eddy_viscosity_u! / _v! / _w!:

\[\sum_c ν_c D_c = −\sum_{\text{faces}} \mathbf u·\mathbf T_{sgs}(\mathbf u, ν) \quad\text{for every cell-centred } ν ≥ 0,\]

so $P_s = ν_t D$ (see shear_production!). Every flux of the momentum operator, $f_L = ν_L δu_L/Δ$ with $ν_L$ the cell value (normal fluxes) or the 0.25-weighted average of the four cells around an edge (cross fluxes), contributes $−u_p\,(±f_L)/Δ$ to $\mathbf u·\mathbf T$ at each velocity point $p$ it enters; that is apportioned back to the cells of $ν_L$ with the same weights. Halo shares are folded onto the interior cells they are copies of (periodic image or mirror, from bcs, the boundary conditions $ν_t$ was filled with), which is exact because a halo $ν$ equals its image. In the interior each flux contributes $(δu/Δ)^2 ≥ 0$, so $D ≥ 0$; for $u = Sz$, $D = S^2 = |S|^2$.

D lives at CellCenter; the halos of u, v, w must be filled. Built from the same flux locations as the momentum kernels (the face-to-face and edge differences), not from Operators._velocity_gradient_at, whose off-diagonal two-cell differences are not the dual of that operator.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.dual_velocity_gradient — Method
dual_velocity_gradient(u, v, w, i, j, k; bounded = (false, false, false), mask = nothing)
    -> NTuple{9}

The velocity gradient $G_{ik} = ∂u_i/∂x_k$ at the centre of cell (i, j, k) (storage indices), row by row, that is the exact dual of the stress placement of stress_edges!: the diagonal is the face-to-face difference through the cell, each off-diagonal the mean of its edge differences over the cell's four edges in that plane (a closed edge counts zero). The production $P_{ij}$ of ReynoldsStressClosure is built from it, so the resolved kinetic energy the stress divergence removes is exactly the SGS energy produced. For a linear velocity field it is the exact gradient away from closed edges.

initialize_reynolds_stress!(c::ReynoldsStressClosure, sim; e_min = 0) -> c

Seed the stresses isotropic, $τ = \tfrac23 e I$, with $e$ the local equilibrium of the TKE closure, tke_equilibrium of the cell's dual_strain! shear, $N^2$, wall cap and $c_ε$ factor, at least e_min. ($τ = 0$ is a fixed point of the stress equations.)

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.initialize_reynolds_stress! — Method
initialize_reynolds_stress!(c::ReynoldsStressClosure, τ::SymmetricTensorField) -> (c, n)

Copy a subfilter stress (e.g. the exact one of a filtered DNS, Operators.subfilter_stress!, P56-c, resampled to the LES grid) into the closure's six stresses — the a-priori-in-time start of the P56-e validation (#192). Returns the closure and n, the number of cells the projection changed. Solid cells get $τ = 0$.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.initialize_tke! — Method
initialize_tke!(c::TKEClosure, sim; e_min = 0) -> c

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.is_realizable — Method
is_realizable(τ; rtol = 1e-12) -> Bool

Schumann's (1977) realizability conditions for a symmetric stress τ: $τ_{αα} ≥ 0$, $τ_{αβ}^2 ≤ τ_{αα}τ_{ββ}$ and $\det τ ≥ 0$ — all the principal minors non-negative, i.e. τ positive semi-definite — with a round-off slack rtol relative to $e^n$ (n the order of the minor).

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.project_realizable — Method
project_realizable(τ) -> (τp, changed::Bool)

The nearest realizable (positive semi-definite) stress to τ in the Frobenius norm: the eigenvalues of τ clipped at zero (Jacobi diagonalisation). τ itself, unchanged, when is_realizable. The trace can only grow: $½ \operatorname{tr}(τ_p − τ)$ is the energy the correction adds.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.reynolds_stress_budget — Method
reynolds_stress_budget(c::ReynoldsStressClosure, sim) -> NamedTuple

Instantaneous volume integrals at the current state (m⁵ s⁻³; pure): shear $Σ −τ_{ik}G_{ik} V$ with the dual gradient (so it is minus the resolved-KE tendency of the stress divergence, up to the isotropic part times the discrete divergence), buoyancy $Σ βh_3 V$, dissipation $Σ ε V$, and tke $Σ e V$ (m⁵ s⁻²). Fills halos.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.reynolds_stress_rhs — Method
reynolds_stress_rhs(τ, G, dθ, r, cθ, β; C1, C2, C3, Ω) -> NTuple{6}

The local right-hand side $P + G + C + Φ − \tfrac23 εδ$ of the Reynolds-stress equation (the rhs of reynolds_stress_terms).

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.reynolds_stress_shear_equilibrium — Method
reynolds_stress_shear_equilibrium(; C1 = 1.8, C2 = 0.6) -> (a11, a22, a13)

The LRR-IP equilibrium of a homogeneous shear $∂u_1/∂x_3$ at production = dissipation (the equilibrium of the SGS closure, whose $ε/e$ grows with $e$): $τ_{11}/e = a11$, $τ_{22}/e = τ_{33}/e = a22$ and $−τ_{13}/e = a13$, from $P_{ij} + Φ_{ij} = \tfrac23 εδ_{ij}$ with $P = ε$:

$a22 = \tfrac23 − \tfrac23(1 − C_2)/C_1$, $a11 = \tfrac23 + (2(1 − C_2) + \tfrac23 C_2 − \tfrac23)/C_1$, $a13 = \sqrt{(1 − C_2) a22/C_1}$ — 0.962963, 0.518519, 0.339450 for the LRR-IP constants. The surface value of ReynoldsStressClosure uses it.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.reynolds_stress_statistics — Method
reynolds_stress_statistics(c::ReynoldsStressClosure) -> NamedTuple

What advance_sgs! booked: clips (cell updates projected onto the realizable set, cumulative) and clipped_energy (the SGS energy $Σ ½ \operatorname{tr}(τ_p − τ) V$ those projections added, m⁵ s⁻², cumulative); step_clips, step_clipped_energy (the last step's); and the volume integrals of the last step (m⁵ s⁻³, at $τ^{n+1}$): shear ($Σ −τ_{ik}G_{ik} V$), buoyancy ($Σ βh_3 V$), dissipation ($Σ ε V$, the rate the implicit step applied), transport; steps; surface_energy (the SGS energy setting the surface value added, m⁵ s⁻², cumulative); step_split_cells (cells of the last step whose source update was sub-stepped, see reynolds_stress_step); step_unrealizable_input (cells of the last step whose stresses were already unrealizable before the source update — after the transport, whether the closure's own or a driver's — the share of step_clips the transport causes).

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.reynolds_stress_step — Method
reynolds_stress_step(τ, G, dθ, r, cθ, β, dt; C1 = 1.8, C2 = 0.6, C3 = 0.5) -> τ′

The local source update ReynoldsStressClosure applies in every cell: backward Euler, $(I − h\,L)τ_{k+1} = τ_k$, with $L$ the linear map of reynolds_stress_terms at frozen r, cθ (no Coriolis). A fixed point $τ′ = τ$ is a root of the stress equations.

Backward Euler inverts a growing mode (production faster than the sinks) once $h λ ≥ 1$ — the update changes sign and a realizable stress becomes wildly unrealizable. So the step is split into $n = \lceil 2\,dt\,μ_∞(L) \rceil$ equal sub-steps when the logarithmic norm $μ_∞(L) = \max_i (L_{ii} + Σ_{j≠i}|L_{ij}|)$ — an upper bound on the growth rate of every mode — is positive, which keeps $1 − hλ ≥ ½$. Same operator in every sub-step, so the fixed point is unchanged.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.reynolds_stress_terms — Method
reynolds_stress_terms(τ, G, dθ, r, cθ, β; C1 = 1.8, C2 = 0.6, C3 = 0.5,
                      Ω = (0, 0, 0)) -> NamedTuple

The source terms of the Reynolds-stress equation at one point, each a 6-tuple in the order (11, 22, 33, 12, 13, 23):

  • production $P_{ij} = −τ_{ik}∂_k u_j − τ_{jk}∂_k u_i$ (exact);
  • buoyancy $G_{ij} = β(δ_{i3}h_j + δ_{j3}h_i)$ with the Daly & Harlow (1970) heat flux $h_i = −c_θ τ_{ij}∂_jθ_v$ ($c_θ = C_θ e/ε$);
  • coriolis $C_{ij} = −2Ω_k(ε_{ikm}τ_{mj} + ε_{jkm}τ_{mi})$ (trace-free);
  • pressure_strain, the LRR-IP model of Launder, Reece & Rodi (1975) with the buoyancy term of Gibson & Launder (1978): $Φ_{ij} = −C_1 (ε/e)(τ_{ij} − \tfrac23 eδ_{ij}) − C_2(P_{ij} − \tfrac13 P_{kk}δ_{ij}) − C_3(G_{ij} − \tfrac13 G_{kk}δ_{ij})$;
  • dissipation $−\tfrac23 ε δ_{ij}$ (local isotropy),

and rhs, their sum. G is the velocity gradient $G_{ik} = ∂u_i/∂x_k$ as a 9-tuple, row by row; dθ is $∇θ_v$; r = ε/e (s⁻¹, so $ε = r e$); cθ the Daly–Harlow factor (s; $h = −c_θ τ ∇θ_v$); β = g/θ₀. For frozen r and cθ every term is linear in τ — the 3-D closure builds its implicit step from that (see ReynoldsStressClosure).

Half the trace of rhs is $−τ_{ik}∂_k u_i + β h_3 − ε$, the TKE equation's production, buoyancy and dissipation (the pressure-strain and Coriolis terms are trace-free).

References: Launder, B. E., Reece, G. J. & Rodi, W. (1975), J. Fluid Mech. 68, 537; Gibson, M. M. & Launder, B. E. (1978), J. Fluid Mech. 86, 491; Daly, B. J. & Harlow, F. H. (1970), Phys. Fluids 13, 2634; Deardorff, J. W. (1973), J. Fluids Eng. 95, 429.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.shear_production! — Method
shear_production!(P, u, v, w, νt, bcs) -> P

$P_s = ν_t D$ with $D$ from dual_strain! (P holds $D$ on the way): the shear production whose volume sum is exactly the resolved kinetic energy the SGS momentum operator removes, $Σ P_s V = −Σ \mathbf u·\mathbf T_{sgs}(ν_t) V$. νt must have filled halos (with bcs), as the momentum operator needs.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.stress_anisotropy — Method
stress_anisotropy(τ) -> (b, II, III)

The anisotropy tensor $b_{ij} = τ_{ij}/(2e) − δ_{ij}/3$ (6-tuple) and its invariants $II = b_{ij}b_{ji}$ and $III = b_{ij}b_{jk}b_{ki}$ (Lumley 1978; 0 for isotropic turbulence, $II = 2/3$ for one-component turbulence).

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.stress_edges! — Method
stress_edges!(S::StressTensor, τ::NTuple{6,<:Field}; bounded = (false, false, false),
              mask = nothing) -> S

Place a cell-centred symmetric stress on the C-grid of Operators.add_stress_divergence! (P55-b): the deviatoric diagonals $τ_{ii} − \tfrac23 e$ at the cell centres (the isotropic part is a pressure), the off-diagonals averaged from the four cells around each edge. An edge on a wall of a bounded direction, or touching a solid cell of mask (storage-sized, halos consistent with the boundary conditions), carries no stress: the wall stress is the surface exchange's. The halos of τ must be filled. With this placement the momentum operator and dual_velocity_gradient are exact duals, $−Σ_{faces} \mathbf u·∇·τ^{dev} V = Σ_{cells} τ^{dev}_{ik} G_{ik} V$.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_budget — Method
tke_budget(c::TKEClosure, sim) -> NamedTuple

Instantaneous volume-integrated terms of the TKE equation at the current state and $e$ (m⁵ s⁻³; pure: no memory changes): shear $Σ P_s V$, buoyancy $Σ P_b V$ (with the half-face share of a prescribed surface heat flux, as in advance_sgs!), dissipation $Σ ε V$ ($ε = c_ε e^{3/2}/l$), transport ($Σ V$ of the flux-limited advection and the $2ν_t$ diffusion: zero up to boundary fluxes), tke $Σ e V$ (m⁵ s⁻²), wall_work $Σ a q_F^2 ΔV$, the wall-stress work booked in shear at the faces of attached walls, and wall_stencil, the stencil production $Σ ν_{t,e} q_F (q_F − q_N)/Δ_d^2 ΔV$ taken out of shear there (attach_immersed_walls!; both 0 without walls). With walls, shear $= −Σ \mathbf u·\mathbf T_{sgs}V −$ wall_stencil $+$ wall_work, $\mathbf T_{sgs}$ the unmodified stencil.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_critical_richardson — Method
tke_critical_richardson(; c_k = 0.1, c_ε0 = 0.19, c_l = 0.76) -> Ri_c

The gradient Richardson number above which the Deardorff closure has no equilibrium $e > 0$ (the $l → 0$ limit of $P_s + P_b = ε$): $Ri_c = 1/(1 + c_{ε0}/(c_k c_l^2))$ = 0.233 with the Moeng coefficients.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_dissipation_coefficient — Method
tke_dissipation_coefficient(l, Δ; c_ε0 = 0.19, c_ε1 = 0.51, factor = 1) -> c_ε

Moeng's (1984) $c_ε = (c_{ε0} + c_{ε1} l/Δ) × factor$ (0.70 at $l = Δ$); factor is 3.9 at the first level above a surface.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_eddy_coefficients — Method
tke_eddy_coefficients(e, l, Δ; c_k = 0.1) -> (ν_t, κ_t)

Deardorff's (1980) eddy viscosity $ν_t = c_k l \sqrt e$ and diffusivity $κ_t = (1 + 2l/Δ) ν_t$ (Moeng 1984) for the mixing length l of tke_length_scale. The one definition: TKEClosure and ReynoldsStressClosure (its $ν_t$ for the $2ν_t$ transport and the calibration of its Daly–Harlow coefficient) both call it.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_equilibrium — Method
tke_equilibrium(S2, N2, Δ; c_k = 0.1, c_ε0 = 0.19, c_ε1 = 0.51, c_l = 0.76,
                l_wall = Inf, factor = 1) -> e

The equilibrium SGS TKE of a homogeneous shear $S^2$ and stratification $N^2$: the root of $ν_t S^2 − κ_t N^2 − ε = 0$ by bisection on $\sqrt e$ (0 if there is none, e.g. $N^2/S^2 ≥$ tke_critical_richardson; Inf without dissipation, $c_{ε0} = c_{ε1} = 0$). Built from tke_length_scale and tke_dissipation_coefficient, the same pointwise functions as the 3-D closure. Neutral, $l = Δ$: $e = (c_k/c_ε) Δ^2 S^2$, i.e. $ν_t = (C_s Δ)^2 S$ with $C_s = c_k^{3/4} c_ε^{-1/4}$ = 0.1944.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_length_scale — Method
tke_length_scale(e, N2, Δ; l_wall = Inf, c_l = 0.76) -> (l, s)

Deardorff's (1980) mixing length $l = \min(Δ, l_{wall}, c_l\sqrt e/N)$ (the stability limit only for $N^2 > 0$), and $s = \sqrt e / l$ (s⁻¹), the rate every sink is written with. s stays finite where l → 0 (stable air, $e → 0$): there $s = N/c_l$. With $N^2 ≤ 0$ and $e = 0$, $l = \min(Δ, l_{wall})$ and $s = 0$.

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_statistics — Method
tke_statistics(c::TKEClosure) -> NamedTuple

What advance_sgs! booked: clips (number of cell updates where $e^*$ was negative and set to 0, cumulative), clipped_energy (the TKE those clips added, m⁵ s⁻², cumulative), surface_energy (TKE added by setting the surface value, m⁵ s⁻², cumulative), and the volume integrals of the last step (m⁵ s⁻³): shear, buoyancy ($Σ P_b V$, signed), dissipation ($Σ ε V$), buoyant_destruction (the implicit sink of the negative $P_b$), transport, wall (the wall-stress work $Σ a q_F^2 ΔV$ booked in shear at wall-modelled faces, in place of the stencil share there: attach_immersed_walls!; 0 without walls); steps (commits).

Aeolus.PrognosticSGS.tke_surface_value — Method
tke_surface_value(u_star, c_ε; c_k = 0.1) -> e_s

The SGS TKE in local equilibrium with a constant-stress surface layer, $e_s = u_*^2/\sqrt{c_k c_ε}$: from $ν_t S = u_*^2$ and $ν_t S^2 = ε$ with $ν_t = c_k l\sqrt e$, $ε = c_ε e^{3/2}/l$.
