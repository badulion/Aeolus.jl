Budgets

Aeolus.BudgetSystem (ONE-c, #452) is the one budget system of the model: energy (the surface classes and the heat of the air), water, momentum and tracers, in one Budgets container. It replaces three systems that grew apart: the urban case's energy and water ledger, its air-heat ledger (#253) and the SBL run type's mean-momentum budget (P58-d, #194).

  • Measured budgets. A LevelMeter measures the per-level content of a field in the air. A StageBudget attributes every change of it to the stage entries of a step and the dynamics, with no reporting by the components. This is the stage-context measurement of the old air-heat ledger, and it is now the core mechanism for every measured quantity. The model's domains use it (with_budgets), and so do the urban case and its nest parent.
  • Reported terms. Components report surface energy terms per surface class (add_surface!), water sources (add_water!) and named heat (add_heat!, book!).

Each budget closes to round-off when nothing escapes: storage − Σ terms (stage_budget, surface_budget, water_budget). The mechanism is described on Components and the stage pipeline.

using Aeolus, Aeolus.BudgetSystem
mesh = RegularGrid(; size = (16, 16, 16), extent = (1600.0, 1600.0, 800.0))
θ = Field(CellCenter(), mesh)
b = StageBudget(; bins = 1)
budget_start!(b, LevelMeter(mesh, fill(1.2, 16), ones(16), 1004.6))   # ρ̄, Π̄, c_p
budget_begin_step!(b, θ)
budget_open!(b, :radiation, :sun, θ); interior_view(θ) .+= 0.1; budget_close!(b, θ)
budget_end_step!(b, θ, 60.0)
stage_budget(b).terms[(:radiation, :sun)]       # 1.2 · 1004.6 · 0.1 · 800 J m⁻²
Aeolus.BudgetSystem — Module
Aeolus.BudgetSystem

The one budget system of the model (ONE-c, #452; the design is docs/src/design/single_model.md, "Budgets and checks"): energy (the surface classes and the heat of the air), water, momentum and tracers, in one Budgets container. It replaces the urban case's energy and water ledger, its air-heat ledger and the SBL run type's momentum budget (since ONE-g #456 the SBL cases measure momentum like every other quantity), which were three separate systems.

  • LevelMeter measures the per-level content of a field in the air (the heat of the air, the mass of water or a tracer, momentum);
  • StageBudget attributes every change of one measured quantity to the stage entries of a step and the dynamics, automatically: the stage-context measurement of the urban air-heat ledger (#253), now the core mechanism of every measured budget. The model's domains attribute their stages through it (Components), and so does the urban case;
  • Budgets holds the measured budgets, the surface energy balance per class and the water of the air from reported sources (add_surface!, add_water!, add_heat!).

Components book their terms through Components.budget!; the closures are reported by stage_budget, surface_budget and water_budget.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.Budgets — Type
Budgets(; bins = 24, order = ())

The budgets of a run, integrated over the steps taken since they were (re)started. See budgets/budgets.jl for the conventions.

  • measured[q] — the StageBudget of quantity q of the air: :energy (the heat of the air; heat_budget, add_heat!) always; :water, :momentum_x, :momentum_y, :tracer where the run measures them (bins, order: of these);
  • surface[term] — per surface class, $Σ_{columns} ∫ F\,dt$ in J m⁻² (summed over the class's columns);
  • water[term] — $∫$ source of water to the air, kg m⁻² of domain;
  • water_air — change of the water in the air, kg m⁻² of domain;
  • terms[name] — free-form accumulated diagnostics (add_term!);
  • ncol — columns per class, elapsed (s), nsteps;
  • water_tracers — tracers counted as water in the air;
  • ρ — air density per level used for the water integral (kg m⁻³), the reference density every component converts its fluxes with; empty falls back to 1;
  • Ts_start, W_start — snapshots at the start of the current step;
  • dt — the length (s) of the current step, set before the components' budget! calls. Use it, not a configured step, to turn a step's energy into a flux: with the adaptive time stepper the two differ.
Aeolus.BudgetSystem.LevelMeter — Type
LevelMeter(mesh, ρ̄, π̄, cp, solid = nothing; W = nothing)
LevelMeter(sim, ref, solid = nothing)

Measures the per-level content $M_k = ρ̄_k c\,Π̄_k Σ_{ij} x\,Δz_k / (N_x N_y)$ of a field x on mesh (per unit ground area of the domain). For the heat of the air, $H = Σ ρ̄_k c_p Π̄_k θ\,ΔV / A$ (J m⁻²), x = θ and cp, π̄ are the specific heat and the Exner function; for the mass of water or of a tracer (kg m⁻²) π̄ = 1 and cp = 1.

  • ρ̄, π̄ — reference density (kg m⁻³) and Exner function at the Nz cell centres; cp — the specific heat (J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹) the model converts heat fluxes into θ increments with (1 for a mass);
  • solid — a halo-padded Bool array (an IBM.SolidMask's mask): solid cells are not air and are left out; nothing = all fluid;
  • W — the per-level weight of the quantity the dynamical core's transport conserves, $Σ W_k θ ΔV$ (1 for the Boussinesq core, ρ̄ for the anelastic core, ρ̄θ̄ for the pseudo-incompressible one). The ledger uses it to split the change of H by the dynamics into what the transport conserves and the redistribution between levels of different ρ̄Π̄ (see split_transport). Default 1.

The second form, the meter of the heat of the air, takes ρ̄, π̄ and cp from the fields ρ̄, π̄, cp of a reference state (an urban CaseReference, or the anelastic base state) and W from the core (Aeolus.Anelastic.constraint_weights for a sound-proof core).

Level thickness: mesh.dz on a RegularGrid, the cell thickness dzc on a StretchedGrid.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.StageBudget — Type
StageBudget(; bins = 24, order = ())

The budget of one quantity of the air, attributed to the stage entries of the steps (see budgets/stage_budget.jl). Fields:

  • meter — the LevelMeter (set by budget_start!);
  • acc[(stage, term)] — accumulated change of the quantity (per unit ground area of the domain: J m⁻² for the heat of the air), as an (Nz + 1) × bins matrix: row k ≤ Nz is level k, row Nz + 1 the part booked without a level (book! without k); column b the steps of bin b (the urban case: the steps that started in local hour b − 1);
  • storage — the measured change of the quantity, same layout;
  • elapsed — seconds per bin;
  • canopy_top — top (m) of the :canopy layer of stage_budget (the urban case sets it to the tallest roof);
  • order — the stages in the order the reports list them;
  • active — true while a step is being attributed.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.book! — Method
book!(b, term, J; k = nothing) -> b
book!(b, term, J::AbstractVector) -> b

Book J (per unit ground area of the domain: J m⁻² for the heat of the air) added to the air by term (negative: removed) during the current context — the stage entry or dynamics step that is running. It is stored under (stage, term) and taken out of what the context books for itself, so the context's own entry (for the dynamics: :transport) becomes the residual of the terms named inside it. k names the level the amount went into; without it the term is known only for the whole column, and layer budgets report it as unlocated. The vector form gives one value per level.

Must be called while a context is open (from a stage entry, or from code that runs inside the core step); called after the step it is an error: that amount would have no place in the step's balance.

Terms other issues book (#246): :top_flux (N-parent-b), :boundary_heat, :relaxation, :mass_correction (N-open-d), :feedback_heat (N-berlin-twoway, #267, in the parent's budgets).

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.budget_begin_step! — Function
budget_begin_step!(b, x, hour = 0; gap = (:output, :between_steps)) -> b

Start attributing one step: measure the quantity of the field x (any change since the end of the previous step is booked under gap, (:output, :between_steps) by default; a nested domain names it its nest exchange, NEST-p2 #482). With 24 bins the step is binned by its local hour; with one bin hour is ignored.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.budget_close! — Method
budget_close!(b, x; exchange = nothing) -> b

Close the innermost context: measure the quantity, and book the change minus what was booked inside it under (stage, name) (or split, see budget_open!). A nested context's change counts as booked for its parent. A deferring context commits what its nested contexts booked.

exchange = (stage, term) (a split context only: the dynamics of a domain with nest faces, NEST-p2 #482): the column total of what the transport would conserve, Σ_k conserved_k of split_transport — for a closed domain the numerics residual, for a domain with open faces what crossed them — is booked under that key as a column amount (no level), and taken out of (stage, :transport), whose column total is then the residual alone.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.budget_discard! — Method
budget_discard!(b) -> b

Drop what the contexts nested in the innermost deferring context booked (the model calls it when the core restarts a step it rejected: the stages inside the core run again with the shorter step). Contexts still open above it (a stage entry the rejected attempt left by throwing) are closed without booking.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.budget_end_step! — Method
budget_end_step!(b, x, dt) -> b

Finish the step of length dt (s): measure the quantity, book anything that changed it outside every context as (:core, :unattributed), and add the step's change to storage.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.budget_open! — Method
budget_open!(b, stage, name, x; split = false, scale = 1, defer = false) -> b

Open the attribution context (stage, name) on the field x. At the top level its starting content is the last measured value (nothing touches the quantity between two top-level contexts); nested, it is measured. With split = true (the dynamics) what the solid cells gained is booked as (stage, :solid_exchange) (negated: what the air lost to them), and the rest of the change not booked through book! is split by split_transport into (stage, :transport) and (stage, :redistribution), applied to air and solid cells together (the transport conserves their sum, not the air's alone).

x may be a tendency instead of the state (a component inside the core adds to the tendency, which the predictor applies over the step): then scale is the length (s) the tendency acts over, and the context books scale × the change of the measured tendency. defer = true keeps what nested contexts book until this one closes (see budget_discard!). A no-op unless a step is being attributed.

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.split_transport — Method
split_transport(meter, dH) -> (conserved, redistribution)

Split a per-level change dH of the measured quantity (J m⁻² for the heat of the air, e.g. what one dynamics step did) into two per-level parts that sum to it, according to what the transport conserves. With the per-level θ-sum change $ΔS_k = dH_k / w_k$ and $c = Σ w_k / Σ v_k$ (w the ledger weight ρ̄c_pΠ̄Δz/(NxNy), v the conserved weight WΔz/(NxNy)):

  • conserved_k = c v_k ΔS_k — its column sum is $c Σ_k v_k ΔS_k$, the change of what the transport conserves (in heat units), so it is zero to round-off for a flux-form transport with closed boundaries. Over the whole column it is the numerics residual; over a sub-layer it is the heat the dynamics carries across the layer's top;
  • redistribution_k = (w_k − c v_k) ΔS_k — the heat the ledger sees created or destroyed because the transport conserves $Σ W θ$ and not $Σ ρ̄ Π̄ θ$: moving warm air up into levels of lower ρ̄Π̄ lowers H.

The same split as water_split makes for water (#131).

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.stage_budget — Method
stage_budget(b; window = nothing, layer = :column) -> NamedTuple

The budget over the steps that started in the local-hour window = (h0, h1) (integer hours, 24 bins only; (20, 5) is 20:00–05:00, crossing midnight; nothing = every step; several days add up), for the layer: :column (the whole domain depth), :canopy (0 to canopy_top, the tallest roof in the urban case) or (z0, z1) in m above the ground (e.g. (0, 100)). Returns

(storage, terms, closure, elapsed, layer, unlocated)

  • storage — the measured change of the quantity in the layer (J m⁻² of domain for the heat of the air);
  • terms::Dict{Tuple{Symbol,Symbol},Float64} — the attributed changes, keyed (stage, component) or (stage, term);
  • closure = storage − Σ terms — round-off if nothing escaped;
  • elapsed — seconds in the window (storage / elapsed is in W m⁻²);
  • unlocated — for a sub-layer, the column-only terms (book! without a level) that it cannot include.

For a sub-layer, (:dynamics, :transport) is what the resolved and SGS transport carry across its top (for the whole column it is the numerics residual of the transport).

Aeolus.BudgetSystem.water_split — Function
water_split(budgets, mesh, dq, W = nothing) -> (transport, density_redistribution)

Split water_content(budgets, mesh, dq) of a transport increment dq into two parts that sum to it, according to what the transport conserves: $Σ W q$ with the per-level weight W (length Nz), or $Σ q$ when W is nothing (W ≡ 1). With $c = \bar ρ_m / \bar W_m$ (column means of the budgets' ρ and W) the first part is $c Σ_k W_k Σ_{ij} dq\,Δz/(N_xN_y)$, zero for a flux-form transport that conserves its $Σ W q$, and the second $Σ_k (ρ_k − c W_k) Σ_{ij} dq\,Δz/(N_xN_y)$, the water the budget sees created or destroyed because the transport conserves a different weight than the budget's ρ̄. On the Boussinesq core ($∇·u = 0$ conserves $Σ q$) that is the incompressible transport moving q between levels of different reference density; upward transport of vapour makes it negative. On the anelastic core the transport is ρ̄-weighted (scalar_advection, #133), W = ρ̄ = budgets.ρ and the second part is exactly 0; on the pseudo-incompressible core W = ρ̄θ̄. With ρ ≡ 1 and W = nothing it is exactly 0.
