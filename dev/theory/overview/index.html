Theory overview

Aeolus solves the filtered incompressible Boussinesq equations for a turbulent boundary-layer flow. This overview names the pieces and links to the detailed pages.

What we are solving

We integrate the equations of motion for a resolved velocity field u = (u, v, w), a resolved potential temperature θ, and a pressure p, on a Cartesian domain. The system is:

\[\begin{aligned} \frac{\partial u_i}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}(u_j u_i) &= -\frac{1}{\rho_0}\frac{\partial p}{\partial x_i} + g\,\frac{\theta - \theta_0}{\theta_0}\,\delta_{i3} - f\,\epsilon_{ij3}\, u_j + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\!\left[(\nu + \nu_t)\frac{\partial u_i}{\partial x_j}\right] \\[4pt] \frac{\partial u_j}{\partial x_j} &= 0 \\[4pt] \frac{\partial \theta}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}(u_j \theta) &= \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\!\left[\!\left(\frac{\nu}{Pr} + \frac{\nu_t}{Pr_t}\right)\!\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial x_j}\right] \end{aligned}\]

Here:

  • ρ₀ is a reference density (Boussinesq approximation — density variations enter only through buoyancy).
  • g is gravitational acceleration; θ₀ is a reference potential temperature.
  • f is the Coriolis parameter (f-plane approximation).
  • ν is the molecular kinematic viscosity.
  • νₜ is the subgrid-scale (SGS) eddy viscosity, closed by the Smagorinsky-Lilly model (see SGS).
  • Pr, Prₜ are molecular and turbulent Prandtl numbers.

The three big pieces of physics — advection, pressure/incompressibility, and buoyancy — each get their own pages below.
