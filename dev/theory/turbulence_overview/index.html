Turbulence closure: overview

The turbulence-closure work (Phases 54–59) rebuilt the subgrid-scale (SGS) model, the walls and the stable boundary layer around one closure contract. This page covers four things: that contract, how the closures, wall models and stability functions fit together, what the Berlin flagship runs by default, and the measured validation. The physics of each piece is on its own page (list at the end). The numbers quoted here are taken from the merged PRs (#203–#231, #240–#244, #271) and the roadmap.

One contract for every closure

Every closure is an AbstractSGSClosure of Operators (closure contract). A closure computes νt and κt from the current state with compute_sgs!. That call is pure, so substages, CFL estimates and rejected tries can call it freely. advance_sgs! then commits the closure's memory once per accepted step, and sgs_state / restore_sgs_state! let step_adaptive! roll back a rejected try bit for bit. All five cores take the closure through one sgs keyword: Euler step!, step_rk3!, step_imex!, step_adaptive! and the anelastic and pseudo-incompressible cores. The CFL limit uses max(νt, κt). The contract also carries:

  • The momentum form (sgs_stress = :laplacian | :symmetric). The stress form ∂j(2νij Sij) takes separate horizontal and vertical viscosities. It gives exactly zero tendency for solid-body rotation and dissipates −Σ 2νij Sij Sij to round-off. Its explicit limit is 8νΣΔ⁻², twice the Laplacian's.
  • The scalar form: a diagonal (κh, κv) operator, a general symmetric Kij in triad form, or the rotated isentropic tensor. qv and every transported tracer are diffused with the closure's κt (sgs_tracer_diffusivity!).
  • Traits: sgs_explicit_stress, so that step_imex! refuses a closure it cannot treat implicitly, and laminar_consistent.

Wrappers are closures too. StabilityCorrected(base) makes any eddy- viscosity closure depend on Ri_g and anisotropic. IsentropicDiffusion rotates its scalar tensor, and SolidMaskedSGS zeroes every coefficient inside buildings. A wrapper with neutral settings reproduces its base bit for bit.

The stability functions fm(Ri) and fh(Ri) are defined once, in TurbulenceStability (theory). The StabilityCorrected wrapper, the fog column and the core N² profiles all call it. Its MOSTConsistent family inverts Ri(ζ) = ζφh/φm², so a closure and the surface layer agree on the same stability.

Walls

WallModels (theory) applies a wall law implicitly at the end-of-step velocity, so it conserves momentum and stays stable far above the explicit limit. The laws are Spalding, a rough log law, MOST, and MOST with a prescribed flux. MOST takes κ and the stability functions from SurfaceExchange. The input can be sampled at a matching height and filtered in time and in space, which removes most of the log-layer mismatch. The same operator acts on z-walls, on immersed (mask) walls, with the stencil flux removed so nothing is counted twice, and on cut-cell walls through an image-point probe. Two further models are optional: a non-equilibrium TBLE column with a pressure gradient and the Wang & Moin dynamic κ, and the Anderson & Meneveau dynamic z0 for partially resolved roughness. A resolved NoSlipWall and moving walls cover laminar and wall-resolved flow (transition).

Stable boundary layers and transition

LargeScaleForcing (theory) provides the geostrophic pressure gradient, nudging, advective tendencies, a surface- temperature series and subsidence for every case driver. GABLS1–4 and CASES-99 are configurations of the model (its LargeScale, SurfaceLayer, retargeted Sponge and Towers components; the sbl run type until ONE-g, #456) (theory). The maximum sustainable heat flux (Van de Wiel et al. 2012) in SurfaceExchange predicts when the surface layer collapses. The Transition module measures the turbulent fraction γ, intermittency, Rif, Reb, growth rates and the relaminarisation criteria (theory).

Most of these are resolution-limited; the roadmap gives the evidence for each. The SBL runs are dry.

In the 3-D flagship

The Berlin case runs the TKE closure by default, with the stress-form divergence and κt diffusion of qv and every tracer. Every eddy-viscosity provider is wrapped in StabilityCorrected. The ground and facade wall models are on, and TKE production at the facades matches the facade stress. The :geostrophic coupling uses the geostrophic pressure gradient. `turbulencediag` writes N², Rig, fm, γ and the relaminarised fraction, and the surface exchange writes |H|/Hmax for every column ([the Berlin case](../design/berlincase.md)).

The Berlin night is not yet physical (#238). Its constant background ν = 2 m²/s is about 100× the night-time SGS κ, so it hides most of the closure differences measured above.
