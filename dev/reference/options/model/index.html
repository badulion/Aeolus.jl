Options: the model

Every key a config file of the model may hold is a row here. The guide is Run configuration.

[spinup]

The surface spin-up before the run (#519; PALM's spinup_time): the slow surfaces (soil, ground, roofs, building envelopes) advance alone over time seconds before the start, at negative model times, against a prescribed near-surface air and the sun, without the 3-D atmosphere, which starts as initialised. A fresh run only (a restart has spun up already). An urban site's surfaces.

keytypedefaultunitsallowed / rangedoc

pt_amplitudefloat0.0K[0, Inf]solar forcing: the amplitude multiplying cos(solar zenith) (negative at night)
soil_moisturebooleanfalsethe soil water evolves during the spin-up (PALM's calc_soil_moisture_during_spinup); false: frozen at its initial state

Cross-field checks

Checked after every option has passed its own checks; every failure is reported with the others in one ConfigError.

  • exactly one of time.duration and time.nsteps
  • noslip is a z-wall condition: not for boundaries.x, boundaries.y
  • an open face needs a domain dynamic_driver, the other lateral axis open or periodic, z walls, the boussinesq core, a uniform grid and no sponge
  • initial.field (isotropic turbulence) needs a triply periodic uniform grid and sets u, v (no initial.u, initial.v); energy_spectrum needs a triply periodic uniform grid, dissipation a uniform one; initial.velocity_perturbation.project needs the boussinesq core; time.start is 0 with open faces; coriolis.f and coriolis.latitude exclude each other
  • the surface layer (surface.model = "most") needs a uniform grid, z walls and surface_theta_flux.bottom = 0; its temperature = {kind = "driver"} and the [large_scale_forcing] driver tables need the domain's dynamic_driver; a geostrophic wind needs [coriolis]; sponge.target = "geostrophic" needs [large_scale_forcing]; the towers need a uniform grid
  • the anelastic core needs z walls, dynamics.theta0 > 0, no moisture and a uniform grid
  • [fringe] needs periodic x, a no-slip floor (boundaries.z = "noslip"), a uniform grid, a fringe shorter than the domain with rise + fall ≤ length, and dynamics.nu = velocity / inflow_reynolds; [kinematic_flow] runs in a box without open faces (a rotation with omega ≠ 0); [inflow_statistics] needs an [sgs] closure and a uniform grid
  • on a stretched grid sgs.model is none, smagorinsky or tke and sgs.stability is none
  • sponge.z_start lies below the top
  • an initial.qv profile needs dynamics.moisture = true
  • a Taylor-Green initial u or v needs periodic x and y
  • the enabled components hold at most one component per slot and an :advection provider, and meet each other's requires and incompatible (Components.component_problems)
  • every [checks] entry is published by the core or an enabled component (Components.check_problems): a closure check has the requirements of its budget_* output
  • [checks.reference] comparisons need checks.reference_file, which must hold each quantity, and their variables are in the output catalogue; a reference_file without comparisons is an error
  • a run with a coupling on ([forcing], [surface], [microphysics], [tracer], [particles], [obstacles]) has [checkpoint] off, the boussinesq core, a fixed time.dt, a uniform grid and no open faces
  • a surface model ([surface] model = "slab" or "flux_map") needs z walls and surface_theta_flux.bottom = 0; the slab needs surface.theta_ref or dynamics.theta0
  • Kessler microphysics needs dynamics.moisture and z walls; initial.ql needs it
  • [tracer] sources or a decay time need tracer.enabled; particles need a triply periodic box
  • an urban site ([urban] enabled) selects a compatible set of providers (the declared incompatibilities, with their reasons), with options of the right form
  • an urban site takes a fixed [time] dt with nsteps (duration = "none"), a uniform grid and no moisture (its water is tracers)
  • [urban] theta_top is "initial_gradient", "noflux" or a gradient in K/m; [checkpoint] needs the Boussinesq core
  • the first domain is the root (no parent); every later domain names an earlier one as its parent
  • a root has no origin or refinement, and feedback stays two_way (it has no parent); a nested domain gives both
  • a nested model steps every domain with time.dt: a fixed dt (each child takes max(refinement) substeps per step of its parent) or adaptive (the root picks its step, each child the smallest whole number of equal substeps that keeps it stable; a failed step retries the window); with [nesting] budget_exchange = "off" it measures no budgets (no budget_* output, no closure check); with "exchange_term" (the default) each domain closes its own, with one nest-exchange term per quantity
  • every domain of a nested model runs the boussinesq or the anelastic core (mixed freely, two-way included: a conservative density conversion in the boundary data and the feedback) on a uniform grid between z walls; an anelastic nested domain needs [timestep] scheme = "euler" (its SSP-RK3 step takes no open faces), and one nested in an anelastic parent the same dynamics.theta0 and g (one reference profile)
  • a nested domain lies inside its parent (origin ≥ 0, origin + grid.extent within the parent's extent, its height at most the parent's), with grid.size = grid.extent / parent spacing × refinement
  • a nested domain may enable [inflow_turbulence] (synthetic turbulence on its lateral nest faces, with its parent's resolved statistics: statistics = "parent_resolved", no length_scale); the root may not
  • every field the nest exchanges (qv, the Kessler ql, qr, the tracer) exists in a nested domain exactly when it exists in its parent (the PALM rule; a nested domain could carry an extra field only with its own dynamic driver giving its boundary values, and has none yet)
  • a nested domain keeps its parent's [boundaries], has no dynamic_driver and none of the couplings ([forcing], [surface], [microphysics], [tracer], [particles], [obstacles])
  • with a fixed time.dt, time.cfl and time.dt_max keep their defaults (they belong to adaptive steps), except in a nest with an urban site, whose root steps adaptively with them while time.dt is the site's step
  • every output.variables entry is in the catalogue of the core or of an enabled component (Components.output_problems): the qv ones and budget_water need moisture, ql, qr Kessler, tracer and budget_tracer the tracer, budget_energy on the boussinesq core dynamics.theta0 > 0; in a nest, of at least one domain (each domain writes those it has)
