3D Taylor–Green vortex

The canonical benchmark for incompressible LES cores. The initial condition is analytically divergence-free, and (in the linearized limit) the kinetic energy decays as exp(-6νt).

Location: examples/taylor_green_vortex.jl.

$ julia --project=. examples/taylor_green_vortex.jl

Setup

u(x, y, z, 0) =  sin(x) cos(y) cos(z)
v(x, y, z, 0) = -cos(x) sin(y) cos(z)
w(x, y, z, 0) =  0

on 32³ over [0, 2π]³, triply-periodic, molecular viscosity ν = 10⁻² (Re ≈ 100), for T_end = 2. Time step from min(convective, viscous) CFL with factor 0.4.

Expected output

TGV N = 32³   ν = 1.00e-02   dt = 7.69e-02   nsteps = 26
t = 0.000   KE = 3.10063e+01   max|div| = 5.33e-15
t = 0.154   KE = 3.07439e+01   KE/KE₀ = 0.9915   max|div| = 1.71e-15
t = 0.462   KE = 3.02204e+01   KE/KE₀ = 0.9747   max|div| = 1.71e-15
t = 0.923   KE = 2.94203e+01   KE/KE₀ = 0.9488   max|div| = 1.50e-15
t = 1.385   KE = 2.85746e+01   KE/KE₀ = 0.9216   max|div| = 1.55e-15
t = 1.846   KE = 2.76550e+01   KE/KE₀ = 0.8919   max|div| = 1.67e-15
t = 2.000   KE = 2.73280e+01   KE/KE₀ = 0.8814   max|div| = 1.55e-15

What to look for

  • Divergence at 10⁻¹⁵ throughout — the projection is working correctly.
  • Energy decay: at t = 2, KE/KE₀ = 0.881. Linearized-Stokes analytic value is exp(-6ν t) = exp(-0.12) = 0.887. The ~0.7% discrepancy is real physics — nonlinear energy transfer to smaller scales.
  • Stability: no blow-up, energy strictly monotonically decreasing.

For a full validation you would push to a much later time and compare against the enstrophy peak at t ≈ 9 from Brachet et al. (1983). Not yet in Aeolus's benchmark suite.
