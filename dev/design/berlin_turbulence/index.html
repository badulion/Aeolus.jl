The Berlin case: turbulence and SGS

Turbulence diagnostics (#170)

turbulence_diag (components/urban/turbulence_diag.jl, addon, on by default) is the Berlin coupling of the stability contract TurbulenceStability (see the theory page). At the output cadence it evaluates, on copies of u, v, w, θ, q_v whose halos it fills with the core's boundary conditions (the model state is not touched):

  • the pointwise $N^2$ of $θ_v = θ(1 + 0.61 q_v)$ (buoyancy_frequency_squared!, the z-interpolation of the core's buoyancy), $|S|^2$ (strain_rate_squared!, the Smagorinsky stencil) and $Ri_g$ (gradient_richardson!);
  • per level, over the fluid cells: the mean $N^2$ (N2_mean), the Richardson number of the means $⟨N^2⟩/⟨|S|^2⟩$ (Ri_g_mean; the mean of the pointwise ratio is dominated by shear-free cells, where the $S^2$ floor makes it huge) and the mean $f_m(Ri_g)$ (f_m_mean). These are written as (z, time) profiles (OutputField dims :z) and summarised in the log line.

Option stability: :brown (BrownEtAl1994, the LEM / MONC functions, default), :lilly, :short_tail, :long_tail, :most (MOSTConsistent with the surface exchange's BDBH functions; the smoke variant). budget! is a no-op: the coupling changes no state. P59-c (#180) extends this addon with the transition diagnostics (below).

Measured (:smoke, after its 12 steps, noon, capping inversion at 60 m, Γ = 3 K km⁻¹ above). In the first two levels above the inversion (z = 65.6 m and 71.9 m) Ri_g of the means is 0.273 and 0.196 and the mean f_m (Brown et al.) 0.049 and 0.0057; with stability = :most the same cells give 0.101 and 0.069 (no critical Richardson number). Below the inversion θ is uniform, and the vapour lapse of the initial state ($q_v ∝ e^{-z/800\,{\rm m}}$) makes $θ_v$ decrease with height: $N^2 ≈ -7 × 10^{-5}$ s⁻² at step 0, and −2 to −16 × 10⁻⁵ s⁻² after 12 steps of noon heating, so Ri_g < 0 and f_m = 2.5–10. The same vapour lapse removes about 2/3 of the dry $gΓ/θ_0 = 1.0 × 10^{-4}$ s⁻² above the inversion (3.3 × 10⁻⁵ at step 0). Cost per evaluation (compiled): 0.15 ms on the 16³ smoke grid (:most: 0.89 ms, a root find per cell), 3.6 ms on the 64 × 64 × 32 flagship grid, once per output record (every 1800 s).

Transition diagnostics (#180)

P59-c extends the same addon (there is no second diagnostic addon; modules_used = (:TurbulenceStability, :Transition)) with the laminar/turbulent diagnostics of Aeolus.Transition (see the theory page), written as (z, time) profiles at the output cadence:

  • gamma_turb — the turbulent fraction $γ(z)$ of the fluid cells, turbulent_indicator! with $|ω'|/⟨|ω|⟩ >$ gamma_threshold (option, default 0.2) and the plane normalisation (gamma_normalization = :plane, or :volume); solid cells are never turbulent;
  • Ri_supercritical_fraction — the fraction of fluid cells with $Ri_g > 1/4$ (supercritical_fraction of the gradient_richardson! field above: Transition does not define a Richardson number);
  • Re_grid — $|U_h| Δ/(ν + ⟨ν_t⟩)$ (grid_reynolds_number) with the plane-mean horizontal wind speed, $Δ = (Δx Δy Δz)^{1/3}$ and cfg.ν.

The log line adds canopy (levels with buildings) and free-stream (above the highest roof + 1, below the sponge) means (transition_summary). smoke_check adds $0 ≤ γ ≤ 1$, no turbulent solid cell, the supercritical fraction in [0, 1] and a finite positive grid Reynolds number.

Measured (:smoke, after 12 steps). Summer noon: $γ$ = 0.74–0.99 on the nine canopy levels (mean 0.871), 0.02 at z = 59 m and 0 above the 60 m inversion, where the initial state is still laminar (free-stream mean 0.438 because it includes the 53 m and 59 m levels); the supercritical fraction is 0 in the canopy and 0.61 / 0.10 in the two levels above the inversion (domain mean 0.044). Winter (:winter initial state) at 06:00: domain-mean supercritical fraction 0.244, with 0.88–1.0 in the four levels from 53 to 72 m — drier air, so the vapour lapse removes less of the inversion's $N^2$. Grid Reynolds number at $ν = 2$ m² s⁻¹: 4.0–5.7 on the smoke grid (Δ = 15.6 m × 15.6 m × 6.25 m); on the 64 × 64 × 32 flagship grid after 60 steps it peaks at 5.7 (2.4 at the ground, 0.14 at the top) — below the "about 8" estimated in the issue. Cost per evaluation of the whole addon: 0.28–0.55 ms on the smoke grid and 7.0 ms on the flagship grid (3.6 ms before #180), once per output record.

Inflow fetch (#266) and the inflow generator (#268)

N-inflow-d measures the development length behind a synthetic inflow on the periodic half channel (the harness validation/fixtures/inflow_fetch.jl, theory: Development length behind the inflow). Its diagnostics (FetchStatistics, development_length) are not coupled; the generator itself is, on the nested child's lateral faces: the addon :inflow_turbulence (N-inflow-e, #268, nesting page). The Berlin child's fetch, day and night, is measured with the 24 h validation (#270); until then the analysis buffer of the observation diagnostics is an estimate (140 m).

Flat-plate transition (#185): stated exception

The spatially developing boundary layer of P59-d (fringe region, FreeStreamBoundary top, Blasius inflow, FreeStreamDisturbance) is not coupled: the Berlin domain is laterally periodic with geostrophic forcing and has no inflow direction, so a fringe and an inflow generator have no place in it. Inflow boundaries arrived with nesting (#265), and the inflow generator is coupled there (#268, above). Transition stays coupled through turbulence_diag above. The one shared piece that does run in every Berlin step is the damping profile: the Rayleigh sponge (sponge_α_max = 0.05 s⁻¹ from 75 m in :smoke) now takes its rate from Operators.DampingProfile, the implementation the fringe also uses. The smoke run's final state is bit-identical with the verbatim Phase-7 kernel reinstalled; the sponge's column kinetic-energy sink $∫α|\mathbf u_h|^2dz$ after the 12 smoke steps is 0.139 m³ s⁻³. No Berlin coupling was switched off.

Prognostic SGS TKE (#182)

sgs_tke (components/urban/sgs_tke.jl) is the :tke provider of the :sgs slot and its default: PrognosticSGS.TKEClosure, the Deardorff (1980) / Moeng (1984) closure (see the theory page). It is PALM's urban-LES default and the only closure here whose mixing responds prognostically to stratification (its $0.76\sqrt e/N$ length scale; no stability function is applied on top of it).

Not wrapping it in StabilityCorrected is by design. :tke has none of that wrapper's $κ_h ≠ κ_v$ anisotropy (one $Δ$, as in PALM): at $dx/dz = 2.5$ it mixes 3.39× more vertically and 1.84× less horizontally than StabilityCorrected Smagorinsky of equal $C_s$, and no :isentropic tensor is offered (details). Its $N^2$ (and the StabilityCorrected providers' and turbulence_diag's) is that of cloudy air: the ql tracer's loading, and the saturated $N^2$ in fog (#239).

  • The closure is the sgs of every dynamics step, so the cores evaluate $ν_t = c_k l\sqrt e$, $κ_t = (1 + 2l/Δ)ν_t$ from the committed $e$ and commit the sources (shear production, the exact dual of the SGS momentum operator; buoyancy production with the $θ_v$ of the qv tracer; the implicit sinks) once per accepted step; step_adaptive! rolls $e$ back with the state.
  • $e$ is the tracer :e (advect = true, positive = true, m² s⁻², written to the NetCDF file). The :transport stage advects it with the case's scheme and limiter and diffuses it with $2ν_t$ (its diffusivity), mask-aware; the closure runs with transport = false.
  • $l$ is capped at $κ$ × IBM.wall_distance of the building mask and the floor (option wall_cap = true), $c_ε$ gets Moeng's 3.9 above the floor and the roofs (IBM.first_level_cells), $e = 0$ inside the buildings.
  • The first air cell of each column (bus.k_air) holds $e_s = u_*^2/\sqrt{c_k c_ε}$ with the friction velocity of the MOST surface exchange (bus.u_star, option surface = true).
  • init! seeds $e$ with the local equilibrium of the initial flow, at least e_min = 1e-4 m² s⁻² ($e = 0$ is a fixed point).
  • budget! books nothing: $e$ carries no surface energy or water (its dissipation heating is neglected, as with every closure here).
  • smoke_check: $e ≥ 0$, $ν_t ≥ 0$, $e = 0$ in the buildings, $κ_t ≥ ν_t$, and (Boussinesq core) $Σ P_s V$ equal to the SGS sink of kinetic_energy_budget(sim; sgs = closure) to 1e-8. The smoke variant runs it without the wall cap and the surface value.

The default changed from :smagorinsky to :tke because the coverage ratchet then required a new src module to run by default and no frozen list could grow (the coverage lists were removed on 2026-10-06, #320); whether it stays the default is decided at review (#46). Berlin tests that pinned the Smagorinsky default now select :smagorinsky explicitly where they test its bit-for-bit equivalences (MLSGS, the commit counter, the stale-halo demonstration).

Differential Reynolds stress (#188)

sgs_reynolds_stress (components/urban/sgs_reynolds_stress.jl) is the :reynolds_stress provider of the :sgs slot. It is an option, a genuine alternative to the default :tke, and it uses the same module (PrognosticSGS), so no coverage list changes. The closure is ReynoldsStressClosure (see the Reynolds-stress section of the theory page).

  • The closure is the sgs of every dynamics step. The cores take three things from the committed stresses: $ν_t$, the Daly–Harlow tensor $K = c_θτ$ (θ goes through the P55-c tensor operator), and the momentum $−∂_jτ^{dev}_{ij}$ through the P55-b stress operator. advance_sgs! is the source stage, run once per accepted step: implicit sources (sub-stepped for growing modes), exact Coriolis, the realizability projection and the booking. step_adaptive! rolls it back with the state. A separate case stage could not be rolled back.

  • The six stresses are the tracers :tau11 … :tau23 (m² s⁻², written to the NetCDF file). The :transport stage advects them, with the diagonals positive and therefore flux-limited, and diffuses them with $2ν_t$ (their diffusivity), mask-aware. The closure runs with transport = false.

  • The wall cap $κ d_{wall}$ (option wall_cap) and Moeng's 3.9 come from the TKE closure's functions. $τ = 0$ in the buildings, and an edge that touches a building or the ground carries no stress.

  • The first air cell of each column (bus.k_air, option surface) holds the LRR-IP constant-stress layer:

    • $τ_{13}, τ_{23} = −u_*^2(\cos α, \sin α)$ against the cell's wind, with bus.u_star from the MOST exchange;
    • $e_s = u_*^2/0.3395$.

  • budget! books nothing, because the stresses carry no surface energy or water.

  • Incompatible with timestepper = :imex: step_imex! represents the SGS momentum by an implicit eddy viscosity, and it refuses the closure (Operators.sgs_explicit_stress).

  • Stated exception. The other tracers (qv, ql, the species, the pollutant) are diffused by the :transport stage with the isotropic $κ_t = \operatorname{tr}K/3$ (sim.κt), not with the full tensor.

  • smoke_check:

    • $τ$ is positive semi-definite in every cell and $τ = 0$ in the buildings;
    • the realizability corrections are below 1e-3 of the SGS energy;
    • $Σ e V > 0$ and $ν_t ≥ 0$;
    • the closure commits once per accepted step;
    • on the Boussinesq core, $Σ P V$ equals the SGS sink of kinetic_energy_budget(sim; sgs = closure) to 1e-6.

    The smoke variant runs the closure without the wall cap and without the surface value. That variant caught a code bug: a single backward-Euler source step inverted a growing mode at a street corner, and the projection then added more SGS energy than the domain held. The fix is the sub-stepping.

The canopy stresses are anisotropic, with $II ≈ 0.07$; in the neutral shear equilibrium of the LRR-IP model $II = 0.091$. The vertical component dominates: $τ_{33}/τ_{11} = 1.35$ at noon, where the canopy is convective and buoyancy produces $τ_{33}$.

The 02:00 value is the same, and that is physical in this case, not a defect. The canopy at night is still unstably stratified: the city surfaces stay warmer than the air, and a 5-minute diagnostic run from 02:00 has θ falling by 0.22 K over the lowest 75 m. Above the roofs, where the night air is stable, $τ_{33}/τ_{11} = 0.43$ (level 24).

The realizability corrections come from the component-wise transport. In the last step, 127 cells reached the source update already unrealizable at noon and 126 were corrected; at night the numbers were 632 and 616, or 0.1–0.5 % of the 131 072 cells. Over 30 min the corrections added 2.1e3 m⁵ s⁻² at noon and 1.5e3 m⁵ s⁻² at night. That is 0.09 % and 0.16 % of the final SGS energy $Σ e V$ (2.38e6 and 8.85e5 m⁵ s⁻²).

The closure is a cost option. Its advance_sgs! adds 72–82 ms per step to step!, from a 6×6 solve in every cell. The five extra tracers add 56–76 ms to scalar_advection. The whole step is 1.3× the :tke cost at noon and 2.0× at night. These were sequential runs on a heavily loaded machine, so the ratios are rough.

A-priori metrics, outputs and a 1 h comparison (P56-e, #192)

No new physics. Both prognostic providers now add a-priori metrics to their diagnostics (apriori.*, #338; a report line before). It comes from sgs_test_filter_metrics in components/urban/sgs.jl, which compares the closure's subfilter stress over the canopy fluid cells with the resolved (Germano) stress at the test-filter scale. That stress is $L_{ij} = \widehat{\bar u_i\bar u_j} − \hat{\bar u}_i\hat{\bar u}_j$, computed by subfilter_stress! of the LES velocity under the 3-cell masked box filter (P56-c). The line reports:

  • $k_{test} = ½⟨L_{kk}⟩$;
  • the ratio $e/k_{test}$;
  • the off-diagonal correlation $ρ(τ, L)$;
  • the median II of $L$ and of the model stress;
  • the fraction of cells whose model stress lies outside the Lumley triangle.

For :tke the model stress is the implied $\tfrac23 e δ − 2ν_t\bar S$; for :reynolds_stress it is $τ$.

There are new output_fields. :tke writes II_sgs; its $e$ is already the tracer e. :reynolds_stress writes e ($½ τ_{kk}$) and II_sgs. The smoke checks now also require $k_{test} > 0$, a finite ratio, TKE II ≥ 0, and RSM II in $[0, 2/3]$.

The canopy $ν_t$ of the noon Reynolds-stress run was not recorded; the night value is the one the issue asks for. The :tke values are the report's three-decimal ones.

  • Under both closures the resolved stress at the test filter is far more anisotropic (median II ≈ 0.25–0.30) than either closure's stress. The Reynolds-stress closure is ten times closer (0.08 against 0.006–0.008). In 2–10 % of the canopy cells the TKE closure's implied stress is not realizable.
  • At night the Reynolds-stress stress correlates better with $L$ (0.25 against 0.07). At noon TKE's does (0.21 against 0.13).
  • The Reynolds-stress closure costs 2.0× the whole step. It also makes ~10⁶ realizability corrections per hour at night.

Recommendation for the maintainer. Keep :tke as the default. The Reynolds-stress closure wins clearly only where its physics is structural. It reproduces the stress anisotropy in stratified decay ($⟨τ_{33}⟩/⟨τ_{11}⟩$ 0.76 against DNS 0.70; TKE 1.00) and has the smallest KE error under rotation. It does not win on resolved KE in isotropic decay, and its heat flux is not closer to the DNS (see the validation). At 2× the cost that is not a clear win, so this PR does not change the default.

Relaminarisation of the stable layer (#195)

P59-e extends the same addon once more (no new module, no list change): relam_fraction — per level, and relam_frac over the whole stable layer, the fraction of the fluid cells with $Ri_g > 1/4$ that are relaminarised, i.e. γ-laminar and carry $ν_t/ν < 10^{-2}$ (Transition.relaminarised_fraction; $ν_t$ the larger of the active closure's $ν_h$ and $ν_v$, Operators.sgs_fields; $ν$ = cfg.ν). The laminar mask uses the volume normalisation (option relam_normalization = :volume): a quiet, shear-free free stream is laminar against the domain-mean $|ω|$, while the plane-normalised indicator, being scale-free, marks its weak disturbances turbulent. The log line adds the percentage and the number of stable cells. For a laminar-consistent closure (Operators.laminar_consistent: :sigma) smoke_check asserts that $ν_t ≡ 0$ (bitwise) in the parallel flow of the current plane-mean wind on the Berlin mesh (a deepcopy of the closure on a scratch core, so the case's closure is untouched). The issue asked for $ν_t = 0$ "in laminar cells" of the running flow; the γ-laminar cells of the smoke run are not exactly laminar (weakly 3-D), so σ's $ν_t$ there is small but not zero (12 steps: max 0.117 ν, mean 0.007 ν over the 251 plane-γ laminar cells, the same as Smagorinsky's 0.086 ν / 0.010 ν) — the exact statement is the parallel-flow one. AMD and Vreman (:amd, :vreman, P55-d) do not declare laminar_consistent yet; their own smoke_check asserts the laminar-reference switch-off.

Measured — :winter from 05:00 to 06:00 (1800 steps of 2 s, before the 08:15 sunrise), 64 × 64 × 32: the lowest 50 m are convective (the warm city under 271 K air: $N^2 = -7 × 10^{-4}$ s⁻² at 9 m) and an elevated stable layer lies above (supercritical fraction 0.48–0.69 between 60 and 140 m). Of the 49 056 stable fluid cells 4.0 % are relaminarised with the default Smagorinsky closure and 4.1 % (48 750 cells) with :sigma — mostly at the ground level (0.42) and in the quiet top levels inside the sponge (0.61 and 0.91 at z = 184 and 197 m); the stable layer between them is γ-turbulent. The background viscosity $ν = 2$ m² s⁻¹ dominates the result: with $ν = 0.2$ m² s⁻¹ (not the default; same hour) the stable cells drop to 15 392 and only 0.1 % of them are relaminarised (grid Reynolds number 28.7 against 3.8 in the canopy). Cost of the added evaluation: one more laminar_mask! per output record (the addon measured 34.6 ms per call with Smagorinsky, 43.2 ms with :sigma, on the flagship grid under a shared load).
