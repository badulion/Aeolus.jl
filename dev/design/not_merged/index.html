Built but not merged

A catalogue of work that was implemented, tested and in most cases measured, but deliberately left out of master. Each entry says where the code is, what it does, what was measured, why it was left out, and what reviving it would take. The branches stay on origin. Do not delete them: they are the only copy.

Add an entry whenever a finished PR is closed without merging or only part of it is taken. Remove an entry when its code reaches master, with a note saying how.

How to read the numbers

Unless an entry says otherwise, "run 1" is the HPC benchmark of #566 on Fritz (NHR@FAU), 2026-11-10: Berlin 100 m (200 × 200 × 60) and 50 m (400 × 400 × 60), median s/step with loop_fusion = "if_possible", one process per configuration. The results are in benchmarks/hpc/results/run1/ on issue/566-hpc-benchmark (report.md, summary.md).

  • "Alone" is the add-one build against master.
  • "Within all" is all against the build without that group.
  • Differences under about 3 % are within the run-to-run spread.
  • Run 1 pinned the threads (JULIA_EXCLUSIVE=1). That slows high thread counts (#575) but affects every build in the same way. At 1 thread pinning plays no part.

The speedup integration of 2026-11-14: what was left out

The integration PR (branch issue/566-speedup-integration) brought these groups to master:

  • C: loop fusion, #533 (#545, #550, #551–#553).
  • B: the budget passes, #541.
  • A: the urban surface, #543 and #544.
  • E: the kernel fixes, cherry-picked from the D branches.
  • #542: documentation.

This is the build bench/all-minus-D that run 1 measured, plus today's master. Measured against master: 1.42× (100 m, 16 threads), 1.42× (100 m, 72), 1.39× (50 m, 16), 1.53× (50 m, 72).

Group D and group F were left out (maintainer decision, 2026-11-14).

D1. The TKE update as one cache-blocked pass (#554, PR #555 part 2)

  • Where: issue/554-tke-blocked, head 0dc066a0, PR #555 (base issue/533-sweep-interface).
  • What:
    • The generic blocked driver. The #533 slab-and-ring driver becomes FiniteVolume.blocked_update! with a BlockedUpdate interface, and the predictor is one such update.
    • The TKE pass. The prognostic TKE update advance_sgs! runs as one pass over cache-sized slabs (src/prognostic_sgs/tke_blocked.jl): ν, face fluxes, the limiter ratio, divergence, diffusion and the update, row by row, two rows ahead. Every expression comes from the separate loops' point bodies through adapters.
    • Selection. loop_fusion chooses it, and results.toml reports tke_update_blocked.
  • Measured, run 1: group D (D1–D4 together):
    • alone 1.07× / 1.00× (100 m, 16 / 72 threads), 1.11× / 1.09× (50 m);
    • within all 1.01× / 0.98× (100 m), 1.10× / 1.02× (50 m);
    • all against all-minus-D, s/step: 100 m 16 threads 0.289 against 0.291, 72 threads 0.256 against 0.249; 50 m 16 threads 1.036 against 1.132, 72 threads 0.859 against 0.874.
  • Why left out: with the rest of the speedups in, D gains nothing at 100 m and up to 1.10× at 50 m and 16 threads. In return it adds a second code path for each closure: blocked and separate, with their own tests.
  • Taken from it: part 1, the two kernel fixes:
    • E1, the update of e row-parallel and branch-free: 16ad00c9, cherry-picked as 4d247af2. Measured 2.63× at 1 thread, 1.30× at 72.
    • E2, the dual strain rate without bounds checks in the bulk: cf704ba3, cherry-picked as 9f7c68e1. Measured 2.40× at 1 thread, 2.08× at 72.
  • Not taken:
    • the roadmap section of #554, part 1 (6c61a9da, 71329614, 0dc066a0);
    • the review commits of the pass (7c638734, 949ab673, ef8f8337, 411aefa1, c7892591, 96b2567e). The _sqrt_nonneg note and the anelastic_cbl validation fix (411aefa1: it read κt from the closure's scratch) may matter without the pass. Check them before discarding.
  • Reviving it: rebase onto master and re-target the PR to master. The #533 driver on master is the pre-generic one, so 3c89e281 (the generic driver) is the first commit to port. Measure at 20 m (1000 × 1000 × 150) first: D was never measured there.

D2. N² and the strain rate inside the TKE pass (#556, PR #561)

  • Where: issue/556-tke-n2-strain, head 3d6ef342, PR #561 (base issue/554-tke-blocked). Needs D1.
  • What:
    • Without immersed walls, the TKE pass computes N² and the dual strain row by row instead of as whole-field loops ahead of the pass.
    • It also refactors N² into one point body for both grids (Operators.n2_args, _dz_level, _n2_dz for the StretchedGrid). That removes the StretchedGrid's own N² loop and chooses the moisture variant in one place (PrognosticSGS._with_n2_args).
  • Measured: part of group D (see D1).
  • Why left out: it builds on D1.
  • Worth keeping without the pass: the N² refactor is a one-source-of-truth improvement: two N² loops become one body. It could be extracted without the pass.

D3. Urban tracer SGS diffusion as one cache-blocked pass (#559, PR #562)

  • Where: issue/559-tracer-diffusion-blocked, head 11db7226, PR #562 (base issue/554-tke-blocked). Needs D1's generic driver.
  • What: Operators.blocked_eddy_diffusion! makes the masked and weighted scalar diffusion of the urban tracers one blocked update. sgs_diffuse!(::TracerSGSDiffusion) uses it unless loop_fusion = "never". The scratch is built with the struct, so a call allocates 0 B.
  • Measured: part of group D (see D1).
  • Why left out: with D.
  • Taken from it: E5, the masked diffusion vectorises. _open and _vertical_divergence did not inherit @inbounds, so every mask read was bounds-checked.
    • Commits: 33b3ed00 and the review's 811a9b8e, cherry-picked as 22505bde and fdbd6948.
    • Measured 1.78× at 1 thread and 1.21× at 72. On the development machine (200 × 200 × 60): 11.3 → 4.8 ms at 1 thread.
  • Not taken: the roadmap section of #559 (5ba96cfc, d6044d38, 11db7226).

D4. The Reynolds-stress closure's update as one cache-blocked pass (#560, PR #565)

  • Where: issue/560-rsc-blocked, head 232829b9, PR #565 (base issue/556-tke-n2-strain). Needs D1 and D2.
  • What: with transport, advance_sgs!(::ReynoldsStressClosure) as one blocked pass, built from the TKE pass's pieces generalised to several scalars.
  • Measured: the PR's own finding is that the pass does not pay as planned. The update is limited by arithmetic: the 6 × 6 backward-Euler solve costs about 350 ns per cell. if_possible takes it only with centred advection on several threads. The PR asked for a maintainer decision on that rule.
  • Why left out: with D, and it does not pay on its own.
  • Taken from it: E6, the Reynolds-stress source update allocated on the heap in every cell.
    • Commit: ea8c3fcb, cherry-picked as ad83640d.
    • Measured: the update is 1.20× faster at 1 thread and 5.00× at 72. Allocation per call drops from 857 MB to 0 B at 1 thread and to 0.1 MB at 72 (run 1, node 17).

E3 and E4 (#557, PR #564): code taken, documentation not

  • Where: issue/557-branchfree-flux, head f34e2fea, PR #564 (base issue/556-tke-n2-strain).
  • Taken:
    • E3, branch-free face values (7bb2cd6a → 536cbc4e), and its review test (f7ed09ad → 1239aa52). Measured 0.97–1.00×: no gain at any thread count.
    • E4, the moist N² row loop vectorises (0ff95557 → ef6a98a2). Measured 1.34× at 1 thread and 0.98× at 72.
  • Not taken: the roadmap and ka_port.md text of #557 (cec6b35b, f34e2fea).

F. The cache-blocked loop split in j as well (#558, PR #563)

  • Where: issue/558-blocked-j-split, head ee965c24, PR #563 (base issue/554-tke-blocked). Needs D1's generic driver.
  • What: the blocked driver cuts the interior into slabs in k and row ranges in j (SweepBlock, sweep_blocks, edge_rows), so the useful thread count is no longer capped at nk ÷ 2lz. Both the predictor sweep and the TKE pass use it.
  • Measured: never on Fritz. Group F was dropped from run 1 (maintainer, 2026-11-10). The PR has the development machine's numbers.
  • Why it may matter later:
    • At 20 m and 72 threads (#566, REPORT_threads_20m.md, item 13), the fused sweep's _b_sweep_slab! takes 21 % of the step with only 37.6 of 72 threads busy. The proposed fix there, splitting in j as well, is what this PR does.
    • Reviving it means porting it onto master's #533 driver, or reviving D1 first.
  • Ported for the predictor sweep (#566, branch issue/566-sweep-jsplit): master's fused sweep (blocked_predictor!) now splits in j with the same rule (sweep_blocks, edge rows, a third launch), without D1's generic driver; see the roadmap section "Fused sweep split in j (#566 thread utilisation, port of #563)". What remains here is the TKE pass part, which needs D1.

Thread utilisation (#566 follow-up): what was left out

A2. The step-start copy as a threaded launch

  • Where: commit 158a139b on issue/thread-utilisation, reverted in 4326dbf6 before the PR. copy_storage! launched _copy_point! over the array above 4 194 304 points (_COPY_THREADED_MIN), with a test in test/thread_utilisation.jl.

  • Why it was built: at Berlin 20 m on 72 threads, the copy of the state at the start of each step ran on one thread.

  • Measured on a 24-thread desktop, 1006 × 1006 × 156, after #577:

    • copy: 61.5 ms threaded against 61.5 ms for copyto! at 4 threads, and 72.7 against 60.6 ms at 24;
    • zeroing: 41.9 ms threaded against 17.5 ms for fill!.

    One thread already saturates that machine's memory bandwidth.

  • What is missing: the same comparison on a two-socket Fritz node. There a single thread reaches a small part of the node's bandwidth, and the pages are spread over both sockets by the first touch. Reapply the commit if the launch wins there.

Tools that are not in master

The launch trace (#566)

  • Where: bench/hook (f4e22fc5) and bench/hook-dyn (efb5ec4c and the test fix 5444aa27). The same commits are on bench/all (8a360b01, 6da4b9e4, 21196f5e).
  • What: Backends.set_launch_trace!, off unless AEOLUS_LAUNCH_TRACE=1. It measures wall and busy time per launch name, idle thread time inside launches, and the serial time between them.
  • Status: benchmarks/hpc/bench_run.jl (trace and profile modes) needs it. Every thread-utilisation finding of #566 comes from it. No PR has been opened. It belongs with the #566 PR, or in its own, so that later speedups can be measured the same way on master.

The HPC benchmark itself (#566)

  • Where: issue/566-hpc-benchmark. It has benchmarks/hpc/: the drivers, the scripts, the results of run 1 and of the 20 m reruns, the reports and the handoffs.
  • Status: no PR (HANDOFF_scaling20m.md §6, item 7).

Measurement builds (not for merging)

bench/master, bench/all, bench/only-{A,B,C,D,E,543}, bench/all-minus-{A,B,C,D,E} and bench/all-revert-E{4,5,6} are the run 1 builds. They are kept so that run 1 can be reproduced (benchmarks/hpc/HANDOFF.md §3).
