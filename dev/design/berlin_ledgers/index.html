The Berlin case: ledgers and tests

The budgets

case.budgets, a Budgets of the one budget system (Budgets, Aeolus.BudgetSystem, ONE-c #452: it replaced the case's Ledger and AirHeatLedger), integrates, step by step, the surface energy balance of every surface class, the water budget of the air and the heat of the air (below, #253). They are the budgets of the case's Domain (ONE-e, #454): the case's own component snapshots bus.Ts into budgets.Ts_start and the water in the air first in :clock, and after :budgets the pipeline calls every coupling's budget! and the case books the change of the water in the air. A coupling reports only what it did during that step:

callmeaning
add_surface!(l, term, F, sfc, dt)a W m⁻² map F on the columns, over dt s, sorted by surface class
add_water!(l, term, m)m kg m⁻² of domain added to the air (negative: removed)
water_tracer!(l, :qi)count a new tracer as water in the air (not one that is part of another, like P3's rime mass)
water_content(case.budgets, case.mesh, q)$Σ_k ρ_k Σ_{ij} q Δz / (N_x N_y)$, for measuring a change of a tracer
l.dtthe length of the current step (s); use it, not cfg.dt, to turn the step's energy into a flux — they differ under the adaptive stepper

Summation order (KA-b, #296). Every ledger sum has one fixed order, independent of the thread count. The water integrals build on level_sums(q), the per-level plane sums $S_k = Σ_{ij} q$ in the canonical order of plane_partials! (kernel port), combined over $k$ in ascending order; the heat of the air takes its fluid and solid level sums the same way (measure_levels!). add_surface! sums the step's column contributions per class in column-major order and adds that step total to the accumulated term. The couplings' own column totals — the surface exchange's evaporation, the aq_chem emissions and dry deposition, the parent's area sums of its tile fluxes — are column_sum totals (KA-m, #301): one work item per column, the columns' terms added in column-major order from zero, the same bits serially and threaded.

Surface energy. :Rn (net radiation into the surface), :H, :LE, :G (sensible, latent and ground heat flux out of it) and :dS (storage change of the surface layer) are the core terms. Every other term name is an extra flux into the surface. surface_budget(case.budgets) returns per class, and for the whole domain,

residual = Rn − H − LE − G − ΔS        (what the four-term balance misses)
closure  = residual + Σ extras          (round-off if nothing is missed)

The residual is a physical baseline: the terms this model has that the conventional balance does not. The closure is bookkeeping and the smoke test asserts it to 1e-6 W m⁻² (measured ≤ 3.5e-10).

Who books what in the default case: surface_exchange books, on each surface step, the whole interval's Rn, H, LE and G (the heat conducted into a soil column; a slab's storage C_s ΔT goes to ΔS instead), and every step the change of the extra energy it holds for the next surface step (in ΔS); fog_dew the dew's latent heat (dew_heat); buildings_citymean the window sun that leaves the wall cells (window_sun); glass the shortwave transmitted into the building (glass_transmission). Before #129 the slab booked the same terms per step and its Ts clamp as clamp; nothing is clamped now.

Water. water_budget(case.budgets) gives the change of water in the air ΔW (measured by the ledger over qv, ql, qr and every water_tracer!), the booked sources and residual = ΔW − Σ sources. Sources in the default case: evaporation (surface exchange; negative where the surface takes up dew), building_vapour, dew (deposition, negative), precipitation (Kessler rain out of the bottom), qv_clamp (the Kessler coupling's clamp), transport (the change of water the transport stage makes; zero for a conservative scheme, and measured at ~1e-19 kg m⁻² at ρ = 1 since #132) and sgs_diffusion (the same for the tracers' SGS diffusion, #177; ~1e-19). The ledger integrates the water with budgets.ρ, one density per level, the reference density case.ref.ρ̄ (#131). A coupling that puts a flux into the air with a different density than the ledger's shows up as a water residual, so a coupling converts every flux with ref_density(case, k).

The incompressible core does not conserve that ρ̄-weighted water: ∇·u = 0 conserves Σ q, so vapour carried up into thinner air loses ρ̄-weighted mass. The transport stage therefore books its change of water in two parts (water_split): transport, the column-mean ρ̄ times its change of Σ q (zero for a conservative scheme; before #132 the halo leak), and density_redistribution, the rest. Under a sound-proof core (#133) the transport is W-weighted and conserves Σ W q, and the split is by that weight: transport is c Σ W dq (c = ρ̄m/Wm), zero for a conservative scheme, and density_redistribution Σ(ρ̄k − c Wk) dq, exactly 0 on the anelastic core (W = ρ̄). Over the 1 h flagship run (06:00–07:00, before #132's halo fix) with moist buoyancy on, density_redistribution is −8.1e-4 kg m⁻² (72 % of the slab's evaporation, 1.13e-3) and transport −7.0e-4; with it off, −6.3e-4 and +1.5e-5. Without the split both would be booked as transport (−1.5e-3, against −2.7e-4 at ρ = 1 in the #127 baseline), and fixing the #132 halo leak could not bring it to zero. Kessler sedimentation has the same effect inside its precipitation term (rain falling into denser levels), which #143 owns.

The water integral runs over every grid cell, solid ones included. Before #129 the slab's evaporation and the dew exchange acted on level 1 of every column, which under a roof is inside the building; the ledger closed either way, so a closed water ledger does not show where the water went. Since #129 both act on the first air cell (bus.k_air), and the surface exchange's smoke check counts writes into solid cells (zero).

The site logs the ledgers at the end of the run (Components.run_end!) as Info events (event = :budget, one per class, the water and each air-heat layer and term, the values as key-values; #338), and the run monitor writes their means and closures to diagnostics.csv as budgets.* (Diagnostics). budget_report(case.budgets) is the same as a table, for scripts. The cost is about 1 ms/step on the flagship grid (the two water integrals), plus about 0.4 ms/step for the Kessler coupling's own bookkeeping.

The heat of the air

energy(case.budgets), a StageBudget, closes the heat of the air

$H_{air} = Σ_k ρ̄_k c_p Π̄_k Σ_{ij ∈ fluid} θ_{ijk} Δz_k / (N_x N_y)$ (J m⁻²),

the dry enthalpy $c_p T$ ($T = θ Π̄$) of the air above a unit of ground, with the case's one reference state (case.ref) and without the solid cells. The meter (LevelMeter, src/budgets/meter.jl) and the stage budget (src/budgets/stage_budget.jl) are functions of (mesh or sim, reference state, solid mask) and of the θ field, not of the Berlin Case, so the parent run of the nesting epic (#246) keeps its own with the same code, and so does every domain of the model (ONE-c, #452).

Every heater converts with the same weight. A heat flux q (W m⁻² into level k) raises θ by $q Δt / (ρ̄_k c_p Π̄_k Δz_k)$: ref_θ_per_heat(case, k) is that conversion, the inverse of the meter's weight. surface_exchange and traffic_heat call it; buildings_citymean applies the same $1/(ρ̄_k c_p Π̄_k)$ inline in apply_building_heat! (its ρ, cp, π keywords, passed from case.ref), and column_radiation, whose heating rate is already $dT/dt$ (K s⁻¹), divides it by $Π̄_k$ only. Every heater therefore uses the same weight, but the conversion is written in three places, not delegated to one function. Before #253 they divided by $ρ̄ c_p$ only and the air received Π̄₁ ≈ 1.004 times the flux at the ground. The meter weights θ with the dry $c_{pd}$; the surface exchange converts with the moist $c_p$ of the first air cell, so the ledger books its heating as $H Δt\, c_{pd}/c_p$, about 0.8 % below the surface's $H Δt$ at $q_v ≈ 0.01$ (exactly $H Δt$ in dry air). The ledger still closes, because it books what it measures.

Attribution is automatic. Couplings book nothing. Every stage entry of the case's Domain, and the dynamics step, is an attribution context (stage, coupling) of the pipeline's budgets (ONE-c #452; the case's since ONE-e #454): the ledger measures $H_{air}$ per level when the context closes (the value it opened with is the previous close) and books the difference. Contexts nest: the :tendencies, :body_force and :post_predictor entries run inside step! and are taken out of the dynamics' share (the first two measured on θ's tendency, which the predictor applies over the step, the last on the predicted θ). The dynamics' rest is split into (:dynamics, :transport), what the core's transport conserves ($Σ W θ ΔV$, $W = 1$ on the Boussinesq core), whose column total is the numerics residual, (:dynamics, :redistribution), the change of $H_{air}$ because the transport conserves $Σ W θ$ and not $Σ ρ̄ Π̄ θ$ (moving warm air up into levels of lower ρ̄Π̄ lowers $H$; the heat analogue of density_redistribution), and (:dynamics, :solid_exchange), the heat the transport and diffusion move into the building cells (θ is carried through the immersed boundary, so the solid cells' θ evolves and exchanges heat with the air through the numerics alone). A change of θ outside every context is still caught: as (:core, :unattributed) within a step, as (:output, :between_steps) between two steps. The smoke test asserts that neither is booked.

Booked terms. add_heat!(case.budgets, term, J; k) (or book! on a StageBudget) books J J m⁻² under (stage, term) from inside a running context and takes it out of that context's own share, so a context's entry becomes the residual of the terms named inside it. The dynamics books :top_flux, the heat through the fixed-gradient θ top (The θ top, N-parent-b, #254), this way; the nesting epic will add :boundary_heat, :relaxation, :mass_correction (N-open-d) and :feedback_heat (N-berlin-twoway, #267: booked in the parent's ledgers as (:nest_feedback, :feedback_heat) and (:nest_feedback, :feedback_water), see Nesting). Called outside a step it is an error: that heat would have no place in the balance.

Layers and windows. Every term is kept per level and per local hour of the step's start, so heat_budget(case.budgets; window = (20, 5), layer = :canopy) returns the 20:00–05:00 integrals of #238 for the canopy ((0, cfg.canopy_top); NaN, the default, takes the tallest roof), or for layer = (0, 100), :column. For a sub-layer (:dynamics, :transport) is the heat the dynamics carried across the layer's top. budget_report(case.budgets) prints the whole-run table (column, canopy, 0–100 m); stage_report(energy(case.budgets); window = (20, 5)) the night one.

In the Alexanderplatz scene the tallest roof is the Fernsehturm, clipped at the 200 m domain top (the next tallest roof is 143.75 m, the 90th percentile of the built columns 37.5 m, the mean 21.2 m), so the default :canopy is the whole column there; set cfg.canopy_top for the street canyons.

Tests (the former test/berlin_air_heat.jl and test/berlin_smoke.jl, removed in #320 with the rest of the Berlin suite; the heat content of the discretely hydrostatic reference against this analytic value is now asserted in test/anelastic.jl, the ledger closure by the smoke runs of test/urban_checks.jl). The meter against the analytic heat of a uniform-θ hydrostatic layer ($c_p^2 θ p_0/(R g)\,[Π^{c_p/R+1}/(c_p/R+1)]$: 3.1e-8 at Nz = 16, 2.0e-9 at Nz = 64, second order). A closed box (every θ source off): the transport's column change is ≤ 2e-16 of $H$ per step; $H$ itself changes by up to 3.7e-7 per step, all of it redistribution. (The issue expected $|ΔH_{air}|/H_{air} ≤ 10^{-13}$ per step for a flux-form θ advection. That holds for what the flux-form Boussinesq transport conserves, $Σ θ ΔV$; for $H$, which weights θ with ρ̄Π̄, the expectation was wrong.) H₀ = 100 W m⁻² into the lowest level of a flat, motionless variant: the attributed term and $ΔH_{air}$ equal H₀Δt to 3.9e-13 (relative). Every smoke run (everything on included) closes to ≤ 1e-10 W m⁻² (measured ≤ 1.9e-14 in all 39 smoke runs).

Cost. One measurement is one pass over the interior (20 µs on 64 × 64 × 32); the flagship step opens ≈ 29 contexts, 31.1 measurements per step: 1.06 ms/step warm (0.7 % of a 150 ms step; 1.27 ms of 178 ms over the 24 h run on a shared machine). The issue's target was < 1 ms/step; the measurements are memory-bound, so the rest would need fewer contexts.

The θ top

Since N-parent-b (#254) the θ condition of the top face is cfg.θ_top = :initial_gradient, on by default: FluxBC(Γ) with $Γ = ∂θ/∂z$ of the initial profile across the top face (θ_top_gradient(cfg), 3 × 10⁻³ K m⁻¹ above the 100 m inversion), the fixed-gradient top of PALM and DALES. Before it the top was a closed no-flux lid, still available as θ_top = :noflux (a number sets Γ in K m⁻¹). The physics is src/ (BoundaryConditions.FluxBC, the halo fill of src/boundary_conditions/gradient_top.jl; theory in Large-scale forcing); the case code is site/top_boundary.jl. qv and the tracers keep their no-flux top.

Ledger. The heat the top face carries is booked as (:dynamics, :top_flux) at the top level. The composed body-force hook captures $Γ\,ρ̄_N c_p Π̄_N ⟨ν + ½(κ_{v,N} + κ_{v,N+1})⟩$ in every dynamics stage (the SGS closure has just filled $κ_v$), and each accepted (sub)step books it times its length (dynamics_step!, each piece of step_adaptive!, step_imex!). What is left to (:dynamics, :transport) over the column is round-off: 3.5e-11 W m⁻² (adaptive) and 8.4e-10 W m⁻² (fixed step) on the smoke case against a top flux of 7.18 W m⁻², 3.1e-6 J m⁻² over the 24 h flagship against 6.2e5 J m⁻². With the IMEX stepper the residual is the CG tolerance (−1.0e-2 W m⁻² at tol = 1e-8, −8.6e-7 at 1e-12; −1.4e-3 with the lid), on the anelastic core the core's unweighted diffusion (2.0e-2 W m⁻², 1.8e-2 with the lid).

The flux is downgradient, into the domain (the free atmosphere above is warmer), and nearly constant: $ρ̄_N c_pΠ̄_N νΓ$ = 7.09 W m⁻² with the case's molecular $ν$ = 2 m² s⁻¹ is almost all of it, the SGS $κ_v$ at the top adding 0.12 W m⁻² over the 24 h. The nightly canopy air is 0.99 K warmer (20–05 h mean), 0.81 K at 20:00 rising to 1.14 K at 05:00, and cools 0.33 K less during the night (7.13 against 7.46 K). In the afternoon the convective layer fills the 200 m domain, and a fixed gradient then imposes $κΓ$ regardless of the real entrainment: the open top of the nested run (N-open-b, N-berlin-nest #265) replaces it.

Stated exception: no θ damping in the periodic child. θ is not Rayleigh-damped in the periodic 200 m child. Its velocity sponge starts at 150 m, and a θ damping toward a constant target there would clamp the top 50 m (#238, approach B). With lateral = :open (#262) the top is open and θ is relaxed over the same layer toward the boundary data's profile, the state the open top imposes (cfg.θ_relaxation, see Open boundaries); in the nested run that is the parent's. LargeScaleForcing.ReferenceDamping is the one θ-damping type in both places.

Open boundaries

cfg.lateral = :open (N-open-d, #262; site/open_boundary.jl) gives the case open west, east, south, north and top faces fed by analytic data (with the nest switched off, nesting.enabled=false; the nested default of #265 feeds the same faces from the parent, see Nesting):

  • Data. ProfileBoundaryData(u = U_g, v = V_g, w = 0, θ = initial_θ) — the :geostrophic coupling's wind (also when it is off) and the initial θ profile (case_boundary_data). The normal velocity of every face is mass-corrected by the uniform global correction (N-open-b), the outflow is cfg.outflow (:dirichlet, default; :convective, :orlanski, N-open-c).
  • Sponge. The core's Rayleigh sponge relaxes u, v, w toward the same data (sponge_target(data, mesh), the one damping kernel; a sponge below an open top without a target is an error), so the :geostrophic coupling's own retargeting of the periodic case is off. θ is relaxed toward the data's profile over the sponge layer (cfg.θ_relaxation = true, ReferenceDamping in the body-force hook): in the gravity-wave test of #254 damping θ as well as the velocity cut the reflection of a layer one vertical wavelength deep from 0.17 to 0.034.
  • Geometry. cfg.boundary_frame cells (default 3, 47 m in the smoke window, 47 m on the flagship grid) next to each open side are kept free of buildings; a building that still touches a face (frame 0, or the Fernsehturm through the top) is solid there: mark_open_boundary_solids! gives its boundary points zero velocity and takes them out of the mass-flux correction.
  • Neighbours. No coupling wraps across the box any more: every former mod1 reads through neighbor_index (centre_wind for the cell-centred wind of the surface exchange, the deposition and the facade wall model; the wall tagging; the a-priori stress centring); masks and tracer halos take case_scalar_bcs (zero gradient at an open face; a tracer has no boundary data, so it leaves with its own value and enters with the value next to the face); the pressure solvers read the all-Neumann axes from the case's bcs.

Ledger. The open faces carry heat and water. Inside the dynamics context the composed body-force hook captures, per level, with the state and the SGS diffusivity the core's tendencies were built from:

termwhat
(:dynamics, :boundary_heat)$-\sum ρ̄_k c_p Π̄_k (θ\,u·n - K ∂_nθ) A$ through the four sides and the top (boundary_scalar_flux: the core's centred θ face values, $K = ν + ½(κ_{in} + κ_{out})$), without …
(:dynamics, :mass_correction)… the part the global correction $δ$ carries, $δ\,ρ̄c_pΠ̄\,θ_{face} A$ on every face it adjusted
(:dynamics, :relaxation)the θ relaxation toward the data
water :boundarythe vapour and cloud water the faces carry, boundary_scalar_flux with the transport's own scheme (scalar_advection)

Each accepted (sub)step books them times its length (book_dynamics_heat!, also called for the fixed-gradient top). The mass-correction term is large next to its velocity because it multiplies the absolute θ (≈ 293 K): $|δ|/U$ = 7.8e-5 carries 19 W m⁻² in the convective run below, of the same sign as the net mass the correction removes; only the sum of the two terms is the boundary's heat. For the forward-Euler steps of the default :adaptive and the :fixed stepper the booked heat is the heat the step moved; under :imex it is the last stage's rate times dt (a residual in (:dynamics, :transport), as for the top flux: −2.4e-2 W m⁻² on the open smoke window); on the anelastic core the column transport keeps the core's unweighted-diffusion residual of #254 (2.2e-2 W m⁻² open, 1.8e-2 with the lid). The smoke check asserts < 1e-6 W m⁻² for the forward-Euler Boussinesq steps and < 0.1 W m⁻² otherwise. The diffusive part is the closures' $κ_h$/$κ_v$ form: the cross terms of the tensor closure (sgs = :reynolds_stress) at the faces are not in it.

The open top's heat is the diffusion of the Dirichlet θ halo (the initial profile, 3 K km⁻¹ above the inversion) through the top face: 7.19 W m⁻², the 7.18 W m⁻² of the periodic case's fixed-gradient top. With Dirichlet outflow the data balance ($δ = 0$); the convective outflow radiates the interior's building-perturbed wind at the faces and the correction removes the imbalance, which moves heat through the top as well. The column transport stays a numerics residual (1e-10 W m⁻²) in every configuration.

Smoke test

The smoke runs were test/berlin_smoke.jl until #320 removed the Berlin test suite; they were then the smoke part of the by-hand examples/urban/berlin_checks.jl, and since #438 are test/urban_checks.jl (a heavy test; julia --project=. test/run.jl --only urban_checks.jl; the flagship and parent runs are validation/urban_checks.jl), while every run writes its ledger closure to output/results.toml. They run the case in examples/urban/berlin_smoke.toml (each run of the matrix is that file plus --set overrides, CFG-c #358): a 250 m × 250 m × 100 m window of the real OSM file (16 × 16 × 16, dx = 15.6 m, 312.5 m south of the anchor, 69 building and 50 park columns, tallest roof 31 m) at local noon, 12 steps of 2 s, radiation every 8 s, soil every 4 s. Before the first step it seeds a fog layer from a fixed RNG seed (Xoshiro(127)): air saturated at the reference state (q_v = q_s(θ Π̄_k, p̄_k)) with ql = 0.2 g/kg ± 10 % in the lowest three levels and rain qr < 5e-5 in levels 2–3, so dew, fog, sedimentation and precipitation all do real work. The flagship's pollutant stacks are outside this window, so the smoke runs put one stack in it, 40 m up.

The run matrix is derived from the urban components (URBAN_COMPONENTS, the reference selection URBAN_DEFAULTS; smoke_specs() in test/support/smoke_matrix.jl, written as overrides by smoke_overrides):

  1. summer, everything on — every additive coupling on, including those off by default, and the default provider of every slot;
  2. winter, everything on — the same with the values berlin_winter.toml sets (its initial state);
  3. one run per non-default provider of every exclusive slot;
  4. one run per entry of every coupling's smoke_variants.

A run that selects a coupling declared incompatible with an additive coupling switches that additive coupling off; one incompatible with the default provider of another slot switches that slot to its first compatible provider (so core = anelastic runs with pressure_solver = density_weighted_cg, and that solver's own run with the anelastic core). Every run must finish without a blow-up, leave every field and tracer finite, pass every smoke_check of its couplings, and close the surface-energy ledger per class (1e-6 W m⁻²) and the water ledger (1e-12 kg m⁻²). The first run also asserts that the domain gains radiative energy at noon (Rn > 0, ΔS > 0) and that the transport conserves water (|transport| < 1e-12 kg m⁻²; measured ~1e-19 since #132). Finally every urban component must have run in at least one configuration. (As a test it also had to take less than 300 s; the by-hand script has no time limit.)

At #127 the matrix was 2 runs (no slot had a second provider yet); #130 added the column radiation's surface_sw = :bird variant, and #132 the non-default providers of the numerics slots (momentum_advection = weno_z, pressure_solver = multigrid and mg_cg, sgs = dynamic and mlsgs) and the scalar-advection variants (TENO5; WENO-Z7 with halo 4). Run on its own the test took about 66 s at #132, almost all of it first-call compilation (each run takes 0.3–1 s once compiled, the multigrid runs a few seconds). The :core and :timestepper slots of #133 add six (anelastic, pseudo-incompressible, pressure_solver = density_weighted_cg, adaptive, IMEX, adaptive + ARK2): 18 runs.

The coverage test test/berlin_coverage.jl and its lists (BERLIN_EXEMPT, BERLIN_PENDING, BERLIN_DEFAULT_OFF, BERLIN_OPTION_ONLY) were removed (maintainer decision 2026-10-06), and with #320 the rest of the Berlin suite: the checks that the "everything on" runs select every addon and default provider and that the matrix is derived from the registry (plumbing of the former flagship rule) are gone; the every-coupling-ran check stays in the smoke runs.
