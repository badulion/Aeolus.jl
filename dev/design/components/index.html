Components and the stage pipeline

Status: built in ONE-a (#450), the first step of the single-model epic, with the configuration schema of ONE-b (#451), on both dynamical cores since ONE-j (#460). The target is One model; this page says what the code does today, and where it still differs from the target.

What a step does

step!(model, dt) steps every domain of the Model: one alone, or several as a nest (ONE-f, #455; see "Nesting by domains" below). For one Domain:

  1. the domain's drivers advance to the step (the open faces' boundary window, advance_feed!);

  2. the components of the stages before the core run, in PIPELINE order: :clock, :nest_sync, :radiation, :sw_modifiers, :surface_flux, :sources, :chemistry, :transport, :microphysics, :tracers;

  3. the core steps — step! (Euler) or step_rk3! (SSP-RK3), on either core — and calls the components of stages 6–8 from inside every (sub)step:

    stagewhere the core calls it
    6 :tendenciesafter its own tendencies: advection and diffusion, the :sgs closure, buoyancy
    7 :body_forceright after stage 6, before the predictor
    8 :post_predictorafter the predictor u* = u + Δt F, before the projection

    Stages 6 and 7 run in the core's body_force! hook, stage 8 in its post_predictor! hook (callable structs, so the calls are static). When no component attaches to them the hooks are nothing, and a Boussinesq Euler step without SGS keeps the cache-blocked DNS core of SC-e (test/components_pipeline.jl checks that its sweep buffers are used). Stages 9 (:open_boundaries) and 10 (:projection) are the core's own operations; no component may attach to them yet;

  4. the components of the stages after the core run: :surface_update, :column_tendency, :nest_feedback, :budgets, :checks, :output.

A stage outside the core may have a cadence (Stage(:radiation; every = 60)): it runs in the step that crosses a multiple of every (floor(t₁/every) > floor(t₀/every)), and with first = true also in the step that starts at model time 0. Within a stage, components run by slot first (:advection, :sgs, :microphysics, :radiation, :surface), then by component_name, except where a component declares its place in a stage with Stage(…; order): a lower order runs first. The orders are the components' own (never configuration); they exist for work that depends on another component's in the same stage — the column radiation's fluxes before the radiosity that distributes them, before the buildings that absorb them (ONE-e, #454). The domain stores its components in the one order that meets every stage's declared orders and is otherwise by slot and name (order_components, a topological sort; orders that contradict each other across stages are an error), as a concretely typed tuple, and the pipeline walks the tuple recursively, so every run_stage! call is statically dispatched.

:transport (stage 5, between :chemistry and :microphysics) is the advection of the tracers components carry with the velocity at the step's start; the urban case's scalar transport runs there, as it ran in the urban stage loop. (The design's scalar advance after the projection, stage 10, would change the urban results; moving it there is a physics change left to the port of the tracers.)

An adaptive step (step_adaptive!(model), a domain with cfl) picks dt from the core's CFL limits and every component's limit_dt (compared right after the core's own Coriolis limit, so a Coriolis component reproduces the core's pick and its :coriolis limiter), runs the stages before the core with it, and lets TimeIntegration.step_adaptive! take it, retrying with half the step if the guard fails. The stages before the core are not repeated on a retry.

The urban site in a nest (NEST-p3, #483). An urban site is a [[domain]] nested in a domain of the model's core: it joins its parent's nest hierarchy while it is built (its :nesting component's init!, Nesting.add_nest!, a prebuilt NestLink; link_domain! links the domains before it), and the engine steps it through its own pipeline in each window, every step of the length the site picks (next_dt, landing on Nesting.nest_window_end); a fixed-step site makes the root's window a whole number of its steps (window_dt). Such a nest does not save and retry its windows: the site retries its own pieces.

The numbers the run types computed at the end of a run are now computed from the output by the cases' scripts. inflow_fetch and obstacle_channel ran on pressure-driven channels and sgs_decay is the filtered-DNS study; all three are dropped (the design drops the channel cases and the a-priori study), their harnesses kept as test fixtures (validation/fixtures/inflow_fetch.jl, sgs_validation.jl), and so is the channel harness of WallModels (test/fixtures/channel_cases.jl).

Every domain has an :advection provider; [sgs] model = "none" enables no :sgs component. Each component has its own per-domain section of the model's configuration, generated from its schema (COMPONENTS, ONE-b #451), and is switched on by it. The component of an exclusive slot is a provider of the slot's section instead (ONE-m, #476): its schema is a slot_section with its provider(s), the model's schema merges the parts of one slot (merge_sections), and enabled compares the selector with its provider name (opts.model === :most); the section holds the chosen provider's options only (enabled(C, opts), from the options alone). Before anything is built, the enabled component types of every domain are checked (component_problems): at most one per slot, an :advection provider, and each component's requires and incompatible, every problem keyed by the section it concerns; validate_components adds the stage rules for the built instances. The schema, the domains and the bare-config dry LES are on Run configuration.

The model builds its core with the dynamics only (viscosity, buoyancy, moisture, boundary conditions) and hands SGS, advection, Coriolis, the sponge and the θ fluxes to these components. The plain Smagorinsky closure is therefore the :sgs component's StaticSmagorinsky, no longer the core's built-in C_s; its runs now report the sgs.nu_h_max, sgs.nu_v_max and sgs.kappa_h_max diagnostics every other closure already reported.

The couplings EX-d (#441) put around the model's step — [forcing] radiative cooling, the [surface] slab or flux map, [microphysics] Kessler, [tracer], [particles] and [obstacles] — are one component of the domain for now, SimulationCouplings, which runs each piece where it acted before the pipeline: the slab's flux of the step and the flux map in :surface_flux, the slab's floor flux in :tendencies (it used to set the core's θ_flux_bottom), the obstacles' IBM penalty in :body_force, and cooling, the slab's energy balance, Kessler, the tracer and the particles right after the core, in :surface_update. Splitting them into components of their own puts Kessler and the tracer in stage 5, before the core, as the design has them: a change of the splitting order, so of the results, which is left to the step that ports them.

The components are thin adapters, so their port is a question of the physics module they call. On the anelastic core the SGS fluxes (SGS) are in the ρ̄-weighted flux form of its advection (Operators.flux_weights; see Sound-proof cores), and so is the prescribed surface flux (SurfaceThetaFlux) as it was (the base state's wall faces carry the adjacent cell's weight): Σ ρ̄ θ ΔV changes only by the surface fluxes, to round-off, and the SGS terms conserve Σ ρ̄ uᵢ ΔV. CentredAdvection is the core's own advection (weighted_momentum_advect! on the anelastic core); Coriolis and Sponge act on the velocity and are the same on both cores.

test/components_both_cores.jl runs the contracts of every component on both cores and both schemes, the checkpoint round trip on both cores with Euler, RK3 and the adaptive step, and a dry CBL on both cores (the 30 min run is validation/components_both_cores.jl). Its numbers (16³, the DCBL config): the θ budget closes to below 1e-11 on both cores with every scheme; after 30 min the anelastic mixed layer has warmed more than the Boussinesq one by the column-mass ratio ρ̄_bot h / ∫₀ʰ ρ̄ dz within a factor of 1.5. The anelastic digests of the simulation variants (simulation_digests_anelastic.toml) were re-captured on ONE-j: the weighted SGS fluxes changed those with a closure, which test/components_pipeline.jl asserts; the variant without one kept master's digests bit for bit.

Bit identity

Every model-config variant of test/support/simulation_digests.jl — the four shipped configs plus overrides switching on each SGS closure, Coriolis (also as the adaptive step's binding limit), the sponge, the top θ flux, moisture, a stretched grid, the anelastic core, RK3 and the adaptive stepper: 28 runs at 16³ — reproduces the state digests captured on master ead7b986 before the pipeline, bit for bit, and its diagnostics.csv byte for byte apart from those three new columns (test/components_pipeline.jl). Budgets: the stage-context measurement

A domain measures its budgets only when they are switched on (Components.with_budgets, which a run does when [output] variables selects a budget_* variable). With the budgets off the pipeline is the plain loop above: nothing is measured, and the hook-free fast path holds. With them on, every stage entry is an attribution context of every measured quantity. This is the mechanism of the urban case's air-heat ledger (#253), which is now Aeolus.BudgetSystem.StageBudget (ONE-c, #452):

quantitymeasuredweight per level
:energyθ: the heat of the air $Σ ρ̄ c_p Π̄ θ ΔV / A$ (J m⁻²)$ρ̄ c_p Π̄ Δz / (N_x N_y)$
:waterqv and the water the components carry (ql, qr)$ρ̄ Δz / (N_x N_y)$
:momentum_x, :momentum_yu, v$ρ̄ Δz / (N_x N_y)$
:tracerthe components' tracer$ρ̄ Δz / (N_x N_y)$

ρ̄, Π̄ and cp are the anelastic base state. On the Boussinesq core they are its constant density ``ρ0 = p{ref} / (Rd θ_0)$with$Π̄ = 1$, because the Boussinesq transport conserves$Σ θ``. Solid cells (the obstacles) are not air.

  • Outside the core (stages 1–5 and 11–13), the budget measures the quantity when a component's entry opens and when it closes, and books the difference under (stage, component).
  • Inside the core (stages 6–8), a component adds to the core's tendencies, and the predictor applies them. The entry is therefore measured on the tendency array and scaled by the substage's weight times Δt (1 for Euler; 1/6, 1/6, 2/3 for the SSP-RK3 substages). A :post_predictor entry changes the predicted state, so it is measured on the state and scaled by the weight. An entry that books what it does itself inside the dynamics' context (add_heat!) opts out with Components.attributed(c, Val(stage)) = false, so it is not counted twice: the urban site's open faces, whose θ relaxation is (:dynamics, :relaxation) (ONE-e, #454).
  • The core step is one context (:dynamics). Whatever the core does beyond the components inside it is split into (:dynamics, :transport) (what the transport conserves; over the whole column it is the numerics' residual) and (:dynamics, :redistribution) (what moved between levels of different weight). Exchange with solid cells is booked as (:dynamics, :solid_exchange).
  • Retries. An adaptive step can reject an attempt and retry it with half the step. What the in-core entries booked is held by the dynamics' context until the step is taken. The domain notices a retry when the first in-core hook runs again at the step's start time, and drops that booking.
  • Closure. Every change is attributed, so each budget closes by construction: storage − Σ terms is round-off. A change outside every context is booked as (:core, :unattributed).

After the stages of 11–12 and :budgets (13) have run, each component's budget!(c, domain, budgets) books the terms it reports: surface energy per class, water from outside the air, free-form terms. The step then closes and :checks and :output run.

Measured (test/budgets_output.jl, 6 × 5 × 8 box):

  • The prescribed θ fluxes are attributed to (:tendencies, :surface_theta_flux) as $ρ_0 c_p (F_{bottom} − F_{top}) t$ to rtol 1e-12. This holds with Euler, RK3 and the adaptive RK3 step.
  • Coriolis is the whole change of a uniform flow's momentum (rtol 1e-12).
  • The sponge's momentum term is zero below z_start.
  • An adaptive step whose first attempt throws books its in-core heating once.
  • Every closure is below 1e-12 of the largest term.
  • The state is bit for bit the same with the budgets on or off.

Output: catalogues and the one writer

Every component publishes an output catalogue (outputs(c): a list of Aeolus.Output.OutputVariables, named as output_names(C, opts) declares for its type). The core publishes Components.CORE_OUTPUTS:

  • u, v, w, p, θ, qv as 3-D fields;
  • the horizontal-mean profiles, and (ONE-l #475) the total heat-flux, w-variance and resolved-TKE profiles and the shear-production, buoyancy-flux and turbulent-fraction profiles of the transition diagnostics;
  • the domain means and w_rms as time series; u_squared and vorticity_y as 3-D fields;
  • the budget_* groups.

[output] variables selects from the catalogues of the core and the enabled components. The configuration is checked against the declared names, so selecting a variable whose component is off is an error. The default is u, v, w, theta and the time series of the domain means.

One writer, Aeolus.Output.OutputWriter, writes the selection into one NetCDF-4 file. Records (3-D, maps, profiles) go out every cadence, and the time series every series_cadence. Each value is either the value at the record or its mean over the record's interval (averaging). A spectrum (dimension k, the shell wavenumbers) is a record too: the core's energy_spectrum (ONE-g #456, with kinetic_energy, dissipation and dissipation_sgs). The urban case writes through the same writer: the urban couplings' output_fields are catalogues of OutputVariables.

Checks

Every component publishes its checks (checks(c): Aeolus.Checks.Checks, named as check_names(C) declares for its type), and the core publishes Components.CORE_CHECKS: the sanity check finite and the closure check of each measured budget. The model's [checks] section has one entry per published name, name = {required, tolerance} (an empty table is off), and the reference comparisons [checks.reference.<quantity>] against reference_file. The configuration is validated against the declared names and against the requirements of a closure's budget. A closure check turns its budget on.

The checks run in stage 13, after the budgets close and before the output: each step, a check that accumulates (a reference comparison over a time window) samples the step; at the end of the run every enabled check is evaluated and output/results.toml is written. A failed required check ends the run with Checks.ChecksFailed, and aeolus run exits with 3. A run that stops with an error records the failed sanity check before the error propagates. See Checks.

Nesting by domains (ONE-f, #455)

A model of several domains is built by nested_model from one NestLink per child; the configuration builds it from its [[domain]] blocks (Run configuration). The coupling is Nesting.NestHierarchy (#328), unchanged: its operators, boundary data, subcycling, flux registers and two-way feedback. What is new is that each domain's advance is its own pipeline: PipelineCore runs a domain's drivers, its stages before the core, the core step with stages 6–8 (the nest's register hook chained before the domain's body-force hook, a nest sponge after its post-predictor hook) and its stages after the core. A child therefore runs every stage once per substep, and its in-core stages once per stage of its scheme. The design's :nest_sync (1) and :nest_feedback (12) are the two halves of the coupling the model runs around the domains' pipelines: the boundary data prolongated at both ends of the parent's step, then the feedback after the children reach its end.

Measured (test/model_nesting.jl; 16 × 16 × 8 parent at Δ = 100 m, its 16 × 16 × 8 child at Δ = 50 m, RK3, dt = 4 s):

  • one-way, the parent's state after 6 steps is the parent run alone, bit for bit;
  • two-way, the composite θ of the nest is conserved to round-off: a relative change of 2.0e-16 over 6 steps (the test bound is 1e-13), and 4.0e-16 over 3 steps of a three-level two-way nest; one-way, where nothing is refluxed, it changes by 8.4e-7. The parent differs from the parent alone under the child;
  • a child's pipeline stages run twice per parent step (2 substeps), its in-core stage 3 × 2 times with RK3;
  • an interrupted nested run resumes from its checkpoint bit for bit, both domains;
  • a one-way child that goes non-finite stops the run as a blow-up (the guard inspects every domain, not only the root it steps), and the crash checkpoint holds the whole nest at its last good state.

A root step of this nest costs 1.27 ms one-way and 1.71 ms two-way, against 0.19 ms for the parent alone (the child has as many cells as the parent and takes two substeps). The single-domain step is unchanged: a 64³ RK3 step of the bare config takes 33.6 ms on the branch and 33.4 ms on its base (minimum of 15, two alternating runs each).

The one nest engine (NEST-p2, #482)

The nest is stepped by the model's own engine (nest_window!, on the links of the hierarchy): a domain's pipeline is split around the nest — stages 1–11, then its children's windows and the two-way feedback (stage 12, :nest_feedback), then stage 13 — so a parent's budgets close after its children's feedback, which they book as its nest exchange. With fixed steps the prognostic state is that of Nesting.step_nested!, bit for bit; on a 64 × 64 × 32 root with a 64 × 64 × 32 child at r = 2 (RK3, one thread, minimum of 4 × 5 windows) a window takes 0.0501 s two-way and 0.0393 s one-way, against 0.0511 s and 0.0397 s on the hierarchy's own path (4 threads: 0.0366 / 0.0299 s against 0.0385 / 0.0298 s). The engine adds adaptive steps with the smallest stable whole number of child substeps and window retries, per-domain budgets with a nest-exchange term, siblings in parallel and a one-way parent below the root stepping ahead of its children, synthetic inflow turbulence with the parent's statistics, mixed Boussinesq/anelastic cores, the PALM rule for the exchanged fields and the subgrid handover; the measured numbers are on Nested time coupling and Two-way nesting, the configuration on Run configuration.

Measured on the nest of this section (test/model_nesting.jl): every domain's energy and momentum budgets close to 1e-12 of their content (one-/two-way, Euler/RK3, fixed and adaptive), and the parent's exchange and the child's add up to the heat the covered parent cells gained, the composite conservation; an adaptive two-way nest with two retried windows changes its composite θ by 1.8e-15 over 6 windows; with inflow turbulence the added mass flux is at most 1e-12 of the mean-flow flux and the two-way composite θ changes by 2.2e-15.

The couplings now gather the case's fields and hand the results on (the case's reference state itself is Anelastic.reference_from_profile of its initial profile); small formulas that remain are the radiative temperature of the surface $(Σε T_s^4/Σε)^{1/4}$ (the column radiation and, summed differently, the nest parent), the friction velocity $\sqrt{τ/ρ}$ of a surface step, and the independent recomputations inside the smoke_checks.

  • ONE-e (#454), second part: the urban case on the pipeline and on the model's configuration.

    • The stage loop is gone. A built case is a Domain of the pipeline (case.domain; Urban.build_domain!): the case's own component UrbanCore (the local hour and the budgets' snapshot first in :clock; the heat captures of the fixed-gradient top and the open faces, with the open faces' θ relaxation, in :tendencies) and one component UrbanEntry per enabled coupling, carrying the pipeline stages, cadences and orders the coupling declares. A step is the pipeline's stages around the :timestepper provider's core step, whose hooks are the pipeline's (core_hooks). The urban stages became the pipeline's: :nest_parent is :nest_sync, :anthropogenic is :sources, :fog_dew the second entry of :microphysics, :transport a new stage of the pipeline, and the integer orders within a stage are Stage(…; order), which order_components meets. The case's ledger is the domain's budgets (DomainBudgets around case.budgets, binned by the local hour). Measured: the 54 runs of the smoke matrix (12 steps, seed_fog!, 1245 state digests of the child, every tracer, Ts and the nest parent) are bit for bit those of master 37053292.

    • Berlin on the model. examples/urban/berlin_*.toml are model configs: [urban] enabled makes the domain an urban site, and every urban component has a section of the model's schema, generated from its default_options (enabled, default false, and its options). aeolus run builds and runs the site (then UrbanCase.run_urban!); the urban run type, its schema and [physics] are gone. The configs of the four scenarios resolve, field for field and bitwise, to the CaseConfigs of the fixture (test/config_berlin.jl).

    • Its checks. The site publishes checks of the one checks system (ONE-d): urban_budgets (its budgets' closure against BUDGET_TOLERANCES) and <component>_checks per enabled component (its smoke_checks that fail), besides the core's finite and energy_closure of its domain; [checks] enables them, the run evaluates them into results.toml, and a failed required one ends it with exit code 3.

    • The coupling registry is gone. The couplings are urban components, UrbanComponent <: AbstractComponent, with the component interface's component_name (their section), slot (nothing, or an urban slot of URBAN_SLOTS) and incompatible (component types, not slot => provider pairs). The site's components are the fixed list URBAN_COMPONENTS; a config enables one by its section (enabled), and CaseConfig.components holds the enabled ones with all their options (the registry's REGISTRY, register!, selected_couplings, is_default, default_provider and CaseConfig.physics/options are gone; URBAN_DEFAULTS is the reference selection of CaseConfig() and the smoke runs). The smoke matrix's 54 runs hash bit for bit as before (3809 hashes of the thread-invariance worker's smoke section).

  • ONE-k (#474): the urban site on the model.

    • A component takes the core step. A component may take its domain's core step itself: takes_core_step(c), advance_core!(c, d, dt) (with the pipeline's hooks), next_dt(c, d) (its step) and check_dt(c, d, dt) (an explicit step it refuses). step!(d, dt) runs it in place of the core's step!/step_rk3!; pick_dt(model) and step!(model) take the step it picks. The stages after the core see t0 + dt (an adaptive step's retried pieces sum to it only to round-off). The urban site is that component: the case object became UrbanSite <: AbstractComponent, the first component of its domain, with the site's shared state (its options, geometry, surface bus, tracers, budgets and reference state); its :timestepper component takes the step (fixed, adaptive with retried pieces, IMEX). Its sanity check is its :budgets entry (a blowup throws, and the run monitor diagnoses it), its frame log its first :output entry. step_case! and the case's own step are gone.
    • The run loop is the model's. The run loop of aeolus run (SimulationDriver) asks the component that takes the core step for the run's horizon and steps (run_begin!, run_continues, run_end!; the urban nest prefetches its parent's next window within it, SC-c), the diagnostics row (step_report, run_diagnostics), the numbers it adds to results.toml (run_results) and its state arrays for the bitwise comparisons (state_arrays). Components.run_to!(model, t_end) is the same loop without output, monitor and checkpoints. Gone: run_case!, run_urban!, UrbanSiteRun, the site's NetCDF writer and its restart: the run's writer writes the variables [output] variables selects from the domain's catalogue (the site publishes its frame series, tracers, surface maps and the components' fields, and static_outputs its geometry), and the site is checkpoint component urban of the run's resume!.
    • The configuration is the model's. CaseConfig (a mutable struct with Julia-side defaults, a keyword and a path constructor) is gone: the site's options are site_options(cfg), a NamedTuple read from the resolved model config, changed in TOML or with --set.
    • The module and its files. Aeolus.UrbanCase is Aeolus.Urban, its files under src/components/urban/ (the components), src/components/urban/site/ (the site) and src/components/urban/parent/ (the nest parent).

    Measured: the 54 runs of the smoke matrix (seed_fog!, 24 s each) are bit for bit those of the branch base in every state digest (the child, every tracer, Ts, the nest parent), step count, heat closure, water residual and air-heat term, after every commit of ONE-k.

    What is still the site's own: the urban components run through the UrbanEntry adapter (one concrete type, so a selection does not compile the domain anew; their methods take the site, run_stage!(c, case, Val(S), dt), since they read its shared state); the site builds its own core (the Coriolis force, the Smagorinsky coefficient and the sponge in the core's constructor, not the model's Coriolis, SGS and Sponge components); and its nest parent was not yet a [[domain]] (until NEST-p3, #483, below).

  • NEST-p1 (#481): the parent's physics as components. The tiled surface, the prescribed surface flux, the column radiation, the large-scale forcing with its boundary-layer-dependent nudging weight and the TKE closure on the stretched grid are components of the parent's Domain; the hand-wired code of ParentCase that they replace is gone. ParentCase was the shell the nest and the urban_parent run type stepped (until NEST-p3, #483): its ledgers (air heat, water) around the components' stage entries, in the pipeline's order.   • ONE-m (#476): one key per exclusive slot. After #472/#473 a non-default provider took an enable/disable pair of sections ([pressure_fft] enabled = false with [pressure_multigrid] enabled = true, [aq_chem] with [chem_off]; [surface_layer] enabled beside [surface] model). Each exclusive slot is now one section with a selector key, holding the chosen provider's options only (Config.slot_section, provider!; validated and resolved by the loader, with and the option reference): the model's [core] model, [timestep] scheme (run level), [sgs] model, [surface] model (none, slab, flux_map, most: the SurfaceLayer component), [microphysics] scheme, [advection] scheme, and the urban site's fifteen slots (Urban.URBAN_SLOT_SECTIONS; [chemistry] scheme = "aq_chem" | "none"). Measured: the state digests of every shipped config at its [test] size, of the simulation-digest runs and of the smoke matrix are those of the branch base; the four Berlin scenarios resolve to the fixture field for field.
