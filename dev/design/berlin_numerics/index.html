The Berlin case: numerics and the dynamical core

Numerics slots

Four exclusive slots swap the numerics of the dynamical core and the transport (#132). Each slot is one section whose selector key names the provider (ONE-m, #476; the component name of each provider in parentheses below): [pressure] solver = "multigrid", [sgs] model = "smagorinsky", [momentum_advection] scheme = "weno_z", [scalar_advection] model = "advection_schemes". The section holds the chosen provider's options and no other's (--set pressure.solver="multigrid" --set pressure.tol=1e-8 on the command line: a selector set by --set drops the file's options of the old provider).

slotproviders (default first)how
:pressure_solver:cosine_fft (pressure_fft), :multigrid (pressure_multigrid), :mg_cg (pressure_mg_cg), :density_weighted_direct (pressure_density_direct, sound-proof cores only, the direct FFT/DCT + tridiagonal solve, #309), :density_weighted_cg (pressure_density_weighted, sound-proof cores only, #133)sim_options: the solver of BoussinesqSimulation, built with the per-axis Poisson kinds of the case's velocity bcs (case_poisson_axes(cfg, mesh), #251: periodic x, y and Neumann z by default, all-Neumann for lateral = :open or a nested child; smoke-checked); for a sound-proof core pressure_solver = :direct or :cg (with the CG's tol, maxit), the core building the solver with axes derived from the same bcs
:sgs:tke (sgs_tke, PrognosticSGS.TKEClosure, the Deardorff/Moeng prognostic SGS TKE, #182; Laplacian momentum form, see below), :smagorinsky (sgs_smagorinsky, StaticSmagorinsky(cfg.C_s), the coefficients of the cores' built-in closure bit for bit; the default before #182), :dynamic (sgs_dynamic: LagrangianDynamicSmagorinsky by default, DynamicSmagorinsky for averaging = :plane / :local; with the buildings' mask), :mlsgs (sgs_mlsgs, ML.MLSGS), :amd (sgs_amd, AMDClosure, zero in the building cells), :vreman (sgs_vreman, VremanClosure(cfg.C_s)), :sigma (sgs_sigma, SigmaModel, #184), :reynolds_stress (sgs_reynolds_stress, PrognosticSGS.ReynoldsStressClosure, the differential Reynolds-stress closure, #188; not with timestepper = :imex); all but :tke and :reynolds_stress with option stress = :symmetric (:laplacian); :smagorinsky, :dynamic and :mlsgs are each wrapped in StabilityCorrected (P55-e, #187; cfg.sgs_stability = :brown94; :none gives the bare closure, for :smagorinsky the coefficients of the cores' built-in closure bit for bit) and also take scalar_tensor = :diagonal (:isentropic, with diapycnal, slope_max); :tke and :amd carry their own stability treatment (TKE length scale, AMD buoyancy term) and are not wrapped, nor are :vreman, :sigma and :reynolds_stress (its stresses carry the buoyancy production directly)step_options: sgs, an AbstractSGSClosure (the SGS closure contract, #167), and sgs_stress (the stress form, #175); every core and stepper takes both

Lateral boundaries. The case is periodic in x and y by default (cfg.lateral = :periodic, bit for bit the case before #262). Open (Dirichlet-from-boundary-data) faces exist since N-open-b (#255: OpenBoundary, open_boundary_conditions, the AbstractBoundaryData contract and the global mass-flux correction, called from every core through update_open_boundaries!), with the radiation outflow of N-open-c (#260) and the one boundary-flux function boundary_scalar_flux. cfg.lateral = :open (N-open-d, #262) opens the four sides and the top of the case: see Open boundaries. Since N-berlin-nest (#265) the default case is nested: the same open faces are fed by the parent run (Nesting), and lateral (:periodic, :open) chooses the faces only with the nest switched off.

sim_options and step_options are contract hooks: a coupling returns a NamedTuple of keywords for the core's constructor or for every dynamics step (step!, or step_imex! under the IMEX steppers, which takes the same momentum_advection and sgs); two couplings may not set the same keyword.

SGS providers are closure objects of the SGS closure contract (Closure contract): a provider builds its AbstractSGSClosure once and step_options returns sgs_step_options(closure, stress) = (sgs = closure, sgs_stress = stress). Every provider runs the stress-form momentum divergence ∂ⱼ(2νᵢⱼSᵢⱼ) (sgs_stress = :symmetric, P55-b #175; see Stress form vs Laplacian form), not the cores' default Laplacian form ∂ⱼ(νₜ∂ⱼuᵢ); option stress = :laplacian restores that (a smoke variant), and each provider's smoke_check asserts that the stress-form dissipation Σ2νSᵢⱼSᵢⱼ is non-negative. Under the IMEX steppers the diagonal part is implicit and the cross terms explicit.

Stability correction (P55-e, #187; on by default for the :smagorinsky, :dynamic and :mlsgs providers; the default provider :tke has its own stability length and is not wrapped). The one builder sgs_provider_closure wraps those providers' eddy-viscosity closures in StabilityCorrected (Stability correction): νh = ν ℓh, νv = ν ℓv fm(Rig), κh = ν ℓh/Prt, κv = ν ℓv fh(Rig)/Prt, with the TurbulenceStability functions of [urban] sgs_stability (:brown94 default, :lilly, :long_tail, :short_tail, :most; :none = the bare closure, the pre-P55-e run), the anisotropic length scales Δh = √(dx dy), Δv = dz (ℓh = 1.842, ℓv = 0.2947 at dx/dz = 2.5), Rig from the θv of the qv tracer and the core's own reference θ (θ₀, or θ̄(z) on the sound-proof cores, so it runs with core = :anelastic). :dynamic gets no fm on the momentum (only Prt(Ri)). Each provider's smoke_check asserts κv, νv ≥ 0; κv ≤ κh and νv ≤ νh ℓv/ℓh where Ri ≥ 0; νv ≥ νh ℓv/ℓh where Ri ≤ 0 (the convective enhancement of Brown et al.; not for :dynamic); κv = 0 where Ri ≥ Ric; its diagnostics give the supercritical fraction and the mean κv/κh. Smoke variants: sgs_stability = :none, :lilly. Its validation on GABLS1 and the stably stratified channel (P55-f, #191) adds no Berlin coupling — no new physics, a stated exception — and is in Stable boundary layers. The scalar (θ) flux is the contract's diagonal operator add_eddy_diffusion_scalar!(tϕ, ϕ, κh, κv) (P55-c, #181) with these κh ≠ κv. Option scalar_tensor = :isentropic wraps the closure in IsentropicDiffusion: the rotated (Redi) tensor of θ in the triad form, with κv = diapycnal·κh across the isentropes (nothing: the stability-corrected closure's own κv, < κh in stable air), tapered above slope_max and isotropic where N² ≤ 0 (Tensorial scalar diffusivity). It is option-only, with smoke variants (diapycnal = 0.1, and the corrected κv) that check K is positive semi-definite in every cell. The flagship is a daytime convective case in which N² ≤ 0 over most of the domain. A stateful closure (Lagrangian dynamic, prognostic TKE) keeps its memory in that object; the cores commit it once per accepted step and `stepadaptive!rolls it back with the state on a rejected step. A new provider goes in its own file,couplings/sgs_<name>.jl: the couplings are included in sorted order, so it loads aftersgs.jl, which defines the slot. Which providers combine with the:coreand:timestepper` slots is in Dynamical core and time integration.

SGS diffusion of the tracers (#177). The closure's eddy diffusivity reaches every scalar the case transports. θ gets it inside step!; the tracers get it in the :transport stage: after its advective update each tracer with advect = true (qv, ql, the chemistry species) is advanced by dt · ∇·(κt ∇q) with sim.κt of the last dynamics step (TracerSGSDiffusion, sgs_diffuse!). The pollutant, which has its own :tracers stage, calls the same helper. The flux divergence is the src kernel add_eddy_diffusion_scalar! with the case's solid mask (periodic lateral halos): no flux through a face next to a building and solid cells untouched, so vapour neither leaks into the building interiors nor draws on the stale value they hold. On a sound-proof core it passes the constraint weights, giving (1/W)∇·(W κt ∇q), which conserves Σ W q; on the anelastic core that is the ledger's Σ ρ̄ q. The diffusion books its change of water as sgs_diffusion (zero to round-off) plus density_redistribution. Its smoke_check asserts that the diffusion alone conserves Σ q V (Σ W q V) to 1e-12 and that κt ≥ 0. The core runs with moisture = false, so qv and ql are case tracers and are mixed here, with the same κ as every other tracer. Before #177 no tracer was diffused at all: they saw only the implicit dissipation of WENO, a missing-physics bug. sgs_diffusion = false reproduces that transport for comparison. Measured over the first hour of the summer flagship (06:00–07:00, adaptive steps, 64×64×32), with vs without the diffusion: the mean qv of the lowest fluid level 9.496e-3 vs 9.572e-3 kg/kg and its horizontal standard deviation 4.18e-4 vs 4.96e-4 kg/kg (−16 %); the ground-level pollutant maximum 5.01 vs 6.88 (−27 %, domain maximum 13.6 vs 21.5); in the 1669 wall-nook cells (fluid with two or more solid lateral neighbours) the largest vapour excess over the level mean 1.42e-3 vs 1.86e-3 kg/kg, so the diffusion relieves the IBM nook residual noted in scalar_advection.jl but does not remove it; water residual −1.8e-14 kg/m², sgs_diffusion −6.1e-19 kg/m². Cost: +1.0 ms per advected tracer and step (7 tracers: scalar_advection 57.3 → 64.3 ms/step), +0.9 ms for the pollutant; the masked kernel takes 0.58 ms per call against 0.10 ms unmasked.

One shared κ, also under :amd (#234). AMD offers a per-scalar diffusivity from each scalar's own gradient (Operators.sgs_tracer_diffusivity!, forwarded by SolidMaskedSGS); the stage does not use it, by design. Real high-Re turbulence mixes all passive scalars alike (one turbulent Schmidt number). Per-scalar κ would mix NO, NO₂ and O₃ differently and move them off the photostationary state, and mix qv and ql differently, so q_t and θ_l would no longer be mixed as conserved variables. Its zero-gradient fallback (κ = ν_t/Pr_t = 3ν_t) gives a uniform or flat field the largest κ of all and over-mixes plume edges, and the adaptive step (sgs_cfl_diagnostics) sees θ's κt, not the tracers'. An earlier probe of the :amd run measured cell-wise κ differences against θ's of 54–64 % for qv and 66–92 % for the chemistry, 28 % between NO and NO₂, 196 % between qv and ql, and a fallback maximum of 5.9 against 2.6 m²/s. The design test (one shared κ, in `test/berlinnumerics.jluntil #320 removed the Berlin suite) pinned it: under:amdthe stage is bit for bit the stage with every tracer forced ontosim.κt, and on its 12-step state AMD's own κ would differ from θ's by 77–80 % (Σ|Δκ|/Σκ`) for qv and the chemistry, 24 % between NO and NO₂, with ql (zero there) at the fallback 3ν_t everywhere, maximum 5.08 against 2.71 m²/s. Rationale in full: the SGS theory page.

  • Pressure solvers. All three solve the same 7-point Laplacian with the same boundary conditions (periodic, periodic, Neumann); the multigrid ones iterate to a relative residual of 1e-10. In the :smoke window after 100 steps, MG-CG differs from the FFT by at most 6.1e-10 m s⁻¹ in u, v, w and 2.1e-9 K in θ, with max|∇·u| = 1.9e-13 s⁻¹ (measured by the former test/berlin_numerics.jl; the same agreement on a synthetic 16³ box is test/multigrid.jl). They are here for the stretched-grid and cut-cell solver work (#151, #152), not for speed.

  • SGS. :tke (default since #182) is described in Prognostic SGS TKE (#182) below. :dynamic is the Germano–Lilly coefficient, test-filter ratio 3, with the buildings' solid mask (case.geom.sm.mask): νt = 0 in buildings, and the test filter and the averages see air cells only (#173). The default averaging = :lagrangian is Operators.LagrangianDynamicSmagorinsky (Meneveau, Lund & Cabot 1996: averaged along pathlines, no homogeneous planes needed), and by default in its scale-dependent form (`scaledependent = true, LASD, Bou-Zeid, Meneveau & Parlange 2005; #186) withcadence = 4(the Germano identities every 4th step, the memories transported every step).scaledependent = false(LASI) andaveraging = :planeare smoke variants;:localrunsOperators.DynamicSmagorinsky` too. See the SGS theory page. the diagnostics give the mean Cs over air cells in the canopy and in the free stream (the levels between the highest roof + 1 and the sponge), and with LASD the geometric-mean β there. smoke_check asserts ν_t = 0 in buildings and β ∈ [1/8, 8] in the air. There is no trained model. :amd and :vreman (components/urban/sgs_amd_vreman.jl, #176) are the anisotropic minimum-dissipation and Vreman closures (see the SGS theory page). AMD runs with C = 0.3, its own κt and the buoyancy term with the core's g/θ₀; the case's tracers are diffused with that κt (θ's), not with AMD's per-scalar diffusivity (see One shared κ above). It is set to zero in the building cells: there the resolved velocity is ≈ 0, so the denominator ∂l um ∂l um vanishes while θ still varies, and the buoyancy term gave νt up to 19 m²/s inside buildings, against a fluid maximum of 1.6 m²/s. That only shortened the adaptive step: 20 steps instead of 12 for the 24 s smoke run. In the smoke matrix's everything-on run the two maxima are 8.6 (solid) and 8.9 m²/s (fluid). The large values are the published model's: νt grows like 1/|∇u| wherever |∇u| → 0 while θ varies, and that can happen in fluid too. The wrapper (SolidMaskedSGS) forwards every contract generic to the closure and zeroes νh, νv, κh, κv and the tracer diffusivity in solid cells only. Its smoke_check asserts that the fluid cells match the bare closure bit for bit. Vreman uses c = 2.5 Cs² with the case's Cs. Their smoke_check evaluates the closure on the laminar version of the current flow (u and v replaced by their level means, w = 0). It requires νt = 0 exactly in the free stream above the inversion, where Smagorinsky gives up to 0.013 m²/s. the diagnostics give the canopy and free-stream mean νt against Smagorinsky with cfg.C_s. Neither is the default; flipping it is the maintainer's call.   • Scalar advection. Every tracer registered with advect = true; positive tracers go through the flux limiter (advect_positive!). :weno_z7 needs [grid] halo = [4, 4, 4], :teno8 halo 5; the build errors otherwise. The coupling refreshes the velocity halos first (see the stale-halo defect).

Measured in the :smoke window over 1 h (1800 steps, 12:00–13:00, the free stream is levels 7–12, 37.5–75 m, above the 31 m roofs and below the sponge), the dynamic mean Cs is 0.077 in the free stream and 0.076 in the canopy with ratio 3 (0.128 and 0.115 with ratio 2). That is below the inertial-range 0.17: the window's flow is a 1 m s⁻¹ geostrophic wind over 250 m with a capping inversion at 60 m, far from developed isotropic turbulence, and the dynamic coefficient is lower in stratified and transitional flow (it reaches 0.13–0.15 in the decaying-turbulence run of `validation/sgsnumerics.jl`).

Dynamical core and time integration

Two exclusive slots select the dynamics (#133), both in components/urban/core_select.jl and components/urban/timestepper.jl: [core] model and [timestep] scheme (ONE-m, #476; on an urban site these sections offer the providers below, on a domain of the model's core boussinesq / anelastic and rk3 / euler):

slotprovidersrcwhat it is
:core:boussinesq (default)Simulations.BoussinesqSimulation∇·u = 0, buoyancy g(θ − θ₀)/θ₀ about the constant cfg.θ₀
:core:anelasticAnelastic.AnelasticSimulation∇·(ρ̄u) = 0, buoyancy g(θ − θ̄)/θ̄, ρ̄-weighted advection and projection (Lipps & Hemler 1982)

Stretched vertical grid (#151): a stated exception. The BoussinesqSimulation core runs on a StretchedGrids.StretchedGrid. See Stretched vertical grid for the operators, the direct stretched-z Poisson solve and the measured accuracy and cost. The Berlin child keeps its uniform RegularGrid, and there is no :stretched case option. StretchedGrids is instead coupled by default through the nested parent in N-berlin-nest (#265). The parent's vertical layout is surface_refined_faces(dz_surface = 25, z_uniform = 500, z_top = 3000, ratio = 1.08, dz_max = 100): 53 levels, 20 of them uniform. One step of 192 × 192 × 53 costs 0.225 s on one thread, against 0.526 s for 192 × 192 × 120 at a uniform 25 m.

The sound-proof cores take the case's reference state as their base state: case.ref.state, built by build_reference (θ̄(z) = initial_θ(cfg) integrated hydrostatically from case_surface_pressure(cfg) with the MoistThermodynamics constants, see Reference state), so there is one ρ̄(z) per case — the one the couplings convert fluxes with and the ledger integrates water with — the initial state has no buoyancy, and the flow starts as in the Boussinesq core. The dynamics hooks (IBM, canopy drag, geostrophic forcing, moist buoyancy, surface drag) touch only sim.u/v/w/tu/tv/θ and run unchanged. The projection solves ∇·(W∇φ) = ∇·(W u*) with the core's own solver: directly (FFT/DCT + tridiagonal, the :pressure_solver provider :density_weighted_direct, #309) or by Jacobi-preconditioned CG (:density_weighted_cg, options tol = 1e-10, maxit = 500). The CG is what made the sound-proof cores expensive in the #133 table below; the direct solve removes that cost (#309 numbers after the table).

Tracers under a sound-proof core. ∇·(W u) = 0 does not make ∇·u vanish (∇·u = −w ∂ ln W/∂z), so scalar_advection advects with the mass flux: ∂(W q)/∂t = −∇·(W u q), the scheme applied to (W/Wmin) u and the increment scaled by Wmin/Wk (`Anelastic.constraintweightsgives the core's W). Σ W q is conserved exactly, the positivity limiter stays valid (the scaling is ≤ 1) and a uniform tracer stays uniform: in the smoke window, 24 s, max|q − 1| = 1.1e-11, against 5.8e-4 with the plain velocity (optionweighted = false). On the anelastic core W = ρ̄ is the ledger's density, so the transport books nodensity_redistribution` (exactly 0.0); on the pseudo-incompressible core W = ρ̄θ̄ and the θ̄ part of it is booked there (−1.4e-10 kg m⁻² in the smoke window).

The adaptive step is chosen at the start of a case step (next_dt), so every stage — surface, transport, microphysics — uses the dt the dynamics covers. If step_adaptive! has to roll back and halve, the dynamics covers the rest of that dt in further pieces, so the case clock and the dynamics clock never disagree. dt_max (10 s) is capped at the shortest cfg.cadence period. The ledgers book with ledger.dt, the current step's length, not cfg.dt.

Numerics keywords under every stepper. The :fixed stepper is dynamics_step! (step! with the hooks and case.step_kwargs). The :adaptive stepper hands step_adaptive! a stepper that calls step! (or step_imex! with imex = true) with case.step_kwargs, so every retried piece carries them, and it passes the sgs closure to step_adaptive! too, which snapshots the closure's memory with the state and restores both on a rejected step. The diffusive limit uses the selected closure's νt and κt of the current flow (sgs_cfl_diagnostics, the pure compute_sgs!: it commits nothing), also with C_s = 0, since step! applies the closure whatever C_s is. :imex passes them to step_imex!, whose momentum_advection is the explicit operator and whose sgs supplies the frozen νeff and κeff. Tested with a stateful closure that is Smagorinsky plus a commit counter in its memory: one commit per accepted step under every stepper (12 fixed, 9 adaptive, 12 IMEX in the smoke window), the flow bit for bit the plain run of each stepper; and with a forced retry (one rejected try at 10⁶ × ν_t) the rejected commit is rolled back, so the commits equal the accepted pieces (6 after 5 steps, one of them halved).

Wall times are from runs sharing the machine with five others, so they compare only roughly; the step counts and profiles are exact.

  • The adaptive step saves 18 % of the steps in the morning hour and 6 % at noon, with no retry in either; the step is 1.6–3.5 s (mean 2.43 s in the morning, 2.13 s at noon, where the flow is stronger), bound by advection (C = 0.5) in 98 % / 99.9 % of the steps and by the explicit diffusion (ν = 2 m² s⁻¹ on dz = 6.25 m: 3.70 s) in the rest. The CFL diagnostics cost about as much as the steps saved, so the wall time is roughly unchanged.
  • IMEX buys no longer step here: at dx = 15.6 m, dz = 6.25 m the diffusive limit is only 4 % above the advective one, so ARK2 is bound by advection at nearly the same dt and costs twice as much per step. It is the option for configurations where diffusion binds (finer dz, larger ν); the smoke test shows it stable at dt = 12 s, where the explicit step amplifies the 2Δ mode of θ.
  • In a 200 m deep domain the density changes by 2 % (Hρ ≈ 10.7 km), and the sound-proof cores give the Boussinesq mean profile to 0.002 K (morning) and 0.004 K (noon) after 1 h, at 2.2× the cost. They are there for deeper domains, not for the flagship's physics.

Known limitations.

  • The sound-proof cores take only the built-in Smagorinsky closure and their own centred momentum advection (see the table above): their step has no operator keywords.
  • buildings_citymean uses the nominal radiation period under an adaptive dt (see Cadences).
  • moist_buoyancy adds g (θ_ρ − θ)′/θ₀ with the constant θ₀ on every core; on a sound-proof core the dry buoyancy is about θ̄(z), a difference of θ̄/θ₀ − 1 (< 1 % over the flagship depth).
  • Resolved since the PR was opened: the plain-velocity tracer transport under a sound-proof core (now W-weighted, above), the stale-halo leak that the adaptive stepper's CFL diagnostics used to hide (#132 refreshes the halos in the transport itself), and the ledger density (ρ̄ since #131).
