The Berlin case: nesting

Nesting (#265)

The default case is the child of a one-way nest inside an Aeolus parent run (N-berlin-nest, #265; the Berlin half of #153). The parent is the deep diurnal run of The Berlin parent (#259); the coupling is couplings/nesting.jl (:nesting, an addon, on by default), the src machinery Aeolus.Nesting (N-nest-a…d) and the open faces of BoundaryConditions (N-open-a…d). Two-way nesting is an option, options[:nesting].mode = :two_way (#267), not validated: see Two-way option below.

Parent. options[:nesting].parent holds ParentConfig keywords: empty for :summer (6 km × 6 km × 3 km, refinement (2, 2, 4): Δx = 31.25 m, Δz = 25 m to 500 m, 192 × 192 × 53), the :smoke scenario sets a 1 km × 1 km × 500 m parent at (4, 4, 8) (16 × 16 × 10 cells of 62.5 m × 50 m, the land use a crop of the 6 km table). The parent keeps all of its own physics — the tiled surface (SurfaceTiles), column radiation, the geostrophic pressure gradient, θ/qv nudging, θ damping, the fixed-gradient θ top, the TKE closure, SSP-RK3 under step_adaptive! — and its two ledgers; step_parent!(p, h) is the NestedRun's advance_parent closure (domain_advance). The parent is centred on the child and placed in the child mesh's coordinates, so the child (64 × 64 × 64, 1 km × 1 km × 400 m, deepened for the nest) nests at its own origin: parent cells 81–112 in x and y, layers 1–16.

Pluggable parent. options[:nesting].parent_driver is :aeolus (the parent run) or any AbstractBoundaryData — the interface a mesoscale reader (#58) or reanalysis (#57) implements — which then feeds the same nest faces with no parent run (:profile, the analytic case_boundary_data, is the smoke variant of that path).

Faces. The coupling's sim_options give the core its boundary conditions (nest_case_bcs: a ground wall, the four sides and the top OpenBoundary nest faces with the outflow cfg.outflow, Dirichlet by default): case_boundary_conditions defines none for a nested case, so the periodic box and the θ :initial_gradient top are not built and nothing runs alongside. The default :pressure_fft provider reads the all-Neumann axes from them (case_poisson_axes). The NestBoundaryData of the run fills the faces at every stage time of every core (update_open_boundaries!) with the parent's field set prolongated at the two ends of the parent step and blended linearly in time, and the single mass-flux correction of N-open-b. Building faces inside solids keep u·n = 0 (the child's mask). The core's velocity sponge and the θ relaxation over the same layer (cfg.θ_relaxation) relax toward the parent's mean profile over the child (_covered_profile!, refreshed at every window).

Field set. θ, and every advected tracer, ql and qr. qv and the TKE e (:sgs => :tke) take the parent's values; the tracers the parent does not carry (ql, qr, the chemistry species, the pollutant if advected) get the :inflow halo — zero where the face's wind enters, their own value where it leaves (add_open_scalars!, after the couplings registered them). The tracers' halos are the core's open set (tracer_bcs). The child's initial wind is the parent's, prolongated (a wind that differs from the faces' makes $∇·u ≠ 0$ in the boundary cells, and the first flux-form θ step then moves θ by $-θ∇·uΔt$).

Time. The child steps are the case's own (the :adaptive stepper), landing on the parent's windows:

wherewhat
step_dt → limit_dtthe child's step: subcycle_dt(R, dt), R the time left in the open window, or the parent's next step parent_dt when none is open

The nest is built in init! before the other couplings (init_priority = -1): they initialise from the faces' data and the child's wind.

Ledgers. The heat and water the faces carry are N-open-d's terms: (:dynamics, :boundary_heat), (:dynamics, :mass_correction) and (:dynamics, :relaxation) in the air-heat ledger, :boundary in the water ledger (booked with the face fluxes the transport used, the positivity-limited ones included). The parent's own heat and water ledgers close separately. The nesting smoke checks assert both parent closures (≤ 1e-10 of the largest term), the child's net boundary volume flux after the correction (≤ 1e-12 $UA$) and $|∇·u|$, one geostrophic wind in both domains (the child's :geostrophic coupling takes the parent's $U_g(z,t)$, $V_g(z,t)$), the tile mosaic and the field set; every smoke run closes the child's surface, water and air-heat ledgers. The individual side and lid terms carry the through-flow of ≈ 293 K air and largely cancel; the heat the box exchanges is their sum.

Measured (examples/urban/berlin_nest_check.jl, commit e4b34e7, one thread each on the shared 24-core machine, 29 June 2019 from the Lindenberg sounding).

The nested child is driven by the sounding's wind (the parent's geostrophic wind, ≈ 3.4 m/s at the start) instead of the periodic case's 1 m/s, and its advective step shrinks with it: at the same wind the periodic box already costs 2343 s/h. The side and lid terms are the through-flow of ≈ 300 K air (in through the sides, out through the lid: a mean 1.8 cm s⁻¹ upward at the lid); their sum, +342 W m⁻², is dominated by the weights of the ledger's heat $ρ̄c_pΠ̄θ$: $ρ̄Π̄$ is ≈ 6 % smaller at 400 m than at the ground (ρ̄ −4.6 %, Π̄ −1.3 %), so air that enters low and leaves high books ≈ 6 % of the ≈ 6000 W m⁻² through-flow (≈ 350 W m⁻²) as a gain even at uniform θ — the open-box counterpart of the closed box's (:dynamics, :redistribution). The lowest 100 m cool by 1.0 K more in the nested run in this first evening hour: the realistic wind exchanges more heat with the cooling surfaces (−19.3 against −0.06 W m⁻² sensible heat), and the box no longer keeps its own air. The full evening and night are the 24 h validation of #270.

Tests. The physics of the nest is tested on synthetic boxes in test/era2_nest_external.jl (rule of #321): the split of step_nested! is bit for bit; uniform state, Galilean invariance, mass, the warm-bubble export and the hooks in both domains (numbers on the nested time-coupling page). The Berlin wiring is checked by the coupling's smoke checks in every smoke run (47 runs, 188 s with the nest). Checks derived for the periodic box take the nest off (:nesting => false) and keep their assertions (berlinairheat, berlindynamics, berlinnumerics, berlinopenboundaries, berlinsurfaceexchange); the momentum-conservation and Reynolds-stress identities run in closed-box smoke variants.

Stated exceptions. The two-way option (#267, below) is not validated. The parent's 24 h validation is #315, the nested 24 h validation #270. AdaptiveMesh is not coupled to the Berlin run (AMR blocks, not the nest).

Two-way option (#267)

Unvalidated

Two-way nesting of the Berlin case is unvalidated. It runs, and its ledgers close in the 12-step smoke test, but nothing longer has been run. The long-run stability campaign (multi-hour to 24 h runs against one-way, composite drift, buffer and flux-register stability with buildings, the secondary circulation of #327 M5, cost) is #331, after the KernelAbstractions port. One-way stays the default until #331 validates two-way; that issue then makes two-way the default for self-nesting (Aeolus parent → Aeolus child), as in PALM. Driving from a mesoscale model or reanalysis (#58) stays one-way.

options[:nesting].mode = :two_way feeds the child back onto the parent at the end of every parent window, with TwoWayFeedback (its physics, and the synthetic tests it is validated by, are on the two-way nesting page):

  1. Anterpolation of the field set (u, v, w, θ, qv, the tracers, the TKE e) onto the covered parent cells, solid-aware with the child's building mask; optionally relaxed (τ, default nothing: direct replacement, as in PALM);
  2. not in the buffer of buffer parent cells next to every open face of the child (default 2, PALM's) and not below z_anterp, the canopy restriction (:canopy, default: the mean height of the child's built columns, nest_z_anterp; or a height in m, -Inf for none);
  3. the interface flux correction: the parent's velocity on the interface planes takes the child's flux;
  4. refluxing of θ and qv (reflux = true, default) from Berger–Colella flux registers;
  5. the re-projection of the parent. At the next window the child is projected onto its new boundary data (begin_parent_step!).

Bookings. Neither core is stepped by the NestedRun (the parent by its own step_parent!, the child by the stage loop), so the run's hooks cannot book the flux registers. Every refluxed scalar is booked by the code that transports it, with book_nest_flux! and NestScalarFlux(applied_by = :transport):

θparentstep_parent!'s body_force!, every dynamics stage × the stage weight (SSP-RK3: 1/6, 1/6, 2/3)centred advection + molecular and SGS diffusion (diffusivity = :theta)
qvparentthe samecentred advection + the core's tracer diffusion (diffusivity = :tracer)

Each child step is bracketed by begin_child_step! (in :nest_parent) and end_child_step! (in :nest_feedback), so the slot's and the dynamics' bookings of the step land in its registers. The other tracers are anterpolated but not refluxed; a tracer the parent does not carry gets a parent field of its own in a two-way run (the anterpolation writes into it; it is not transported in the parent).

Ledgers. The feedback is a zero-length step of the parent's two ledgers: its heat is booked as (:nest_feedback, :feedback_heat) in the parent's air-heat ledger and its water as (:nest_feedback, :feedback_water) in the parent's water ledger, so both still close. The child's ledgers are untouched (the feedback changes only the parent). The composite (the parent outside the child plus the child) is not conserved exactly in the Berlin case, and no test asserts it: the child's sponge and θ relaxation toward the parent's mean profile, the parent's nudging and θ damping and both surfaces are sources, and see the residuals below. The synthetic conservation tests are in test/era2_nest_twoway.jl and test/era2_nest_external.jl.

Smoke test. The nest's smoke variant "two-way" (buffer 1 and z_anterp = 0: the smoke child covers only 4 × 4 × 2 parent cells, so the default buffer of 2 would leave nothing to anterpolate) runs 12 steps with every coupling on. Besides every run's closed child ledgers it checks that the feedback ran and named a non-zero feedback_heat, that both parent ledgers close (≤ 1e-10 of their largest term), the interface mass-flux mismatch after the flux correction (≤ 1e-12 of $UA$) and the re-projected parent's $\max|∇·u|$ (≤ 1e-8 s⁻¹).

Measured (the smoke variant, 12 child steps = 24 s, 8 parent windows, 7 feedbacks): parent heat-ledger closure 0.0 and water 1.6e-16 of the largest term; child air-heat closure 3.4e-13 W m⁻², water residual 1.9e-15 kg m⁻²; the feedback put +96.7 J m⁻² of heat and −4.3e-5 kg m⁻² of water into the parent; the parent's ⟨θ⟩ over the footprint at 0–100 m (two levels) was 293.0066 K two-way against 292.9905 K one-way (the domain mean 293.0231 against 293.0222 K). This says the coupling acts, in the expected direction (the warmer urban child warms the parent above it); it says nothing about stability.

Refused. mode = :two_way with a parent_driver other than :aeolus (boundary data cannot take a feedback); refluxing with an IMEX stepper (its θ diffusion is implicit, the bookings are explicit; use reflux = false); a sound-proof child core (a Boussinesq parent and a sound-proof child have no common measure, #327 M1).

Known limitations of the slim option (to be examined by #331):

  • the parent's surface flux over the child's footprint is not replaced by the child's area-mean flux (the original #267 scope item 3); with the canopy restriction, the covered parent canopy keeps its own surface exchange;
  • where the slot's positivity limiter scales an interface flux, the booking is the unlimited one;
  • a rejected attempt inside a core's own step_adaptive! (a retry) keeps its bookings;
  • the cost of two-way per parent step has not been measured on the flagship (the smoke run is dominated by compilation).
