The Berlin case: nesting

Nesting (#265; NEST-p3, #483)

The default case is the child of a one-way nest inside an Aeolus parent (N-berlin-nest, #265; the Berlin half of #153). Since NEST-p3 (#483) the parent is a [[domain]] of the model's config, a domain of the model's core, and the site is the next [[domain]] block, nested in it at its origin and refinement; one engine steps both (the model's Components.ModelNest, NEST-p2 #482). The coupling of the site is components/urban/nesting.jl (:nesting, an addon), the src machinery Aeolus.Nesting (N-nest-a…d) and the open faces of BoundaryConditions (N-open-a…d). Two-way nesting is an option (feedback = "two_way" in the site's block, #267), not validated: see Two-way option below.

Parent. The parent's block configures its whole physics (The Berlin parent, #259): berlin_summer.toml's is 6 km × 6 km × 3 km on 192 × 192 × 53 cells (Δx = 31.25 m, Δz = 25 m to 500 m, then × 1.08 per level up to 100 m: [grid] stretching = "surface_refined"), the site at refinement (2, 2, 4); berlin_smoke.toml's 1 km × 1 km × 500 m on 16 × 16 × 10 cells of 62.5 m × 50 m at refinement (4, 4, 8). Its components: the tiled urban surface of its static driver ([surface] model = "tiles"; the site's own classes under its footprint, AeolusPrep.jl), the column radiation of the plane-mean column ([radiation] scheme = "mean_column"), the geostrophic pressure gradient, θ/qv nudging and θ damping from the sounding driver ([large_scale_forcing]), the Coriolis force and velocity sponge, the TKE closure ([sgs] model = "tke"), the fixed-gradient θ top, SSP-RK3 at the root's adaptive step ([time] cfl, dt_max). The parent's corner is at minus the site's origin, so the site (64 × 64 × 64, 1 km × 1 km × 400 m) stands at its own corner (0, 0): parent cells 81–112 in x and y, layers 1–16.

Pluggable parent. A site alone (no parent [[domain]]) takes its faces from boundary data instead ([nesting] parent_driver): "profile", the analytic case_boundary_data, "offline", the parent-made dynamic driver of an online run ([urban] child_boundaries), or from Julia any AbstractBoundaryData — the interface a mesoscale reader (#58) or reanalysis (#57) implements.

Faces. The coupling's sim_options give the core its boundary conditions (nest_case_bcs: a ground wall, the four sides and the top OpenBoundary nest faces with the outflow cfg.outflow, Dirichlet by default): case_boundary_conditions defines none for a nested case, so the periodic box and the θ :initial_gradient top are not built and nothing runs alongside. The default :pressure_fft provider reads the all-Neumann axes from them (case_poisson_axes). The NestBoundaryData of the link fills the faces at every stage time of every core (update_open_boundaries!) with the parent's field set prolongated at the two ends of the parent step and blended linearly in time, and the single mass-flux correction of N-open-b. Building faces inside solids keep u·n = 0 (the site's mask). The core's velocity sponge and the θ relaxation over the same layer (cfg.θ_relaxation) relax toward the parent's mean profile over the site (_covered_profile!, refreshed at every window).

Field set. θ, and every advected tracer, ql and qr. qv and the TKE e (the parent's [sgs] model = "tke") take the parent's values; the tracers the parent does not carry (ql, qr, the chemistry species, the pollutant if advected) get the :inflow halo — zero where the face's wind enters, their own value where it leaves (add_open_scalars!, after the couplings registered them). This is the site's rule, not the model's PALM rule (a nested model domain carries exactly its parent's fields): the site's extra tracers are not its dynamic driver's either; the nest checks leave an urban child's fields to its :nesting component. The tracers' halos are the core's open set (tracer_bcs). The site's initial wind is the parent's, prolongated (a wind that differs from the faces' makes $∇·u ≠ 0$ in the boundary cells, and the first flux-form θ step then moves θ by $-θ∇·uΔt$).

Time. One window of the nest engine is one step of the parent; the site's steps are its own (the :adaptive stepper, retrying its own pieces), landing on the window's end:

The site joins the nest in its init!, before the other couplings (init_priority = -1): they initialise from the faces' data and the site's wind. The nest does not save and retry its windows (the site retries its own pieces; the structural snapshot of the parent alone cost the summer nest 3.5–4.4 s per window of 1.5 s): a failed step is the run's blow-up.

No parent ahead. NestedRun's prefetch of the parent's next window while the child stepped (SC-c, #403, one-way under several threads) went with NEST-p3: the engine prefetches a one-way parent below the root only (the root's next step is the model's next window). The flagship measured 1.17–1.36× from it at 4–24 threads (the history is on the nested time-coupling page).

Ledgers. The heat and water the faces carry are N-open-d's terms: (:dynamics, :boundary_heat), (:dynamics, :mass_correction) and (:dynamics, :relaxation) in the air-heat ledger, :boundary in the water ledger (booked with the face fluxes the transport used, the positivity-limited ones included; the site books what its faces carry itself, no nest-exchange term). The parent's energy and water budgets (its domain's, when it measures them) close separately, a two-way feedback booked as its nest exchange. The nesting smoke checks assert both parent closures (≤ 1e-10 of the largest term) when it measures them, the child's net boundary volume flux after the correction (≤ 1e-12 $UA$) and $|∇·u|$, one geostrophic wind in both domains (the child's :geostrophic coupling takes the parent's $U_g(z,t)$, $V_g(z,t)$), the tile mosaic and the field set; every smoke run closes the child's surface, water and air-heat ledgers. The individual side and lid terms carry the through-flow of ≈ 293 K air and largely cancel; the heat the box exchanges is their sum.

Measured (with the ParentCase parent before NEST-p3; examples/urban/berlin_nest_check.jl, commit e4b34e7; since #438 these runs are aeolus run examples/urban/berlin_summer.toml with --set overrides — the start hour, nesting.enabled=false, the parent's refinement — and the numbers below are in each run's output/results.toml: [timing] wall_s_per_simulated_h, [open_boundary_heat], [air_heat], theta_mean_below_100m_start/_end; one thread each on the shared 24-core machine, 29 June 2019 from the Lindenberg sounding).

The side and lid terms are the through-flow of ≈ 300 K air (in through the sides, out through the lid: a mean 1.8 cm s⁻¹ upward at the lid); their sum, +342 W m⁻², is dominated by the weights of the ledger's heat $ρ̄c_pΠ̄θ$: $ρ̄Π̄$ is ≈ 6 % smaller at 400 m than at the ground (ρ̄ −4.6 %, Π̄ −1.3 %), so air that enters low and leaves high books ≈ 6 % of the ≈ 6000 W m⁻² through-flow (≈ 350 W m⁻²) as a gain even at uniform θ — the open-box counterpart of the closed box's (:dynamics, :redistribution). The lowest 100 m cool by 1.0 K more in the nested run in this first evening hour: the realistic wind exchanges more heat with the cooling surfaces (−19.3 against −0.06 W m⁻² sensible heat), and the box no longer keeps its own air. The full evening and night are the 24 h validation of #270.

Tests. The physics of the nest is tested on synthetic boxes in test/nest_external.jl (rule of #321): a window driven from outside is step_nested!'s bit for bit; uniform state, Galilean invariance, mass, the warm-bubble export and the hooks in both domains (numbers on the nested time-coupling page). The Berlin wiring is checked by the coupling's smoke checks in every smoke run (47 runs, 188 s with the nest). Checks derived for the periodic box take the nest off (:nesting => false) and keep their assertions (berlinairheat, berlindynamics, berlinnumerics, berlinopenboundaries, berlinsurfaceexchange); the momentum-conservation and Reynolds-stress identities run in closed-box smoke variants.

Stated exceptions. The two-way option (#267, below) is not validated. The parent's 24 h validation is #315, the nested 24 h validation #270. AdaptiveMesh is not coupled to the Berlin run (AMR blocks, not the nest).

Two-way option (#267)

Unvalidated

Two-way nesting of the Berlin case is unvalidated. It runs, and its ledgers close in the 12-step smoke test, but nothing longer has been run. The long-run stability campaign (multi-hour to 24 h runs against one-way, composite drift, buffer and flux-register stability with buildings, the secondary circulation of #327 M5, cost) is #331, after the KernelAbstractions port. One-way stays the default until #331 validates two-way; that issue then makes two-way the default for self-nesting (Aeolus parent → Aeolus child), as in PALM. Driving from a mesoscale model or reanalysis (#58) stays one-way.

feedback = "two_way" (the site's block) feeds the site back onto the parent at the end of every parent window, with TwoWayFeedback (its physics, and the synthetic tests it is validated by, are on the two-way nesting page):

  1. Anterpolation of the field set (u, v, w, θ, qv, the tracers, the TKE e) onto the covered parent cells, solid-aware with the child's building mask; optionally relaxed (τ, default nothing: direct replacement, as in PALM);
  2. not in the buffer of buffer parent cells next to every open face of the child (default 2, PALM's) and not below z_anterp, the canopy restriction (:canopy, default: the mean height of the child's built columns, nest_z_anterp; or a height in m, -Inf for none);
  3. the interface flux correction: the parent's velocity on the interface planes takes the child's flux;
  4. refluxing of θ and qv (reflux = true, default) from Berger–Colella flux registers;
  5. the re-projection of the parent. At the next window the child is projected onto its new boundary data (when the window opens).

Bookings. The parent is a domain of the model's core: the link's register hook books its θ and qv in every stage of its core's step with the stage weights (SSP-RK3: 1/6, 1/6, 2/3). The site moves its θ in its core's step pieces and qv in its :scalar_advection slot, and books both itself with book_nest_flux!: the link's NestScalarFlux(child_applied_by = :transport) (NEST-p3, #483; before, the parent's bookings were explicit too):

scalardomainbooked byflux
θparentthe link's register hook, every stage of the core × the stage weightcentred advection + molecular and SGS diffusion
qvparentthe samecentred advection + the core's tracer diffusion
θchildthe coupling's :body_force entry (order 5; a no-op one-way), every stage of step!centred advection + the closure's θ diffusion
qvchildthe :scalar_advection slot, before its update (#327 M3)the slot's scheme (WENO-Z5) with the velocity it uses, then the SGS diffusion with the tracer's κ

Each site step is bracketed by the link's _begin! (in :nest_sync) and _commit! (in :nest_feedback), so the slot's and the dynamics' bookings of the step land in its registers. The other tracers are anterpolated but not refluxed; a tracer the parent does not carry gets a parent field of its own in a two-way run (the anterpolation writes into it; it is not transported in the parent).

Ledgers. The feedback is booked in the parent's budgets (when it measures them) as its nest exchange, (:nest_feedback, :nest_exchange) (Components.NEST_EXCHANGE), so they still close (before NEST-p3 the ParentCase ledgers named it (:nest_feedback, :feedback_heat) and :feedback_water). The child's ledgers are untouched (the feedback changes only the parent). The composite (the parent outside the child plus the child) is not conserved exactly in the Berlin case, and no test asserts it: the child's sponge and θ relaxation toward the parent's mean profile, the parent's nudging and θ damping and both surfaces are sources, and see the residuals below. The synthetic conservation tests are in test/nest_twoway.jl and test/nest_external.jl.

Smoke test. The nest's smoke variant "two-way" (buffer 1 and z_anterp = 0: the smoke child covers only 4 × 4 × 2 parent cells, so the default buffer of 2 would leave nothing to anterpolate) runs 12 steps with every coupling on. Besides every run's closed child ledgers it checks that the feedback ran, that the parent's budgets close (≤ 1e-10 of their largest term) when it measures them, the interface mass-flux mismatch after the flux correction (≤ 1e-12 of $UA$) and the re-projected parent's $\max|∇·u|$ (≤ 1e-8 s⁻¹).

Measured (the ParentCase parent before NEST-p3; the smoke variant, 12 child steps = 24 s, 8 parent windows, 7 feedbacks): parent heat-ledger closure 0.0 and water 1.6e-16 of the largest term; child air-heat closure 3.4e-13 W m⁻², water residual 1.9e-15 kg m⁻²; the feedback put +96.7 J m⁻² of heat and −4.3e-5 kg m⁻² of water into the parent; the parent's ⟨θ⟩ over the footprint at 0–100 m (two levels) was 293.0066 K two-way against 292.9905 K one-way (the domain mean 293.0231 against 293.0222 K). This says the coupling acts, in the expected direction (the warmer urban child warms the parent above it); it says nothing about stability.

Refused. A site alone takes no feedback (boundary data cannot take one); refluxing with an IMEX stepper (its θ diffusion is implicit, the bookings are explicit; use reflux = false); a sound-proof child core (a Boussinesq parent and a sound-proof child have no common measure, #327 M1).

Known limitations of the slim option (to be examined by #331):

  • the parent's surface flux over the child's footprint is not replaced by the child's area-mean flux (the original #267 scope item 3); with the canopy restriction, the covered parent canopy keeps its own surface exchange;
  • where the slot's positivity limiter scales an interface flux, the booking is the unlimited one;
  • a rejected attempt inside a core's own step_adaptive! (a retry) keeps its bookings;
  • the cost of two-way per parent step has not been measured on the flagship (the smoke run is dominated by compilation).

Synthetic inflow turbulence (#268)

The child's lateral faces take the parent's field at 2–4× the child's spacing (flagship: Δxp = 31.25 m against 15.6 m). The parent's eddies arrive; the eddies between the two resolutions do not, and an inflow fed only with the parent's interpolated field enters the child too smooth (N-inflow-d, #266: with no generator at all, a laminar inflow stayed laminar for 15 boundary-layer depths). The addon `:inflowturbulence(components/urban/inflowturbulence.jl, on by default) adds the missing part on the **inflow** parts of the four lateral faces with [InflowTurbulence](@ref Aeolus.Transition.InflowTurbulence) (N-inflow-c, #264; theory on the [transition page](../theory/transition.md)): velocity only, ramped on per level by the face-mean inflow (`\min(1, -Un/u{\min})`), its per-level mean removed (no mass added), linear in time over a parent window like the parent data, and inserted insideupdateopen_boundaries!` before the mass-flux correction, so no step core is touched.

The parent is read only at rest. The parent statistics are folded from a hook of the nesting coupling (window_hooks, called at the site's first step in a window, after the parent's step), with fold_inflow_statistics!; the child's boundary refresh never reads the parent.

Ledger and checks. budget! books the perturbation kinetic energy the mean inflow carries through the perturbed faces, $ρ⟨\tfrac12|u'|^2\max(-U_n,0)⟩$ (W m⁻² of perturbed face, injected_ke_flux), as the ledger term :inflow_ke (J m⁻²); the diagnostics inflow_ke_flux, inflow_ke and inflow_added_flux_max report it. The added mass flux is zero by construction: smoke_check asserts it ≤ 1e-12 of the face's mean-flow flux on every face at every step, the booked term, a non-zero perturbation on an inflow face and a newest knot that is exactly zero on every outflow level (the applied perturbation blends two knots in time, so a level that has just turned to outflow still carries the older knot's share). Its smoke variants are the FSD generator, the parametrized statistics (with both generators) and the parametrized statistics on the analytic parent_driver = :profile data; the nest's "two-way" variant runs it with the feedback.

No running parent. Faces fed by a driver instead of the running parent (parent_driver = :profile or :offline, or the open box without the nest) have no resolved statistics: the generator is installed there only with statistics = :parametrized. The offline child replays the parent's faces, not the online child's perturbations, which are drawn from the running parent; test/input_dynamic.jl's bit-for-bit online/offline round trip therefore runs with the addon off.

Two-way. With feedback = "two_way" the feedback skips an inflow band of $⌈2\max L_s/Δx_p⌉$ parent cells (exclude_inflow_feedback!). The interface flux correction gives the parent the child's flux minus the generator's normal perturbation (synthetic_normal_velocity, #418). Before #418 it copied the perturbation into the parent's interface faces. The next window's boundary data returned it to the child, and the generator added the next one on top. On the 2 h sheared-CBL nest of validation/inflow_nest_longrun.jl (Hellsten et al. 2021's two-way CBL with a 3 m s⁻¹ wind, r = 3), that made the child's kinetic energy ×12 and the parent's ×2.5, with an overlap θ drift of +0.25 K against one-way with the generator. Two-way with the generator now meets the #268 criteria (1.5×, 0.2 K) in two realisations: child ×0.94 and ×1.09, parent ×1.02, overlap θ −0.003 K and −0.009 K. Two-way without the generator gives ×1.04, ×0.995 and −0.002 K. Analysis and the choice of fix: Two-way with the generator. The Berlin two-way nest itself is unvalidated; its campaign is #331.

Analysis buffer. Behind an inflow face the turbulence is still developing, so the diagnostics compared with observations leave out the columns within cfg.analysis_buffer of the lateral open faces (analysis_columns, site/open_boundary.jl); today these are ustar_roof and ustar_ground of the surface exchange (#270 adds canopy T and the heat island). The default, 140 m, is an estimate: the 1.2-turnover rule N-inflow-d measured, $L = 1.2\,Uk/ε$, with neutral surface-layer values at the mean roof height of the flagship scene (ANALYSIS_BUFFER_DEFAULT). The Berlin fetch itself — on the child with buildings, inflow from the parent at 12:00 and 03:00, generator on and off, to 90 % of the canopy-top TKE plateau and at 100 m — needs hours of model time and is left to the 24 h validation (#270), which replaces the default with it; long Berlin runs were paused when this was written.

Checkpoint / restart (#269)

The nest is checkpointed whole, at the end of a window (where the run loop checkpoints): the model's nest (Components.ModelNest) records its hierarchy with the structural walk, which reaches every domain — the parent's core and components (its surface tiles, radiation, forcing, closure), the site (checkpoint_state(case): its core, every coupling, the surfaces, the ledgers) — and every link: both parent time levels, the step sizes the adaptive controllers continue from, the flux registers of a two-way run, the statistics, the site's boundary data and the inflow generator (planes, RNG, folded parent statistics).

[checkpoint] is on in berlin_summer.toml (every 30 min of wall clock, the newest 2 kept); aeolus run <folder> --resume continues the run. Module-level proof: test/nest_restart.jl (bitwise, one-way and two-way, a checkpoint at a window's end); the resume of the nested berlin_smoke.toml (a checkpoint every 3 windows, interrupted after 5, the site, its tracers and the parent domain bitwise) is in test/config_roundtrip.jl (removed). The 24 h flagship restart (22:00 resumed to 23:00) waits for the long Berlin runs, paused when this was written. Design and limits: Checkpoint / restart.
