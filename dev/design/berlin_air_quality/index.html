The Berlin case: air quality

Air quality: chemistry and deposition (#135)

The :chemistry slot's default provider aq_chem ([chemistry] scheme = "aq_chem", aq_chem.jl; called :nox_o3_voc before ONE-m, #476) runs the Aeolus.Chemistry mechanism (theory) on five tracers registered with advect = true: NO, NO2, O3, VOC and HNO3, in ppb. The :transport stage moves them. The example's toy mechanism, its deposition table and its dead apply_wet_deposition! are gone. What stays in the coupling is case data: the emission inventory, the class → surface-kind map and the roughness per class.

Every step in :chemistry, in this order:

  1. Emissions go into the first air cell k_air of each column (above the roof on building columns). Traffic NOx is emitted on street and building-adjacent columns: 2 µg m⁻² s⁻¹ as NO2 at the morning peak, 10 % primary NO2, with an assumed diurnal shape (traffic_profile). Isoprene is emitted on park columns: 0.3 µg m⁻² s⁻¹ in full sun, scaled by J(NO2). Both are order-of-magnitude estimates, not a Berlin inventory.
  2. apply_photochemistry! with the clear-sky j_NO2(μ₀) of the current sun, OH prescribed as 5 × 10⁶ cm⁻³ × J/J_max, T = θ̄ Π̄ (θ̄ the level mean over air cells) and the reference p̄ per level (case.ref, #131).
  3. Dry deposition of NO, NO2, O3 and HNO3 from k_air with $v_d = 1/(r_a + r_b + r_c)$.
  4. Rain scavenging of HNO3 by qr.

In :surface_update, on the surface cadence, r_a = 1/(C_H U) and u* are recomputed per column. They come from SurfaceExchange.solve_surface_layer between the skin bus.Ts and the first air cell (cell-centre wind, θ, qv at z = Δz/2), with z0h = z0m/10 and the per-class z0m that #129 gives its surfaces. Since #129 (PR #158) the bus carries u* (bus.u_star) and every column's surface carries its roughness, but this coupling still runs its own surface-layer solve; reading them from the bus is open.

[chemistry] scheme = "none" (the provider of NoChem) removes the species. It is not the default. The smoke matrix also runs a night titration variant (start 00:30, J = OH = 0).

The default flagship starts its surfaces (the soil columns and slabs of #129) 8 K below the air (Ts0 = 285 K). At step 0 every class is stably stratified, ra 6.2e3–7.9e3 s m⁻¹ and u* ≈ 0.015 m s⁻¹, short of the clamp. The street soil skins heat within minutes, and by 12:30 the streets are unstable (ra 89 s m⁻¹, vd O3 0.46 mm s⁻¹): the domain-mean O3 deposition is 0.32 nmol m⁻² s⁻¹, 17 × its value with the pre-#129 slabs, when ra reached 1e5–1e9 s m⁻¹ and dry deposition was effectively off. Roofs and park stay decoupled (ra 5.6e6 and 5.8e5 s m⁻¹; mean Ts at 12:30 291.2 K on roofs and 291.05 K in the park, under first-cell air of about 294 K; streets 302.8 K). Warm surfaces do not change that for the park: its skin cools from 300 to about 291 K within the half hour and decouples (ra 1.0e3–1.05e9 s m⁻¹), so the PR #162 expectation that the default case would look like the right-hand column holds for the streets but not for the park. The periodic domain has no inflow of background air, so emitted NOx accumulates: surface-layer NO rises from 1.68 to 7.74 ppb in 30 min.

Wet deposition, measured in the smoke window with qr = 1e-4 seeded in levels 2–4: the HNO3 removed per step equals $s[\mathrm{HNO_3}]\,(Σ q_r Δz)\,Δt$ to 0.1 %. The rain sediments out in about four steps.

Cost. On the flagship grid on master 915bd49 (600 s from 12:00, with and without the chemistry (then [chemistry] scheme = "none", the provider :off of the time), run one after the other on a loaded 24-core machine, first-call compilation included; both runs take the same 230 adaptive steps, mean dt 2.61 s, since the chemistry does not feed back on the flow) the step goes from 233.7 to 289.1 ms (wall time over steps, i.e. 89.6 → 110.8 ms per simulated second; 190.5 → 248.8 ms summed over the stage timings), so the chemistry adds about 24–31 %. The WENO-Z5 transport of the five species adds 50.0 ms (10.0 ms per tracer: scalar_advection 33.7 → 83.7 ms per step); the aq_chem stage costs 12.1 ms (92 ns per cell) including emissions and deposition; the r_a refresh 3.3 ms averaged over steps. The absolute numbers depend on the load: a first pair run alongside five other measurements gave 325.7 → 509.0 ms per step. (Remeasured at 2895fed with 300 steps of 2 s: 135.5 → 169.2 ms. At #135, before #129, the step went from 43.1 to 101.1 ms.) The chemistry stays on by default because the flagship is meant to exercise all the physics, and :off exists for runs that do not need air quality.
